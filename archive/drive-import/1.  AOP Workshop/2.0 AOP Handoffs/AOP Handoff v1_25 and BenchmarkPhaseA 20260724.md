### The finding (CW, Phase A)
- **A.1.8 — model representational adequacy** (a precondition the original order missed): you cannot knock
  out a species that isn't in the model, and the model's output must map onto the measured persistence
  outcome. **All four candidates fail; three on A.1.8.** Systematic, not bad luck: published models explain
  molecular observables, published knockout ground truth is scored on organism-level outcomes, and modellers
  abstract away the very redundancy that makes the ground truth valuable. *No published model contains KinB
  as a species*, so the strongest sporulation fact can't be expressed as a perturbation.
- **Sporulation ground truth is otherwise excellent** (LeDeaux 1995: kinA/kinB singles ~WT, double collapses
  10⁵–10⁷-fold, replicated; kinC synergy) — but no intervenable model.

### The decision in front of Ben (governance — Ben + Prime, not CW)
- **R1** (CW's rec): keep sporulation; have a **blind** seat build the phosphorelay model from published
  biochemistry, **frozen before AOP touches it**. CW *cannot* be builder (has read the key).
- **R2**: switch to **phage λ** — model output *is* the measured quantity, clean species knockouts. CW frames
  as "the clean alternative."

**Prime's pushback (on record):** R1 reintroduces a researcher degree-of-freedom the benchmark exists to

there is nothing to build.** Recommend deciding R1 vs R2 with the Phase A detail loaded, not from summary.
