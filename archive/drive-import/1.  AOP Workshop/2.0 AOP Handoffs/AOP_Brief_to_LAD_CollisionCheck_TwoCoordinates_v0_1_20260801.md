## 1 · The six questions

### Q1 — Scope of the decoupled-reference criterion

**The Ladder has the location wrong, in a direction that matters and that helps it.** The request treats the decoupled-reference material as living in the follow-on. Most of it does. The **architecture itself does not — it is live in the core, at canon, graded SYNTHESIS.**

v1.26 §11a is retitled *"A regulatory architecture that separates classes of persisters"* and retains two full paragraphs. It carries the three-mode contrast (flame / star / cell), the decoupled-and-separately-interventable formulation, **and** the invariant-subspace restatement with its basis-change defence. Verbatim, the two load-bearing sentences:

> The cell has a restoring force driven against a **regulatory subsystem that is dynamically decoupled from the process it regulates** and is separately interventable — a set-point held in one place and read out onto another, corruptible independently of the physiology it steers.

> Stated invariantly, the discriminator is the existence of a **proper invariant subspace whose dynamics are autonomous with respect to the regulated coordinates** — a formulation that is preserved under similarity transformation, since invariant subspaces map to invariant subspaces, and that is not defeated by a change of basis because an arbitrary basis change is not a member of the declared physical intervention class **I** (§12″).

Grade, stated in the canon: **`[SYNTHESIS; the architecture is a coupling fact. Its development as a life criterion is out of scope here.]`**

**What is *not* in the core**, and lives only in the follow-on at frontier grade: the word *alive*; the six-part conjunction; the alive / pausable tiers; the third-person detectability result; the virion/mule separation; the diachronic material; death.

So: **§3.1 is confirmed, and re-sited.** A competence coordinate floored at decoupled regulation duplicates an AOP object that is canon and synthesis-graded — not, as the request assumed, a frontier claim in a relocated draft. The discriminator is the only AOP object in this neighbourhood; the semantic mask (§3) is its detector, exactly as the request's §3.4 concluded.

### Q5 — The scope wall, restated for this case

**Direct answer: continuity-of-instantiation sits *outside* AOP's scope. AOP owns the slot; it does not own the filling.**

What the core retains is the *requirement* — that no two-time comparison is well posed until a tracking relation is declared. It retains none of the *content* of that relation. §4a, which carried genidentity, the transporter/Theseus contrast, and speciation-as-fission, was deleted from the core entire in the v1.26 fold and relocated to the follow-on, which is not canon.

So the Ladder's identity coordinate is **available to build on**, and the Ladder must supply its own tracking relation rather than inherit one. AOP will tell it that a relation must be declared and nothing further.

The reconstructive half of the request's identity coordinate is therefore the Ladder's, with one qualification: if the Ladder adopts genidentity specifically, it is adopting a **settled named view** [DiFrisco 2018; Parfit 1984] with AOP's mapping onto the axes stripped out — not an AOP object. Cite the literature, not us.

**But see the defect at Q6(a).** The answer above is AOP's position; the canon does not currently implement it cleanly.

### Q6 — What the Ladder is about to duplicate and has not spotted

**(a) The tracking-relation slot does not exist.** §1 L43 says the tracking relation *"enters as part of the declaration **D**, §12″."* §12″ enumerates the tuple exactly once, at L592:

> D = (S, E, F, P, δt, τ, R, V, I, N)

Ten slots — system variables, environment, interface, partition, time grain, horizon, reversal convention, viability functional, intervention class, normalization. **None of them is a tracking relation.** The single sentence AOP retains of the diachronic question points at a slot the canon does not define.

This is a live coherence defect in v1.26, of the same class as the flame contradiction the v1.27 fold repaired, and it was not caught by the 85-region verification because that check established faithfulness to the change set, not internal coherence. It is directly load-bearing on Q5: if the Ladder declares a tracking relation "as part of D" as AOP instructs, there is nowhere in D to put it. **AOP-side. Flagged for adjudication, not resolved here.**

**(b) V is a dependency the competence coordinate inherits.** The request looked for a duplication and found one. The quieter item is not a duplication but a requirement. §12″ makes **V**, the viability functional, load-bearing: *"There is no semantic reading without V, and the declarer is the analyst."* The follow-on's detectability result is scoped "up to declared V," and states that V is load-bearing rather than incidental — it is V that certifies a detected set-point as the system's viable target.

A competence coordinate defined as *"uses present and retained differences to alter the conditions of its own continuation"* is making a viability-relative claim. It inherits the V declaration requirement whole. Without a declared V, competence readings are not well posed — and the declarer is an analyst, not the persister. That is not a collision the Ladder needs to avoid; it is a declaration the Ladder needs to make.
