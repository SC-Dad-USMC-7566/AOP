## 2. The assessment (the substance of the session)

Verdict: the bioelectricity work is valuable, and it is the one part of Levin's output where the telos
strips clean. The reason is that the bioelectric program is dynamical-systems work wearing cognitive
clothes — bistable attractors, a tunable coupling parameter, a maintained voltage contrast held against
leak. Levin narrates it as goals and memories; the experiments do not need the narration. He is open
about the escalation himself (voltage gradients → computation → cognition), which is an interpretive
ratchet, not new data.

**Three places it maps, strongest first.**

1. **Memory — the Cμ/E gap made physical.** Durant et al. 2017 is the load-bearing result. A brief
   bioelectric perturbation permanently rewrites planarian anterior–posterior target morphology; worms
   that regenerate *normally* still carry the rewrite, and recutting reproduces the same double-headed
   ratio. Their own word for it is "cryptic phenotype." The animals don't differ from wild-type in
   histology, polarity gene expression, or neoblast distribution, and the state can be reset with a
   hyperpolarizing H,K-ATPase inhibitor. That is stored structure, bidirectionally rewritable, with
   almost no signature in the ordinary past→future channel — i.e. **large crypticity χ = Cμ − E, with a
   knob on it.** The spore case, except you can edit the escrow.

   It also bears on the standing open question ("life is a marginal machine reading a robust tape"):
   this is a *third* store — neither the robust archive nor transient effector state, but a metastable
   pattern memory in the labile layer held up by drive. Not fatal to the framing; it means the
   tape/machine split isn't exhaustive.

2. **Boundary, Drive and Memory collapsing onto one variable.** Vmem is a maintained interior/exterior
   contrast across a membrane, held against a leak, at a housekeeping cost. That is B1 and B4 verbatim,
   and the computed σ_hk ≈ ½Δ²g(g+w)/w (Edit E14) is the thermodynamics of exactly the quantity Levin
   manipulates — the same Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase whose 20–45 % resting-cell ATP share the canon already cites as
   B4's empirical face. Three axes on one degree of freedom is rare; worth the case gallery if anything
   here earns a place.

3. **Integration, with an experimental dial.** Gap junctions are a physical inter-cell coupling
   parameter and are manipulable (octanol, connexin/innexin targeting). Strip "cognitive glue" and the
   cancer story is: lower inter-cell coupling, and the partition at which the system is irreducible
   moves down toward the single cell. That is the Phase D1 crossover with a wet-lab knob.

**The one that cuts AOP's way, and is probably the headline.** §11a's alive/not-alive discriminator is a
decoupled, separately-interventable set-point — the star corrects but is model-free, therefore not alive.
That criterion currently rests on argument. Levin's bioelectric work is the best demonstration anywhere
that in real organisms the reference genuinely *is* separately addressable: readable, rewritable, and
rewritable back, genome untouched throughout. Not a threat to §11a — the missing evidence for it.

**What does not map.** The cognitive light cone is irreducibly teleological (nothing survives the strip).
The Platonic space is non-naturalist; his own argument for it ("we got more out than we put in") is what
a constraint-satisfaction landscape looks like from the inside. The structural opposition — Levin thinks
categories and criteria retard science, AOP's whole value is a criterion — is narrower than it looks,
since AOP already refuses to individuate and already grades individuality per Krakauer.

**Candidate use for the outstanding could-fail benchmark.** Gap-junctional coupling is externally
manipulable and anatomical outcome is externally scored — neither set by the model's topology. It is the
first non-circular candidate that has surfaced. **Do not oversell this to yourself.** Φ_MIP lives on a
static Gaussian Σ = (I + gL)⁻¹; a planarian is neither static nor Gaussian. The bridge may not exist.
One hard look, not a build commitment.

## 4. The Sacco paper, the walk-back, and the pattern behind it

**Read this part.** Prime pitched `rsta.2025.0011` to Ben as a possible Integration-side bounding
principle — an analogue of §8 screenability, asking which interaction topologies can sustain maintained
global order at all. Then prime read it and **retracted that pitch.** Full detail in
`AOP_VerificationMemo_SaccoSakthivadivelLevin2026_20260721.md` (in the source library folder — move it
to Canon Development if you prefer it there; it was left beside its subject deliberately).

Short version. What's sound: Lemmas 1–2, Theorem 2 (1D local Hamiltonians have no ordered phase —
Landau–Lifshitz generalized to finite windows), Theorem 3 (AR(τ) models map to local Hamiltonians),
Theorem 4 + Proposition 3 (clique-structured graphs admit a non-empty temperature window of local order
with global disorder). What's shaky: Proposition 1 subtracts big-O expressions and reads off a sign;
Theorem 1 invokes the one-sided Bogoliubov inequality and then asserts asymptotic equivalence of free
energies, which is partly the conclusion. **Corollary 1 rests entirely on Theorem 1 — do not cite it**
under any framing. (Caveat recorded honestly: the PDF text extraction is garbled inside that proof, so
part of the defect may be artifact. Worth eyes on the typeset version.)

Why the retraction: **the paper is equilibrium theory**, and the authors say so in §7. Every AOP
persister is held off equilibrium by Drive. And the question it asks is about *spontaneous* order under
thermal fluctuation with no drive, where Integration is about *maintained* order in a driven system.

**The pattern worth carrying forward — this is the real deliverable of §4.** This is the second time in
four days that a clean, attractive theorem turned out not to bind AOP's cases because the *hypotheses
don't hold*: first Lee–Seo 2021 and the star (v1.20, Edit E7), now Sacco et al. and equilibrium. Make it
a standing check:

> Before getting interested in a theorem, ask what its hypotheses require, and whether a driven,
> off-equilibrium persister satisfies them. Equilibrium results generally will not bind AOP's central
> cases. This is not a reason to ignore them — it is a reason to state the scope before, not after.

What the Sacco paper *is* good for, both smaller than the original pitch: (a) **Theorem 4 independently
corroborates the Phase D1 level-selection crossover** — same local-order/global-disorder phenomenon,
derived in equilibrium statistical mechanics, thermodynamic condition 2J > T log nᵢ where D1 gives a
coupling-ratio condition b ≈ a/2. Consilience, *not* equivalence — different order parameter, different
regime; the correspondence would itself be work to establish. Grade SYNTHESIS. (b) The equilibrium-side
complement to arXiv:2410.13375 at Edit E10, which it modestly hardens. Neither justifies a version bump.

## 5. A correction prime owes the record

Prime recommended the **Srivastava et al. 2021 meta-analysis** to Ben as *the instrument for checking how
solid the bioelectric base is independent of Levin's framing.* On reading the abstract, that
recommendation was overstated and should not be inherited uncorrected.

It is three things: a compiled list of channelopathies from phenotype databases (a compilation), an
original bioinformatic analysis of regeneration transcriptomes (new work), and an analysis of published
Vmem values in normal versus cancer cells (the only part that is a meta-analysis in the intended sense).
It is also Levin-lab work in a specialist journal in his own subfield. None of that is disqualifying and
the Vmem synthesis may still be genuinely useful — but **it is not an outside audit, and prime implied it
would be.** What actually adjudicates instructive-versus-permissive is independent replication by labs
with no stake in the answer. Nobody has looked for that yet. It is the honest open question about this
whole body of work.
