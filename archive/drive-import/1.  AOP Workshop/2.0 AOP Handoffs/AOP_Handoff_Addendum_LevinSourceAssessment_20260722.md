that would let AOP cite the mechanism without the framing. That instruction is now wrong. Prime read
the paper and the assumption was refuted.**

cognitive lens*. Printed subject area: cognition. Its spine is an analogy chain — planaria →
hippocampal attractor networks → theta flickering → Bayesian generative models → variational
autoencoders — and it states that regeneration works because tissues exploit bioelectric encoding of
"distributed goal states." It carries **more** teleological framing than Durant, not less.

is where the histology, polarity-gene-expression and neoblast controls can be checked, and therefore the

## 3. What v0.2 changed, in one paragraph each

**Claim A — support up, novelty down.** Pezzulo §9 / figure 4 contains an *original* experiment:
Nigericin treatment, washout, then DiBAC₄(3) voltage imaging at one and three weeks, both treated groups
significantly depolarized versus control. That is direct measurement of the persistent bioelectric state,
not just its anatomical consequence — which is what Claim A needed. But the paper also places the
phenomenon in an existing non-Levin lineage: ciliate cortical inheritance (Beisson 2008; Pilling 2017),
attractor states in cancer networks (Huang & Ingber 2006), and **bacterial persistence as a phenotypic
switch (Balaban et al. 2004, *Science*)**. Non-genomic bistable heritable state is an established class.
**AOP is not discovering a third register; it is placing a known one on the axes.** v0.1 read closer to
discovery than it earned. The upside: Balaban 2004 is a widely-replicated anchor entirely outside the
bioelectric field, which does more for the base than any Levin paper.

**Claim D — re-anchored, and improved.** The decisive observation is not the gap-junction/cancer story.
It is that **the unit of decision is the fragment**: two pieces of one worm can decide differently, but
within a fragment there are never mosaic outcomes — every cell goes with the group. Same reported for
left–right identity and melanocyte conversion. That is an individuation observation aimed straight at
§9/§9a.

**Claim R — strengthened, and now operational.** Ben's question was whether the material can be imported
without the baggage. Answer: yes, but not by finding a clean source — there isn't one and structurally
won't be, because this work is published in cognition venues. The rule that came out of it:
**cite figures and measurements, never interpretations.** For Pezzulo: cite figure 1c (a clean
multistable transition diagram with an absorbing state), figure 4 and §9. Do not cite §§4–8. Where a
source supplies its own non-cognitive framing — Pezzulo offers Waddington-sense epigenetics, "a
non-genomic, re-writable medium for information that controls growth and form" — prefer it.

**The retraction condition is still open.** Direct voltage persistence is necessary but does not exclude
a chromatin or methylation correlate co-varying with the bioelectric state. **If Claim A dies, it dies
here.** Durant 2017 is where to look. Aim the adversarial pass at this specifically.
