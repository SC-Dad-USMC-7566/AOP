## 0 · Arrow discipline, stated first because it governs everything below

Per the Ladder Charter: **AOP feeds the Ladder; the Ladder never bears weight in AOP.** A claim born in the Ladder stays tagged as Ladder-born if referenced in AOP, and may never carry weight there.

This note is written to that rule, and the rule is not decorative here, because the Ladder is about to commit something that may already be AOP's. Concretely:

- **Every question in §4 is a question about AOP's own material**, answerable from AOP documents without reference to anything the Ladder has produced.
- **§5 discloses three Ladder-born items** so the workshop understands what prompted the inquiry. They are disclosed as context, walled explicitly, and **must not appear as support in the requested brief.** If the brief finds itself needing one of them to make an argument, that is the signal the argument is Ladder-side and belongs back on the bus.
- **The Ladder is not asking AOP to change anything.** It is asking AOP to say what AOP already owns, so the Ladder does not rebuild it under a different name.

The failure mode this note exists to prevent is not disagreement. It is the Ladder quietly reinventing an AOP object, publishing it as Ladder synthesis, and having that synthesis cited back into AOP as independent corroboration. That would be one source seen twice.

## 1 · What the Ladder is about to decide

A proposal is live in the Ladder's Working Canon (`LAD_Canon_PROPOSED_TwoStacks_v0_1_20260801.md`) to amend the Ladder's committed spine with **two coordinates**, read of every persister the climb produces from the first biological rung upward:

- **Identity** — what organized unit is being maintained or reconstructed.
- **Competence** — how far that unit uses present and retained differences to alter the conditions of its own continuation.

The proposal states both are read through AOP's four axes and neither is a fifth axis. It splits identity into a physiological half (present-tense, AOP measures it) and a reconstructive half (diachronic, handed up). It sets no explicit floor for competence.

The proposal's own red team flagged one item it could not check: **whether AOP already contains something that conflicts with or duplicates the competence coordinate**, naming the semantic mask as the suspected neighbour, and stating this must be checked before Ben rules.

That check has now been partly run from the Ladder side. It found more collision than the proposal anticipated, and in a different place than expected. Hence this request.

## 3 · The three collision sites the Ladder found

Stated as findings about AOP's material, for AOP to confirm, correct, or scope.

### 3.1 The proposed competence coordinate appears to be AOP's decoupled-reference criterion

The follow-on §2 develops three modes of holding off equilibrium — *continue* (flame), *correct model-free* (star), *correct model-based* (cell) — and states a candidate criterion for **alive**: a persister whose maintenance corrects the regulated axes against a **decoupled, separately-interventable internal reference for its own viable set.** §3 spells it out as a six-part conjunction, graded as an AOP hypothesis, frontier, explicitly not forced by the cited control theory. §4 restates the discriminator invariantly as a **proper invariant subspace autonomous with respect to the regulated coordinates**, and computes the star/cell separation on two OU systems, with the threshold as the joint condition *load-bearing ∧ decoupled*.

Ladder working conclusion: **if the Ladder sets competence's floor at decoupled regulation, the Ladder has re-derived AOP's criterion under a different name.** That is the arrow violation this note exists to catch.

### 3.2 The proposed identity coordinate appears to overlap AOP's diachronic individuation material

The follow-on §7 develops individuation over time by **continuity of instantiation** (genidentity), fixes it with the transporter and Ship of Theseus contrast, and marks fission as the honest hard case with speciation as process fission. It states that the core paper retains the minimal operational residue — that no two-time comparison is well posed until a **tracking relation** is declared, entering as part of the declaration **D**.

The proposal's "reconstructive identity" appears to be this object. The proposal treats it as AOP handing the question upward. The follow-on treats it as developed, with its branching problem already marked.

### 3.3 Identity is bottlenecked on an AOP-side gap the Ladder cannot close

The follow-on §9 records that AOP §4 as of v1.26 **no longer licenses the inference from a positive minimum-cut result to "this is one individual"** — the inference is deleted, and a verdict now requires an **individuation panel** (autonomy, causal closure, intervention stability, common-cause controls) that neither paper completes. It further records the collective-scale question as **doubly bottlenecked** — on the individuation panel and on the F2 seam, the nested-level non-stationary extension of minimum-cut dependence that AOP §4 scopes out, open in both halves after the v1.22 level-selection retraction (AOP §13a).

Ladder working conclusion: the identity coordinate cannot be committed as a clean read, because the operation it depends on has been withdrawn upstream.

### 3.4 One thing that is not a collision

The semantic mask — the object the proposal suspected — is the **load-bearing-versus-spectator edge discriminator** (per-edge min–max coalition interval, O-information), independently re-verified on 21 July with primitives re-derived from scratch to six digits, with the added finding that the a\* ceiling is gated at coalition cardinality ≥ 3 and vanishes under a small-coalition reading. The mask is the **detector**. What the proposed competence coordinate duplicates is the **criterion the mask detects**, per §3.1. The proposal's suspicion was correctly aimed and landed one step short.

## 4 · What the Ladder is asking AOP to brief

Six questions. All answerable from AOP material. None requires accepting anything from the Ladder.

1. **Scope of the decoupled-reference criterion.** Is it AOP's, and is it the only AOP object in the neighbourhood of "what a unit does across its cut"? Is there anything in v1.26, or in the v1.27 candidate, bearing on this that the follow-on does not carry?

3. **What the core paper actually retains.** The follow-on says the core keeps a one-paragraph pointer to the architecture, one sentence of the diachronic material, the tracking-relation requirement inside **D**, and that the core does not name anything alive. 4. **The individuation panel.** In progress, parked, or unowned? Is the F2 seam being worked? The Ladder is not asking AOP to prioritise either; it is asking whether to write around a permanent gap or wait on a moving one.

**Form of the brief.** Not prescribed, but what would be most usable: a plain statement of what AOP owns in this neighbourhood and at what grade; what is open and whether anyone is working it; what the core retains verbatim; and a direct answer on question 5.
