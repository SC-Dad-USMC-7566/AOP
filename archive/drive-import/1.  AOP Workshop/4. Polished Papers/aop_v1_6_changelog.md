# AOP v1.5 → v1.6 changelog

Version 1.6 answers the follow-up critique of the v1.5 deflation. v1.5 took the predictive costume
off; v1.6 does the work that becomes necessary *once it is off* — it argues why the carving is worth
keeping, fixes one internal contradiction, and closes the reference apparatus honestly. No new claim
is introduced. Six changes.

## D1 — Table 3 D→M chain corrected (was an internal contradiction).
The Table 3 D→M basis line stated the chain as all biconditionals — `E=0 ⟺ i.i.d. ⟺ time-reversible
⟺ σ=0` — which is false and contradicted Figure DM. Time-reversibility does not imply i.i.d. (a
detailed-balance oscillator is reversible with E>0), and σ=0 does not imply E=0 (the "reversible
oscillator remembers for free" point Figure DM is built to show); as written the chain asserted
σ=0 ⟺ E=0, denying its own figure. Replaced with the single direction actually used:
`E=0 ⟺ i.i.d. ⇒ (time-reversible ⇒) σ=0`, contrapositive `σ>0 ⇒ E>0`, converse fails. The Section 4
prose was already correct; only the table overreached. This is a correctness fix, not a claim change.

## D2 — The carving is now argued, not assumed ("why four axes, not one").
Once the prediction posture is disowned, a Perspective earns its keep by beating the incumbent
organization at something. v1.5 never argued that B/D/M/I carves better than the single-axis
incumbents (Krakauer memory-maximizing individuality [6]; free-energy / Markov-blanket boundary [11]).
A new paragraph at the end of Section 1 supplies that argument: the one-axis carving is lossy, and the
loss is visible in concrete persisters it is forced to mis-score (the flame, a maintained boundary
with no memory; the spore, deep stored structure at near-zero drive). The four-fold carving is
justified in the negative — each axis is separated because collapsing that pair mis-describes some
system that persists. Stated as the framework's synthesis-level argument, not a theorem.

## D3 — The diagnostic cases promoted to primary evidence.
The four worked cases (Section 11) were previously framed as illustrations that "each isolate one
claim." They are now the spine: the primary evidence for the carving (D2). The closing paragraph of
Section 11 reframes them as persisters that each force two axes apart and so refute a collapse the
single-axis incumbents would make. "A carving is justified by the distinctions it refuses to lose,
and each case above is one such distinction made physical."

## D4 — Resolvability residue owned as interpretation (finishing the v1.5 C4 retreat).
v1.5 (C4) already retreated from "three fields converged, so it's real" to "inherited
multicollinearity; what's ours is the pairing." v1.6 finishes the retreat. The pairing itself —
whole sharpens as parts blur — is *not* a second fact that survives the deflation: it is the
stiff/sloppy structure of the sloppiness picture (stiff/collective directions stay tight while
sloppy/individual directions diverge) under a different name. What AOP adds is not a phenomenon but a
*reading* of one — the interpretation that this known collective-vs-component dissociation is the
mathematical shadow of the no-ownership refusal. Section 6 now says so plainly: a lens AOP puts on a
fact sloppiness found, not a thing AOP found.

## D5 — Section 8 (screenability) compressed.
Section 8 was still carrying the event-horizon / black-hole set-piece that set up the de Sitter
holography reach removed in v1.5. That paragraph is cut to its remaining content: gravity's only
boundary is the causal light cone of a system's own worldline (the causal boundary of Figure 4, the
one Section 10 leaves to gravitationally bound systems), plus the syntactically-universal /
semantically-null-or-total split. The screenability taxonomy, the two-boundary hydrogen case, and
Figure 4 are retained. Same knife v1.5 took to Section 10.

## D6 — v1.4 references audited against primary sources; VIF citation finalized.
The v1.5 reference note claimed the five v1.4 additions were "each verified against primary sources,"
but no such audit was on record, and it wrongly listed Lenton 2021 as verified "in full text." v1.6
runs the audit and corrects the record:
- **Rosas 2019** — full text; sign convention Ω>0 = redundancy-dominated, Ω<0 = synergy-dominated,
  confirmed verbatim.
- **Comolatti–Hoel 2025** — full text; over a dozen causal measures grounded in a small set of shared
  primitives, agreeing on rank while differing in value, causal emergence found across measures.
- **Boyle 2025** — full text; biogeochemical cycle variants "compete" by climatic-impact phenotype
  under differential persistence.
- **Lenton 2021** — abstract only (paywalled); the claim is confirmed by the abstract, but the v1.5
  "full text" wording was an overclaim and is corrected.
- **Bouchard 2008** — abstract only (closed access); already handled honestly in v1.5, unchanged.
The open variance-inflation-factor citation is finalized to **Marquardt 1970** (Technometrics, the
origin of the term), bibliographic form confirmed; the VIF = 1/(1−R²) identity is standard and
load-bearing on nothing. The reference apparatus now has no open items.

## A note the v1.5 changelog got slightly wrong
The v1.5 changelog said "no claim that survives is weakened." A claim *was* deliberately killed in
v1.5 — the prediction posture. The precise statement is that **nothing true was lost**: the killed
claim was a costume, not a result. Recorded here because it is the kind of precision the project
cares about.
