Abstract

Persistence is a thing resisting erasure along several distinct directions at once. This paper names four such directions—Boundary (space), Drive (energy), Memory (time), and Integration (parts)—each a relative-entropy difference along its own axis (three static cuts and one, Drive, a trajectory asymmetry), and asks how they depend on one another. The separation is graded, not blanket: Memory is near-orthogonal to the rest across generic systems, while Boundary and Integration are dissociable only by construction and otherwise share a plane — so the honest claim is four distinguishable axes, not four independent ones. The result is a two-layer classification. The first layer is a coupling graph whose edges are sorted by modal status: some couplings are compelled by physical law, some are conditional, some are free. The second layer is a semantic mask that separates the couplings causally required for continued existence from those merely present, isolated by intervention on a system’s viability; it is computed here on the paper's one fully-specified system, returning graded weights on load-bearing couplings and exactly zero on a present-but-inert one. The structure is read out relative to the persisting system’s own viability function and is defined only for systems with a timelike worldline. Energy is the hub, but its spokes are uneven: two are forced and scoped to a stated regime—the thermodynamic uncertainty relation prices reliability, and dissipation forces a floor on predictive memory (excess entropy, a corollary of trajectory irreversibility) but reaches no further—while the rest are conditional or free. Pre-registered gates confirm the reach: the Drive floor forces no stored time-asymmetry (the forward/reverse complexity difference Ξ = Cμ⁺−Cμ⁻ is exactly zero at every drive) and no floor on stored complexity, which covaries with the drive parameter only through the generator's time-symmetric sector, not as a forced consequence of the cost (Section 12). The framework also gains a graded, ownership-free individuation axis — integrated information across the minimum information partition — that tells one individual from many where the Integration and resolvability axes cannot, scoped to static Gaussian models (Section 4). A structural consequence sits alongside the hub: mask-resolvability and Integration trade off, so the framework reads most cleanly on weakly integrated systems and blurs on the strongly integrated ones it was built to describe. That blur is two distinct failures — parts unestimable and edges un-isolable — whose ratio is set by the system's coupling topology, making the limit a family rather than a single curve. We locate the observer precisely enough to contain the homunculus problem, bound the theory’s domain by a binding criterion (binding, not rest mass, draws the wall), and illustrate the abstractions on five cases: four spanning full persistence to a bare minimum (crystal, flame, spore, a bound atom), and a star, which occupies a high-drive, high-integration, gravitationally-bound corner where the framework's own resolvability limit is driven from an assertion into a fact computed from stellar structure, and where the time-grain relativity of Memory becomes concrete (a star's excess entropy is defined on its thermal clock but not its nuclear one). Because semantic weight is an observer-relative quantity, the framework's characteristic measurable is not a single weight but the range a weight sweeps as the declared partition, resolution, and viability are varied — the shape a synthesis of this kind commits to, rather than a forecast that could adjudicate it against a rival theory. The organizing notion — that what survives can be selected by differential persistence rather than differential reproduction — is itself established across scales: it has been argued at the level of lineages and processes, and demonstrated for self-perpetuating feedback systems from autocatalytic networks to ecosystems, belief systems, and economies. This is a synthesis of established results, not new physics; claims are tagged throughout as settled, as this framework’s synthesis, or as frontier.

Keywords: persistence · stochastic thermodynamics · semantic information · biological individuality · entropy production · excess entropy · Markov blanket

### 1Introduction and scope

The question of what makes a thing a persisting thing—an individual, a system that holds together—recurs across nonequilibrium physics, computational mechanics, information theory, and theoretical biology, and each field answers it with a different quantity. This paper does not add a quantity. It proposes an organizing structure over the quantities the field already has, and asks a question those literatures rarely pose to one another: how do the distinct dimensions of persistence depend on each other, and which of those dependencies are compelled by physical law?

The claim, in one paragraph, is this. Persistence is resistance to erasure along four directions: a maintained separation of interior from exterior (Boundary), a free-energy throughput that holds the system off equilibrium (Drive), information the present carries that constrains its own future (Memory), and the degree to which many parts act as one irreducible whole (Integration). We fix the primitive at the outset, because two readings of "resistance to erasure" give opposite answers and the difference is load-bearing. The quantity we mean is *lifetime* — how long the system remains instantiated in the set of states from which it can still recover its identity (its viable set) before a specified perturbation spectrum knocks it out, formally the mean first-passage time to erasure — not *occupancy*, the stationary probability mass sitting in that viable set. The two dissociate sharply: a pure divergence-free current leaves the stationary distribution, and hence occupancy, exactly unchanged while changing lifetime severalfold (deposited gate; §12), so a persistence built on occupancy would declare Drive inert where a flame plainly is not. Every primitive phrase this paper uses — resists erasure, how long until it decays, the spore *lasts* — is lifetime language, and lifetime is what the four axes are operations upon. Two guardrails travel with that framing and are not optional. First, the four are *operations on* lifetime, not *currencies of* it: they are non-commensurable and cannot be traded off at a common price — a pre-registered test found no shared cost, with dissipation varying 157% across an iso-persistence surface (§12), so "four ways to raise the same quantity" must never be read as "four exchangeable amounts of one budget." Second, exhibiting the four as operations on lifetime is not a proof that they are *the* operations: completeness (that no fifth axis is needed) and minimality (that none is redundant) remain open, and the primitive does not close them. These four are not independent decorations; they are coupled, and the couplings admit a classification—forced, conditional, or free. Over that classification sits a second layer that distinguishes couplings carrying causal weight for continued existence from couplings that are merely present. The deliverable is that two-layer object: a coupling graph with a semantic mask over it, read out relative to the system’s own viability, and defined only for systems that have a present tense to persist in.

Three commitments bound the scope from the outset, and are kept deliberately because the source literatures repeatedly press against them.

Three load-bearing refusals

No ownership. Which aggregate is the “true” individual, or which self does the inferring, is never the question. The framework classifies couplings and, as of v1.12, admits a graded, third-person measure of how much a coupled system is one individual rather than many (Φ_MIP, Section 4); what it refuses is ownership — individuating by a self-maintenance scalar, the fraction of its own upkeep a system performs on its own behalf. That narrower refusal is not merely cautious: independent attempts to measure ownership directly — to quantify the fraction of its own maintenance a system performs on its own behalf — have failed at exactly this point. A candidate observational measure of self-performed maintenance was found to collapse into an existing quantity (non-trivial informational closure), unable to separate “the system maintains itself while drawing on inputs” from “the environment supplies the structure,” with three independent lines of evidence converging on the conclusion that observational measures cannot make this separation for open systems. The proposed remedy in that literature is to abandon observation for intervention. We take both lessons: ownership is not observationally recoverable, so we decline to individuate by an ownership scalar; and where a directional judgment about coupling is needed, we intervene (the semantic mask, Section 3) rather than correlate. No metaphysics of selfhood or worth. It does not decide what a self is or what a persisting thing is worth; it describes structure. No master quantity. The four dimensions share one mathematical form—a relative entropy—but each is pointed at a different partition: inside versus outside, past versus future, part versus part, forward versus reverse trajectory. The common form is what makes them commensurable; the different targets are what keep them independent. It is a mistake to read the shared form as the cause of the dissociation—the form would, if anything, invite collapse; the distinct partitions are what force four axes rather than one. The unification is also thinner than it first appears, since nearly every quantity in information theory is a relative entropy: the shared operation is a common language, not a hidden master quantity. It licenses pluralism; it does not license reduction.

Nothing below claims a new law of physics. Every forced edge is tied to a named, published result; every inference this framework makes on its own is flagged as such (Section 12). The contribution, if there is one, is the assembly and its classification.

Why four axes, and not the field's one. A synthesis of this kind has to answer for its carving: the incumbent accounts organize persistence along a single axis. The leading information-theoretic account of individuality identifies the individual as the partition that maximizes a memory-like quantity (autonomy) [6], and free-energy / Markov-blanket accounts read persistence off a single inference boundary [11]. A one-axis carving is not wrong; it is lossy, and the loss is visible in concrete persisters that the single axis is forced to mis-score. A flame is a real persister — a sharp, actively maintained boundary — carrying essentially no memory; an axis that defines the boundary by maximizing memory cannot represent it without either denying it a boundary or importing a memory it does not have (Section 11). A spore is a real persister with its drive switched off and deep structure locked in escrow; because its excess entropy E can read near zero while its stored complexity Cμ is large [13], any account that scores persistence on a single memory-as-E axis reads it as nearly inert, and any drive-centric account reads it as dead. Neither is true. The four-fold carving earns its keep in exactly this negative sense: each axis is separated from the others because collapsing that pair mis-describes some system that persists. This is the framework's synthesis-level argument, not a theorem — the evidence for it is the worked diagnostic cases (Section 11), each of which is a persister that forces two axes apart. It also fixes the count from below rather than above: at least these four, because each is needed to keep one diagnostic case honest, and the count is held open for the same reason (Section 2).


A structural category is not a metaphysics of selfhood. Version 1.13 adds a threshold — *alive*  
(Section 11a) — and a diachronic identity criterion for a persisting process (Section 4a). Neither  
breaches the refusals. "Alive" is defined as a structural property of a coupling architecture (a  
decoupled model-based regulator, below), not as a self, a worth, or an owner; it is read out by  
the same third-person mask and generator spectrum the rest of the paper uses, and it individuates  
nothing by an ownership scalar. The diachronic criterion says when two time-slices belong to *one  
process*, a question about causal continuity, not about which aggregate is the true self. The  
homunculus stays contained (Section 7): everywhere "model of its own viability" appears, the  
model is a material structure and the comparison to it is a physical process, never an evaluation  
performed by anyone.

### 2The object: four dimensions as differences

A persisting thing holds a difference-that-could-be-otherwise along four directions. Crucially, none of the four is a substance one can point at. Each is a computed difference across a cut—a mutual information—that is never observed directly. One observes residues, bent flows, and locked shells, and infers the difference behind them. The event horizon makes this unavoidable: a horizon casts only a shadow—the dark disk in a horizon image is the critical photon ring, not the horizon itself—and the horizon is inferred from what it does to surrounding light. Every dimension is like this. One gets the shadow, never the thing.

Drive is the odd one out. It is not a mutual information across a static cut but the time-asymmetry of the dynamics: entropy production equals the relative entropy (Kullback–Leibler divergence) between the forward and time-reversed trajectory distributions [1]. So all four are relative-entropy quantities pointed along independent axes—forward-versus-reverse trajectory (Drive, directional), past-versus-future state (Memory, time-symmetric), inside-versus-outside (Boundary), and part-versus-part (Integration). The count is held open—at least these four, not a claim that exactly four exhaust the phenomenon. Two of them, Drive and Memory, are computable with no choices to make. Two, Boundary and Integration, exist only relative to a declared partition: which states are “inside,” which parts are “the parts.” That partition-dependence is a permanent scope limit, stated at the outset rather than patched. Boundary and Integration are the soft dimensions precisely because someone must declare the cut, and the answer moves with it.

Integration carries a second, subtler ambiguity beyond the partition: there is no single canonical measure of it. Six distinct candidate measures of integrated information have been reviewed and compared, and on Gaussian network models no two show consistent agreement across all analyses; some can even go negative [12]. This is an instance of a broader regularity: across more than a dozen independently developed measures of causation, the measures turn out to be near-rediscoveries of a small set of shared primitives, agreeing on structural conclusions (such as causal emergence) while differing in the values they assign [Comolatti & Hoel 2025] — direction survives the choice of measure; magnitude does not. We confirmed the qualitative pattern directly — computing two of the closed-form measures on thousands of coupled Gaussian systems, they agree in rank (Spearman ≈ 0.87) but disagree in value, and whole-minus-sum integrated information is negative for a small fraction of systems. AOP does not attempt to resolve this; it inherits the ambiguity but not its stakes, because it uses Integration as one descriptive axis among four, not as a measure of consciousness. For all worked computations we therefore declare an operational default: total correlation, TC = Σ H(Xᵢ) − H(X) [Watanabe 1960], the member of the family that is non-negative by construction, monotone in coupling, and closed-form in the Gaussian case. Where the synergy/redundancy character of the interdependence matters — not just its magnitude — the O-information [Rosas, Mediano, Gastpar & Jensen 2019] is the natural signed companion, negative for synergy-dominated and positive for redundancy-dominated structure; we note it as the measure to reach for when that distinction is load-bearing. This is a choice of operational proxy, not a claim that it is the uniquely correct measure. Accordingly, every Integration-dependent claim in this paper is stated in its directional form — all reasonable measures rise with coupling — and no exact Integration value is load-bearing. Where "simultaneous segregation and integration" is specifically what one wants to capture, causal density is the measure to prefer [12].

### 3Two layers: syntactic correlation and semantic mask

The four dimensions as usually measured are syntactic—plain Shannon correlations, which by themselves carry no meaning. Persistence turns on the semantic subset: the correlations causally necessary for the system to maintain its own existence. The distinction, and the method for drawing it, are due to Kolchinsky and Wolpert [2], who define semantic information as the syntactic information a system holds about its environment that is causally necessary for it to keep itself in a low-entropy state, and operationalize “causally necessary” by counterfactual intervention—scramble a correlation, re-run the dynamics, and measure whether viability drops. What survives scrambling was decorative; what reduces viability when scrambled was load-bearing. Two adaptations should be flagged. Kolchinsky and Wolpert define semantic information for the system–environment correlation; applying the same scramble-and-re-run test to a system’s internal couplings, as we do here, is an extension of their method rather than a direct application of their result. And the test presupposes that the scrambled coupling can be intervened on in isolation—an assumption that fails on strongly integrated systems (Section 6).

This is the central organizing move: a syntactic coupling graph, with a semantic mask over it. Classification then means locating each coupling on both axes at once—its modal status (forced, conditional, or free) and its semantic weight (does scrambling it drop viability?). An edge can be present but semantically inert, or thin but load-bearing.

One point must be stated plainly, because it governs how the second layer may legitimately be read. Semantic weight is a quantity, not a law. Like every dimension magnitude in Table 1, it is observer-relative: its value depends on the declared partition, the resolution, and the viability functional chosen, exactly as Integration’s value does (Section 6). The mask is well defined as a construction; its numbers are not invariants. Treating a semantic weight as a fixed, observer-independent coordinate would import a determinacy the framework does not possess and does not claim.

The mask is not left as a construction to be imagined; it is computed here on the one system in the paper whose dynamics are fully specified, the driven three-state ring of Figure DM (Figure MW). Each internal coupling is scrambled to its detailed-balanced form in isolation, the nonequilibrium steady state is recomputed, and the fractional drop in a present-tense viability functional is read off as that edge's semantic weight. The mask returns graded, positive weights on the three driven ring edges and exactly zero on an added spectator coupling that carries no directed drive — the present-but-inert case the second layer exists to name. Because semantic weight is observer-relative, each weight is reported not as a point but as the interval it sweeps across three present-tense viability choices — steady-state current, entropy production, and relaxation rate — and that interval is the characteristic measurable of Section 13. Two honesty conditions attach and are stated with the figure: the spectator's zero is exact by construction (an already-balanced coupling has no directed drive to scramble), so it demonstrates that the mask does not reward mere presence rather than discovering that this coupling is inert; and the near-agreement of the three functionals on which edge is most load-bearing is a property of the chosen rates, not a rank-invariance regularity — across random driven rates the ordering is not preserved. Like the star's resolvability signature, Figure MW is a demonstration of self-consistency — the mask was always going to return some number — establishing that the number is well defined, that it separates load-bearing from inert, and that its observer-relative spread is a real computable interval; it is not a test that could have failed.

### 4The coupling graph and its classification

Energy is the hub, but its spokes are not alike. This uneven hub is the spine of the structure.

Drive → Memory: dissipation forces a memory floor. Any sustained dissipation leaves some trace: a strictly positive entropy production rate implies a strictly positive excess entropy (σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0). Here excess entropy is E = I(past; future), the past–future mutual information of computational mechanics [13]; it should not be confused with the "excess entropy production" of steady-state stochastic thermodynamics, an unrelated quantity that lives on the Drive axis. Unlike the brittle earlier reading, this edge is not a fresh directional guess but a scoped corollary of the trajectory-irreversibility identity we already invoke for Drive [1]. For a stationary process described by a single complete state, E = 0 holds if and only if past and future are independent, i.e. the process is i.i.d.; an i.i.d. process equals its own time-reverse in distribution, so its entropy production vanishes. The contrapositive is exactly σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0. Figure DM confirms this on a driven Markov ring: every dissipating configuration carries E > 0, while the single detailed-balance configuration has σ = 0 with E > 0 — the converse fails, a reversible oscillator remembers for free, so the necessity runs one way only.

The implication is secure but scoped, and three conditions must be stated or it is either false or vacuous. First, σ and E must be read on the same complete description: coarse-graining can hide a nonequilibrium current so that a reduced observable looks reversible (its E reads near zero) while the full dynamics dissipate — the Memory-axis twin of the observer-relativity of σ itself (Section 5). Second, the process must be stationary, the regime in which σ = D(forward ∥ reverse) is the clean object used here. Third, and as before, what Drive forces is a memory floor (E > 0), not memory depth: it guarantees that some past–future correlation exists, not that the load-bearing memory is large. That the retained past has a thermodynamic price at all, and that the price falls on its wasteful part, is the content of the thermodynamics of prediction: the dissipated work is proportional to the *nonpredictive* retained information — the memory a system keeps that does not help predict its future — so efficiency is what drives stored memory to be predictive, and only predictive memory is free [3]. The spore is the standing witness — near-zero dissipation carrying deep stored structure (Section 11) — showing that the quantity Drive compels (E) and the quantity that does the persisting work (statistical complexity Cμ) can come apart [13]. A pre-registered gate makes this coming-apart quantitative rather than illustrative: on a driven Markov ring, sweeping the cycle current leaves the causal irreversibility Ξ = Cμ⁺ − Cμ⁻ (the forward/reverse statistical-complexity asymmetry [13]) exactly zero at every dissipation rate — the forward and reverse causal-state reconstructions stay equal in complexity however hard the ring is driven. The stored complexity Cμ itself is not fixed — it covaries with the drive parameter — but that covariation carries no floor (it is weak, and it tracks the parameter's reshaping of the generator, not the dissipation rate: driving at a fixed reversible skeleton moves Cμ by ~1% while σ̇ moves twentyfold) and, decisively, it imposes no stored time-asymmetry at all. The drive writes its arrow into E; the stored-structure quantities either ignore the current (Ξ exactly) or merely drift alongside it with no forced floor (Cμ magnitude). So the floor Drive forces reaches predictive memory (E) and stops there, forcing neither a floor on stored complexity nor any stored time-asymmetry. The one edge that bites is the predictive-memory floor (E > 0); the D → M spoke does not reach the stored-structure quantities at all. (An adversarial re-test of that gate first produced a spurious positive — a trajectory-reversal quantity that was the entropy-production rate wearing a memory label — and its retraction is recorded in the status table and the gate ledger below.) Within these conditions the edge is a forced, scoped consequence of a cited law, and it joins the thermodynamic uncertainty relation as the hub's second clean forced spoke rather than a hedged inference.

That the stored-structure quantities escape the Drive floor is not an accident of this one edge; it reflects a structural fact about the whole hub, which a series of pre-registered gates on candidate Drive–Memory and Drive–resolvability joints made explicit (Section 12, gate ledger). In both model classes examined — Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck dynamics and finite Markov chains — the thermodynamic cost σ̇ is a functional of the generator's *antisymmetric* current part, while the memory- and structure-side quantities are governed by its *time-symmetric* part. The split is exact for the two quantities that carry the framework's forced claims: in the OU case the equilibrium covariance Σ that sets resolvability is Lyapunov-invariant to the driving circulation Q, and in the Markov case the causal asymmetry Ξ = Cμ⁺ − Cμ⁻ is identically zero at every drive. It is looser for the magnitudes: the stored complexity Cμ does covary with a drive parameter, because that parameter reshapes the symmetric part of the generator too (and Cμ retains a weak residual sensitivity to the current itself). But that covariation is shared-input — the pseudo-joint the gates were built to exclude — not a forced coupling through the current, and it carries no floor: no intervention on the dissipation rate forces a stored-structure quantity through a shared mechanism. This is why the hub's only forced spokes are the two floor-type edges (E > 0 and the TUR reliability bound) and no more: a forced cross-sector coupling would require a single quantity that depends on the antisymmetric sector as its *mechanism*, and among the tested quantities only σ̇ and E do — E being exactly the one the D → M floor already names. This is why the hub's forced spokes are exactly the two floor-type edges (E > 0 and the TUR reliability bound) and no more — a forced cross-sector coupling would require one quantity to depend on both sectors at once, which none of the tested quantities does. The claim is offered as framework synthesis, secure within the two model classes examined and argued but not proven beyond them; the generalization to arbitrary dynamics is the frontier step. Any quantity that appears to couple the two sectors should be checked for the failure mode the gate ledger records: one sector mislabeled as the other (an entropy-production rate read as a memory asymmetry, or a drift-map scale factor read as a resolvability trade).

Drive → Reliability is forced and regime-bounded. The precision of any current a system runs is bounded by its dissipation: the thermodynamic uncertainty relation prices steadiness [4], [5]. Within the classical Markov regime in which it is proven, this is a law.

Drive → Boundary is conditional. Holding a gradient costs nothing at equilibrium—a potential well is free—and costs drive only when the boundary must be maintained against a leak, with the price set by how leaky it is.

Drive → Integration is free at equilibrium, open under selection. Parts can be correlated at zero dissipation; integration exists statically for free. Whether drive builds or maintains integration over time is a separate, unsettled question, and conflating the two is an error.

Individuation: one individual, or many. Integration measured as total correlation reports how much shared variance a system carries, but it cannot separate one individual from many: two independent, internally rich modules score high total correlation and high per-component resolvability blur, yet they are plainly two things and not one. The quantity that separates these is integrated information across the system’s minimum information partition — Φ_MIP, the mutual information across the least-disrupting bipartition [Aguilera & Di Paolo 2019]. It is exactly zero on any block-decomposable system (a pile of separate parts) and rises, graded, as the parts become jointly irreducible into one whole. It is a third-person coupling measure carrying no self-ownership scalar, and it is the irreducibility flavor of integration, distinct from the redundancy flavor total correlation captures — which is why it, and not total correlation, reads two independent modules as two. Its individuation ordering is invariant under per-component rescaling and robust across minimum-partition normalizations, so it needs no analogue of the resolvability K-convention; it does inherit the integration-measure family’s scope, being the minimum-cut member rather than a canonical Φ. This axis is established here only in the static Gaussian setting and only for the one-vs-many question at a fixed partition; selecting which level of a nested system is the individual, and the non-stationary and critical extensions (where the same construction is defined without stationarity and diverges at criticality), are frontier (Section 13). It is graded framework synthesis — computed by a pre-registered five-criterion gate (deposited), not asserted — and it narrows the framework’s “no individuation” refusal to “no ownership”.

The three couplings that do not pass through energy—Boundary–Memory, Memory–Integration, and Boundary–Integration—are where the genuine open work sits. The current wager is that they are contingent and dissociable, except where a particular construction welds one. In the leading information-theoretic account of biological individuality [6], the boundary is defined as the partition that maximizes a memory quantity (autonomy), which welds Boundary to Memory by construction. But that weld is imposed by a definition, not compelled by a law—and the flame is the standing counter-witness: a sharp boundary with essentially no memory at all (Section 11). Memory–Integration and Boundary–Integration remain genuinely open, and their answers depend on which contested measure of integration one adopts (Section 6). Table 2 records the classification.

### 4a Diachronic individuation: when two slices are one process

a fixed time. Persistence also poses the orthogonal, *diachronic* question: when do two time-slices

This is a settled position imported, not a  
new invention; what is this framework's is the mapping onto the four axes and the present-tense  
principle. **[SETTLED named view; the mapping is SYNTHESIS.]**  
  
Two contrasting thought experiments fix the criterion, and their difference *is* the result. In the  
**transporter/de-extinction** case the process stops and a *description* is re-enacted elsewhere;  
the result is a look-alike, a cover version, not the resumed original — the recreated thing is a new  
process that happens to match a record. In the **Ship of Theseus** case continuity of instantiation  
holds *through total material turnover*, because the pattern is never interrupted; it is the same  
ship though it keeps no original plank. The two cases involve the *same* total substance swap; the  
only difference is whether instantiation was unbroken (Theseus, same process) or  
interrupted-and-re-enacted-from-a-record (transporter, new process). Continuity is unbroken  
instantiation, not preserved material. The verdict rests on the *physical* fact, which is
determinate: was the causal chain of instantiation interrupted? The transporter interrupts it; Theseus
does not. "Same process ≡ unbroken instantiation" is then this framework's stipulated definition, and
"the transporter makes a new process" follows *by that definition*, not by a discovery about identity.
This is consistent with the deflationary reading of personal identity on which there is "no further
fact" beyond continuity itself [Parfit 1984] — no identity fact is claimed *beyond* continuity;
continuity is defined as the fact. (Parfit's thesis is precisely that in such cases there is no
determinate further identity fact to find; the framework does not read a determinate metaphysical
verdict out of his view, it stipulates a physical criterion and reports its consequence.) This is
exactly what the genidentity criterion asserts.

(Section 9). **Death = interruption of instantiation** — not loss of material (Theseus keeps none)  
and not loss of the description (a frozen genome is intact). The mammoth species stopped being  
sailed when the last individual died; its recoverable genome is a description, not a paused process,  
so de-extinction would begin a new process, not resume the old. The operational test is present-

### 5Laws versus quantities: the scale claim, done carefully

A brittle early form of this framework held that “forced means scale-invariant”—that a forced coupling, being a law, holds unchanged from molecules to institutions. That reading breaks the moment one notices that measured irreversibility is itself scale-dependent, and that coarse-graining can create or destroy apparent causal structure [7].

The repaired claim is sharper and survives: the laws are observer-independent; the magnitudes are observer-relative. A forced coupling transfers across scale because it is a law—the second-law inequality σ ≥ 0 does not care about one’s grain. But how much boundary, how much memory, how much σ a system “has” depends entirely on the cut and resolution at which it is measured. The relation is invariant; the number is not. Figure 1 shows this directly: coarse-grained entropy production swings by tens of fold with the observer’s grain (roughly seventy-fold in the model of Figure 1), while the law σ ≥ 0 holds at every grain. That entropy production is measured at all in real systems—in living cells [8] and in the human brain [9]—is what lets the forced, law-tied quantities touch reality; the observer-relative magnitudes should stay scoped to a declared cut.

This relativity has a second face on the time axis, and there it cuts deeper, because one of the four numerators is not merely rescaled by the grain but *defined* relative to it. Boundary, Integration, and σ shift in magnitude with the spatial cut; Memory's excess-entropy numerator E shifts with the temporal one — and can cease to be defined — because E is defined only for a stationary process [Crutchfield & Feldman 2003], and stationarity is a claim about a chosen time-scale. A process stationary at one temporal resolution need not be stationary at another; cross a non-stationarity and E does not merely change value, it loses its definition. This is the time-axis instance of the same claim, not a new one: the law (the memory floor E ≥ 0, within its regime) is grain-independent, while the magnitude E is grain-relative — and on the time axis its very definedness is grain-relative. That complexity is accordingly quoted at a declared temporal grain is routine in the one field that measures it at cosmic scale: the statistical complexity of the cosmic web is reported over a "≈ 200 Myr timescale" [Vazza 2020]. The worked consequence is drawn out in Section 11 — a star is stationary on its thermal clock and non-stationary on its nuclear one, so its Memory has no single value until the clock is named. That E requires stationarity and that complexity is grain-relative are settled results; reading the time-grain as the exact twin of the spatial-grain limit is this framework's synthesis.

### 6The two-axis view

Every coupling lives at a point in a plane: modal status (free → conditional → forced) against semantic weight (inert → load-bearing). Persistence lives up-and-to-the-right—couplings both forced and load-bearing. The modal axis alone was never enough, because an edge can be present but semantically inert, or thin but load-bearing. Two knobs, not one.

The two axes are not of the same kind, and Figure 2 must be read with that asymmetry in mind. The vertical (modal) axis is a law axis: forced/conditional/free is observer-independent. The horizontal (semantic) axis is a quantity axis: as argued in Section 3, semantic weight is observer-relative, so an edge’s horizontal position is not a single invariant coordinate but a value that shifts with the declared partition, resolution, and viability functional. The point placements in Figure 2 are illustrative, chosen to convey rank and region, not measured magnitudes. Read honestly, each edge is better pictured as a horizontal range than as a pinned point—the width of that range is itself a diagnostic (how far the weight moves as one varies the declaration), and measuring it is the natural first computational task the framework invites.

### A resolvability limit: the mask blurs as Integration rises

There is a sharper structural consequence worth stating plainly. The semantic mask is defined by intervening on one coupling at a time—scramble an edge in isolation, re-run, and read the viability drop. That procedure presupposes the edges are separately manipulable. But high Integration is precisely the regime in which they are not: when parts are strongly interdependent, no single coupling can be scrambled without dragging the others with it, and the per-edge weight the mask is supposed to return is no longer well defined. The two axes of Figure 2 therefore trade off against each other. The more a system relies on Integration—the more it is the kind of unified, tightly coupled object the framework most wants to describe—the less sharply its semantic weights can be resolved. This is not a defect of the instrument to be engineered away; it is a genuine limit — by analogy (not by proof) reminiscent of an uncertainty relation, a tension between how integrated a system is and how finely its internal semantics can be resolved. We intend the analogy loosely: there are no conjugate variables and no quantitative bound here, and the honest, measurable form of the tension is the one given in Section 13 — the width a semantic weight sweeps as the system's integration rises. It bites hardest on exactly the systems the framework exists to describe, and Section 13 treats it as a first-class limitation rather than a footnote.

This width is quantified in a minimal Gaussian model of n interdependent components with a single integration knob (equicorrelation ρ, Integration measured as total correlation). Two things happen together as ρ rises. The uncertainty in a per-component semantic weight diverges as (1 − ρ)^(−1/2) — equivalently as 1/√λ_min of the coupling matrix — while the aggregate, whole-system weight simultaneously *sharpens*. The individual parts become unresolvable at exactly the rate the whole becomes better determined.

The per-component divergence, taken by itself, is not a discovery about persistence: it is the ordinary statistics of estimating individual parameters in a correlated system, and it appears under three names already — multicollinearity in econometrics (the variance-inflation factor is the same 1/(1−ρ) form [Marquardt 1970]), parameter sloppiness in physics and systems biology (the Fisher-information spectrum spans orders of magnitude; sloppy directions are unconstrained while stiff directions and predictions are tight [Transtrum, Machta & Sethna, Phys. Rev. Lett. 104, 060201 (2010); Transtrum et al., J. Chem. Phys. 143, 010901 (2015)]), and attribution non-uniqueness in partial information decomposition [Williams & Beer, arXiv:1004.2515 (2010)]. The semantic mask inherits this limit rather than discovering it; that it does so is recorded as a limitation (Section 13), not claimed as evidence for the framework. Nor is the *pairing* — the whole becoming sharply resolvable at the same rate its parts become individually unresolvable — a second fact that survives the deflation. It is the same fact: the stiff/sloppy structure of the sloppiness picture, in which stiff, collective directions stay tightly constrained while sloppy, individual directions blow up [Transtrum, Machta & Sethna 2010; Transtrum et al. 2015]. "Whole sharpens as parts blur" is a restatement of "stiff stays tight while sloppy diverges," not an independent discovery about persistence. What AOP adds is therefore not a phenomenon but a reading of one: the interpretation that this already-known collective-vs-component dissociation is the mathematical shadow of the framework's refusal to individuate (the "no ownership" refusal). That interpretive link — sloppiness's stiff/sloppy split seen as the quantitative face of no-ownership — is the organizing observation, and it is a lens the framework puts on a fact sloppiness found, not a falsifiable forecast. Two refinements sharpen this limit without adding to it. The first is that the blur the mask suffers is not one failure but two, and the opening sentences of this section name both without separating them. There is an *inferential* failure — per-component attribution variance diverges (the variance-inflation factor), so no single edge's weight is separately *estimable* — and a distinct *interventional* failure — because coupled parts share a generator, an intervention on one drags the others, so an edge cannot be *isolated* even in principle by a do-operation. These are the two halves of the mask's own procedure (isolate an edge, re-run, read the drop): the second failure blocks the isolation, the first blocks the reading. They do not travel together. The second refinement is that their ratio is fixed by the system's coupling *topology*, so the resolvability limit is not one curve but a family. The equicorrelation model above is only its mean-field member — every component coupled equally to every other — and it is the degenerate case, with a two-level spectrum (one stiff collective mode, the rest identical). Give the same n components a different wiring and the signature moves in a specified way (Figure TF): a nearest-neighbour chain gives a graded stiff-to-sloppy band with high inferential blur and low interventional drag; a sparse hub-dominated network (a food web) gives the reverse, severe drag but mild inferential blur; a symmetric intervention on the collective mode blurs neither. In every topology the aggregate, whole-system mode stays sharply defined (its variance is conserved to numerical precision) while the parts blur — so the pairing of Section 6 is a property of the family, not of the one model. That the blur decomposes into an estimation failure and an isolation failure is a standard reading of the covariance; that their mix is set by coupling topology, with the aggregate mode invariant across topologies, is this framework's synthesis, computed here (Figure TF) and not asserted.

One convention has to be fixed for any of this to be well posed, because resolvability is coordinate-dependent. Conditioning is not invariant under a change of parameterization: the stiff/sloppy ratio of a coupling read on the precision partition K = Σ⁻¹ is not the same number as the one read on a fused drift generator that absorbs a driving current, and the two disagree exactly when the drive is nonzero. The gate ledger (Section 12) made this concrete — a null computed on the fused object was uninformative precisely because the object was the wrong one. The framework therefore fixes a single convention, applied everywhere including where it yields a null: resolvability is read on the coupling/precision partition K, with any driving current treated as a co-estimated nuisance rather than folded into the resolved object. This is the natural specialization of the "range over the declared partition" language above — the declared partition is K — and it is the choice under which the mask's own scramble-one-edge procedure and the resolvability limit refer to the same object.

### 7The observer, located

“Observer” does real work here, so it must be pinned down rather than left as a black box. At every grain an observer does exactly three things: it declares a cut; it reaches the far side only by intervention (information comes only by posing a question); and it has a stake. What physically plays the observer changes by grain—a coarse-graining at the thermodynamic grain, a model partition at the statistical grain, an intervener at the causal grain. At the grain that matters for persistence, the observer is nothing external: it is the system’s own viability function. Semantic information is meaningful to a system, relative to its viability [2]; the frame that fixes which differences matter is the persisting system itself.

This is where a homunculus threatens to re-enter, and the objection deserves a direct answer rather than an assertion. If the system’s viability fixes which differences matter, who evaluates the viability function? If the answer is “we do, from outside,” the external mind walks back in. The defense is a chain, and every link must be explicit. (i) By the present-tense principle (Section 9), a legitimate viability function is checkable now. (ii) A property checkable now is a structural fact about the current state, not an assessment performed by anyone—the configuration either is or is not viable, whether or not a witness scores it. (iii) What plays the evaluator is selection, which is agentless: the frequency of good environments pays out the wager, with no one at the wheel [10]. Here a distinction earns its keep: what is observer-independent is only the bare fact that the current state either is or is not viable—the magnitude or ranking of viability remains relative to a declared functional V, and the anti-homunculus argument needs only the former. Therefore no external evaluator is required for the existence-fact, and the homunculus does not return. The claim rests on the present-tense principle being airtight; where that principle is soft, the anti-homunculus move is soft, and we mark it accordingly. This is containment, not dissolution: the evaluator role is located and bounded rather than made to vanish.

### 8What can make a boundary: screenability

If a boundary is any physical interaction surface, one must ask which interactions can actually make one. A statistical boundary—inside conditionally independent of outside given the interface—requires the interaction to be screenable, i.e. interruptible. Electromagnetism screens (opposite charges, a Faraday cage, a membrane). The strong interaction confines rather than screens, and the weak interaction is intrinsically short-ranged (its mediators are massive); neither builds a statistical boundary at the scales where persisters live. Gravity cannot be screened: there is no negative mass and its range is infinite, so it forms no statistical boundary at all. It is the anti-boundary interaction (Figure 3).

Figure 3. Which interactions can make a boundary. Electromagnetism screens and so builds membranes; the strong force confines and the weak force is short-ranged, so neither makes a statistical boundary; gravity, being unscreenable and infinite-ranged, forms no statistical boundary—its only boundary is the causal horizon. Gravity therefore imposes an unscreenable floor on Integration: nothing is ever perfectly decoupled. This floor is not a synthesis of this paper but a settled property of gravitating systems: gravity is a long-range interaction (potential ∼1/r, decaying as 1/rᵅ with α ≤ the spatial dimension), and long-range interactions are non-additive — the energy of a whole is not the sum of its subsystems' energies, so subsystems never fully decouple [Campa, Dauxois & Ruffo 2009]. Negative specific heat and ensemble inequivalence, historically first found for gravitating systems, are the thermodynamic signatures of exactly this non-additivity, and the same property underwrites the star's self-regulation (Section 11).

Because gravity cannot make a screening boundary, the only boundary it makes is causal — the light cone of a system's own worldline, a one-way membrane fixed by global structure, not local shielding (this is the causal boundary of Figure 4, and the one §10 leaves to gravitationally bound systems). One consequence is worth stating, and it must be scoped carefully. Gravity is syntactically universal but semantically null for most persisters (scramble a cell's gravitational tie to a distant star and nothing dies). Where it turns semantically total is a gravitationally bound persister that carries *no active radiative screen* — a galaxy is the clean case: mostly empty, starlight free-streams, nothing is screened, and gravity is the entire boundary, membrane-free. A star is not that case. A star is gravitationally bound, so gravity supplies its causal boundary and holds its mass, but it also carries a real screening boundary — the photosphere, an electromagnetic skin built exactly as Section 8 says a screenable force builds one (the interior is optically thick, radiation random-walks out, and inside is conditionally independent of outside given the surface). The star therefore carries two load-bearing boundaries of different force-types at once, escalating the hydrogen atom's "one persister, two boundaries" (Figure 4) from a low-stakes pairing — a nearly free light cone around a cheap EM skin — to one where both boundaries do heavy work: scramble the photospheric opacity and the star stops being a star. "Membrane-free" is true of galaxies, not of stars.

The two notions of boundary—the informational Markov blanket and the thermodynamic gradient-maintenance cost—are genuinely distinct, and conflating them is a known error. The information-theoretic blanket is observer-relative by nature: one must first choose which node is “inside,” after which the blanket appears, and the instrumental and realist readings of that construction should not be run together [11]. A bound hydrogen atom illustrates that one persister can carry two kinds of boundary at once (Figure 4): a cheap, screenable electromagnetic skin — the electron in its Coulomb well, costing essentially no drive — and an unscreenable causal boundary, the light cone of its own worldline. The screening boundary is material, local, and probeable; the causal boundary is non-material and global. The two are distinct in kind, and a single system routinely has both.

### 9Present-tense viability

Any legitimate viability function must be checkable at the present instant. A viability that requires foresight—“this will eventually reproduce”—is illegitimate; it is hindsight worn as a goal. Persistence maintains; it never aims. Apparent foresight, such as a spore seeming to wait for spring, is a present capacity that past environments happened to favor; the wager is structural and retrospective, placed by no one and paid out by the frequency of good environments [10]. This kills teleology cleanly without denying that things can be poised for a future, and it is the principle on which the anti-homunculus chain of Section 7 depends. Present-tense viability splits into three checkable types: staying in a shape (configuration), keeping a process above threshold (process), and staying able to act (capacity)—the three exemplified by the crystal, the flame, and the spore respectively (Section 11).


Lineages do not breach the present-tense principle; they enter it at two admitted points and no  
third. First, *existence now*: a lineage exists now iff it is instantiated now — at least one  
member presently viable — a pure present-tense fact requiring no foresight, and one that fails  
exactly at the instant the last member dies. Second, as a *higher-order individual*: where a  
lineage is itself integrated enough to count as one thing (Φ_MIP > 0 at the lineage partition,  
Section 4), its net reproduction is a present-tense flux — a Drive in this paper's sense —  
maintaining the lineage-form off its extinction-equilibrium on the lineage's own clock (Section  
5), and the lineage carries its own four-axis profile as any persister does. This second route is
available *in principle only*: it leans on Φ_MIP at a cross-generational, non-stationary partition —
exactly the nested level-selection extension Section 4 scopes out of the settled static-Gaussian axis
and marks **frontier**. It is offered as a direction, not an in-hand criterion. What the principle  
continues to refuse is the third move: reading a lineage's *future continuation* ("this lineage  
will persist") as a present-tense fact. That claim is not recoverable now and is the province of  
selection, one level above this framework. So a doomed but still-instantiated lineage has not  
"failed" now — its members persist, its doom is a future-tense selective fact the framework does  
not adjudicate, and it fails (stops existing) only when instantiation is interrupted. Propagation  
as differential continuation is thereby placed *above* AOP, at the scope wall where present-tense  
persistence hands off to the theory of evolution; it is a cross-project (Ladder) propagation-bus  
item, not a fifth dimension. **[Existence-now route: SYNTHESIS. Higher-individual route: FRONTIER (rests on the nested-level Φ_MIP extension, Section 4). A propagation-bus item.]**

persistence separates from propagation, and it is openly unresolved. Faithful copying degrades:

Muller's ratchet [Muller 1964; Felsenstein 1974]. Recombination can purge them, repairing a poor  
copy against a good one and breaking the linkage that lets errors ride along [Felsenstein 1974].  
One leading hypothesis holds that the genetic variation recombination produces is a *by-product*  
of recombinational repair rather than its function [Bernstein, Byerly, Hopf & Michod 1985]; if it  
holds, it weakens the tidy reading on which copying is persistence while mixing is search, since  
mixing would then be persistence-of-the-blueprint throughout. But the maintenance of recombination  
remains a genuinely open problem [Otto 2009], and AOP rests on no resolution of it. The material  
sits above the scope wall — it concerns across-generation lineages (Section 9) — and is recorded  
here only to mark the boundary honestly. **This note is dated and provisional: it is to be  
re-checked against the literature on a fixed cadence and removed or revised if the field settles  
the question or moves away from the recombinational-repair reading.** [FRONTIER, July 2026]

### 9a Individuality at the collective scale: the two-level reading

Section 4 fixes when a set of parts is *one* thing rather than a cloud — Φ_MIP > 0 at the
part-partition, the cut into parts stops being clean — and Section 9 fixes what a persister may
assert about itself in the present tense, placing reproduction and lineage-continuation *above* the
framework. Put the two together and a recurring question in the study of biological individuality
falls into place with a sharp division of labour: when a loose collection of persisters becomes a
single higher persister, which part of that event is inside AOP and which part is above it?

The ordinary word for a part's "self-interest" fuses two things that come apart cleanly on the scope
wall, and separating them is this section's synthesis. The first is **self-maintenance** — a part
running its own boundary, drive, and repair. This is nothing but the present-tense viability of
Section 9 read at the part scale: a part must keep it, because nothing else can metabolize for it,
and a part that loses it is a puddle with nothing for the collective to integrate. It is *inside*
AOP. The second is a **private reproductive stake** — an evolutionary interest in the part's own
propagation, separate from the whole's. That is a claim about differential continuation across
generations, and it sits *above* the wall, in the province of selection (Section 9). The two are
independent precisely because they live on two different levels, and the durable collectives are the
ones in which the parts keep the first and surrender the second: a somatic cell that fully forgoes
its own reproduction, a mitochondrion that has shipped most of its genome to the nucleus and cannot
reproduce outside the cell. A part that *retains* a private stake inside a whole that had suppressed
it is the failure mode the medical literature calls cancer — a breakdown of exactly the cooperation
that made the whole one thing [Aktipis et al. 2015, verified in body]. **[SYNTHESIS; it re-reads the
Section 9 scope wall at the part scale — maintenance below the wall, stake above it — and is the
cleaner statement of why the wall sits where it does.]**

On the near side of the wall, the framework's own machinery applies unchanged. A collective with
Φ_MIP > 0 at the part-partition is a single higher persister and carries its own four-axis profile —
Boundary, Drive, Memory, Integration — read on the collective's own clock (Section 5), exactly as
any persister does; a collective with Φ_MIP ≈ 0 is a pile with no collective present tense, however
much its members cooperate. This is the synchronic reading of collective individuality, and it is
already the content of Sections 4 and 9; the two-level reading above is what connects it to the
part's own viability. **[SYNTHESIS.]**

On the far side of the wall sits the *mechanism* by which a collective drives the private-stake dial
toward zero and holds it there — and this is cited context, not an AOP claim, because it is a
selection process operating across generations, which the framework does not adjudicate. Two devices
recur across the independent origins of multicellularity. Across generations, passing each new
collective through a **single-cell bottleneck** makes offspring-collectives genetically uniform, so
a defector lineage cannot ride between generations and organism-level selection can act on whole
individuals [Grosberg & Strathmann 2007, abstract verified; Michod 2007, abstract verified — "How do
groups become individuals? This is the central question," resolved through fitness tradeoffs and
reproductive altruism]. Within a generation, **policing** — the collective actively repressing
competition among its parts — suppresses defectors before the next bottleneck arrives [Frank 1995, verified in body this session], and where a life cycle evolves, the collective's
fitness can decouple from its cells' [Hammerschmidt, Rose, Kerr & Rainey 2014, verified in body this session]. The organismality this produces is well captured by the definition of
an organism as an entity of high cooperation and near-absent conflict among its parts [Queller &
Strassmann 2009, abstract verified], and the whole staircase — replicating molecules to chromosome,
free bacterium to organelle, single cells to body, solitary insects to colony — is the settled
major-transitions picture [Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995]. **[SETTLED external biology; recorded
here as the above-wall process that motivates the scope wall, and flagged as a cross-project
(Ladder) propagation-bus item, not a persistence claim of this paper.]**

There is one genuinely new structural question the collective scale forces, and it is frontier. The
Section 11a threshold for *alive* is not drive and not correction — it is correction against a
*decoupled, separately-interventable internal model of the system's own viability*. Does a
collective ever hold such a model of the *collective's* viability, one that is not simply the sum of
its members' self-models? An ant colony regulates its nest toward a set-point that no single ant
represents: each ant is model-free, following a local cue exactly as the star of Section 11a
corrects without carrying a reference, yet the colony holds the reference somewhere above any ant. By
the framework's own criterion, a collective would cross into being alive *as* a collective exactly
when it holds a decoupled model of its own viability at the collective scale — a reference one could
intervene on that lives in no single part. Whether any actual colony *clears* that line is not
settled here, and the reason is precise: certifying it requires Φ_MIP > 0 at the *collective*
partition, and Section 4 scopes Φ_MIP to static, Gaussian systems at a fixed partition, marking the
nested-level, non-stationary extension as frontier. This is the same F2 seam on which the Section 9
higher-individual route was downgraded to frontier in v1.15; the collective living-threshold rests on
it and inherits its status. **[FRONTIER; a direct extension of Section 11a to the collective scale,
bottlenecked on the nested-level Φ_MIP extension of Section 4 — the framework's principal open
problem, shared with the Ladder rebuild.]**

### 10The domain and its edge: binding, not rest mass

Every one of the four dimensions presupposes a now. Boundary is an interior held over time; Drive is a rate per time; Memory is past constraining future across time; Integration is parts cohering over time. A now requires a timelike worldline carrying proper time, and carrying proper time in a way a viability function can be checked against requires being bound — localized enough to have a persisting interior at all. Binding, not rest mass, is the domain wall: an unbound, delocalized excitation has no interior to hold and nothing for a present-tense viability to be evaluated on. Rest mass is a corollary, not the criterion — a free massive excitation, unbound and spreading, maintains no interior and hosts none of the four dimensions, so mass alone does not admit a system to the domain. What admits a system is having a bound, localized present to defend.

Binding manufactures a rest frame, and with it a clock: any bound system has vanishing total momentum in its own frame, an invariant mass, and a proper time that advances. Whether binding also manufactures a *screening* boundary depends on the interaction. Screenable (electromagnetic) binding buys both at once — an inside and a present tense together, as in the hydrogen atom. Gravitational binding buys the clock but no screening boundary: by Section 8 gravity is the anti-boundary interaction, so a gravitationally bound system (a star, an orbit) acquires a rest frame and a present tense while its only boundary is causal, not screening. What binding universally supplies is the rest frame and the present tense; the screening boundary is the conditional extra.

These statements — that binding rather than rest mass draws the domain wall, and that binding buys the clock and (conditionally) the boundary — are inferences from standard relativity plus the present-tense principle, not theorems. They are presented as this framework's synthesis and are not leaned on as foundation.

### 11Five worked cases

The abstractions earn their keep on five concrete things. Four form a progression from full persistence to a bare minimum (crystal, flame, spore, bound atom); the fifth — the star — is not a point on that line but a case in a corner none of the others occupy: high Drive, high Integration, gravitationally bound. A mode of persistence is a shape across the four dimensions in semantic weight (Figure 5); a transition is a change in that shape. Two of the four axes — Drive and Memory — are computable without a declared partition; the other two — Boundary and Integration — are partition-dependent and are the softer of the four. The profiles below show all four together for readability, but the two tiers do not have equal footing, and the figure marks which is which.

Figure 5. Three ways to persist, as semantic profiles over the same four dimensions (the crystal, flame, and spore; the bound atom and the star are treated in the text and in Figure R★ rather than as bar profiles). The crystal's semantics are spent (frozen structure, no live drive); the flame's are all in drive (sharp boundary, negligible memory); the spore's are held in escrow (structure and integration stored, drive switched off). A crystal is configuration whose semantics are spent. The living thing was the growth front — drive depositing structure; once grown, drive goes to zero and only the husk remains, memory made solid. Nothing it holds bears weight for continued persistence; it is terminal. A flame is process with its semantics all in drive: a sharp boundary rebuilt each instant from current conditions, with essentially no memory. It is the physical counter-witness that keeps Boundary and Memory from being welded by law; it is also the maximally observer-relative boundary (thermal, ionization, and luminous edges all differ). A spore is capacity with its semantics in escrow: drive switched off, structure locked, viability held in reserve and re-formable when a coupling re-forms. Its coat is a lock, not a reader; the germinant is the key; a dead lock still opens to its key. This is bet-hedging made physical — stored information with zero current viability value and high conditional-future value [10] — and it is what forces the choice of Memory numerator: the excess entropy E can read near zero on a richly structured process whose stored complexity Cμ is large [13], so two systems with identical E can differ arbitrarily in stored structure, and the spore's semantics live in the latter.

A bound atom (the hydrogen atom of Figure 4) is the minimal admitted case: a screenable electromagnetic skin gives it an interior, and its binding gives it a rest frame and a present tense. It persists — briefly, modestly, really — and it marks the low end of the domain rather than a rich interior of its own.

A star is the case at the opposite corner, and it earns its place for a reason none of the other four supply: it is where the framework's own instrument is driven soft. Its four dimensions are all near maximal and all entangled. Drive is the defining axis — fusion is a coherent central entropy source resupplying, against radiative loss, the thermal pressure that balances gravity; kill it and the star dies on a thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) time. Boundary is two boundaries of different force-types, both load-bearing (Section 8): the unscreenable causal boundary from gravitational binding, and the screenable photospheric skin whose opacity sets the leak the drive works against. Integration is very high and non-separable: hydrostatic equilibrium couples every shell to the weight above and the structure below, so no single shell's coupling can be scrambled without the rest hydrostatically readjusting. Memory is left qualitative, as with the spore, but here for a reason the framework can now name precisely: it is the star that makes the time-grain relativity of Section 5 concrete. Mass and composition largely fix the track, yet the excess-entropy numerator E has no single value for a star, because E is defined only for a stationary process and a star's stationarity is clock-dependent — stationary on its thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) clock, manifestly non-stationary on its nuclear-evolution clock, along which composition drifts and the track moves. So the star's Memory is not merely un-asserted between E and stored complexity Cμ [13]; its E is undefined until the clock is named. This is the same observer-relativity Section 5 states for the spatial grain, now on the time axis and sharp enough to remove a magnitude rather than merely rescale it. There is a tension here worth naming rather than leaving for the reader to find: the framework's cleanest, forced claim on the Memory axis — the D→M floor σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0 — holds only for a stationary process, so on the very case the paper spends the most ink on it can be evaluated only on the thermal clock, and goes silent on the nuclear one where the star is non-stationary. The flagship sits precisely in the regime where the Memory-axis law is undefined. We take this as the point rather than an embarrassment: it is the star that shows the floor is a clock-relative statement, not a defect in the floor, and a persister whose Memory is grain-dependent is exactly the object that makes the time-grain relativity concrete instead of abstract. Two things make the star more than a fifth illustration. First, one physical property — gravitational non-additivity, the settled long-range-interaction property of Section 8 [Campa, Dauxois & Ruffo 2009] — is the common root of three of the star's features at once: gravity's anti-boundary character (no negative mass ⇒ unscreenable ⇒ no statistical boundary), the Integration floor (subsystems never fully decouple), and the star's self-regulation, because a self-gravitating system has negative specific heat (radiate energy and it gets hotter), which is the microcanonical signature of that same non-additivity. The property that denies the star a boundary is the property that stabilizes its drive; this is the framework's synthesis, built on settled components, and it is the reason the star is worth working. Second, the star makes the Section 6 resolvability limit physical rather than illustrative: because its shells are locked by hydrostatic equilibrium, it sits high on the integration axis where per-edge semantic weights blur — the mask flags the coupled opacity–gradient–generation–pressure loop as load-bearing but cannot assign per-edge weights, and gravity resists intervention entirely. Here the star's coupling is not imposed but read off its structure, which is what lets it stand as more than an illustration of Section 6. Linearizing the adiabatic pulsation equation on a Lane–Emden n=3 polytrope — the settled apparatus of stellar structure — gives a tridiagonal shell-to-shell operator, a chain with a global hydrostatic constraint, whose spectrum is a graded stiff-to-sloppy band (Figure R★). It is therefore not the mean-field equicorrelation caricature (which the family of Figure TF places as the degenerate two-level member); at matched mean inter-shell correlation the derived operator has a stiff/sloppy spread roughly an order of magnitude wider, and its per-shell attribution uncertainty is *larger* than the caricature's — the parts blur harder precisely because the coupling is the real one. Read through this derived coupling the star sits high on both blur mechanisms of Section 6 at once — per-shell weights unresolvable (inferential) and gravity resisting intervention entirely (interventional) — while the aggregate, collective mode stays sharply defined. The star therefore realizes the Section 6 resolvability limit from its own physics rather than being relabeled into it; it does not escape the mask-blur regime the framework most wants to describe, it is driven into it by the same hydrostatic coupling that holds it up. It should be said clearly what this does and does not do: any strongly, non-degenerately coupled linear system has a stiff/sloppy spectrum — that is the general content of the sloppiness picture — so a real integrated system could not have come out *not* blurred, and the star is therefore a demonstration of self-consistency (the framework says integrated systems blur; here is a real one, built from its own physics, that does), not a confirmation that could have failed. What the derivation adds over the equicorrelation caricature is not informativeness about whether the effect occurs but fidelity about its shape — a graded band rather than an imposed two-level spectrum — and the removal of the suspicion that the result was smuggled in by the model choice.

The star also sharpens a distinction the flame alone left ambiguous. Both are all-in-drive, boundary-rebuilt-continuously persisters — but the flame has no restoring force (marginally stable, at the mercy of current supply; interrupt it and it is gone with no fight), while the star has a strong one: the negative-specific-heat thermostat is a present structural property, not a bet on the future (core cools ⇒ pressure drops ⇒ contracts ⇒ virial heating ⇒ steeply temperature-sensitive fusion climbs ⇒ restores). Same drive-dominated character, opposite stability class. This also answers a Section 9 worry directly: the star's apparent goal-directedness ("keep burning") is not hindsight worn as a goal but present dynamical stability, checkable now — hydrostatic and thermal balance read on the current state — exactly as the present-tense principle demands.

### 11a The living threshold: continue, correct, and correct-against-a-model  
  
The five cases fix the axes; they also expose a threshold the axes do not yet name. Three of the  
persisters actively hold themselves off equilibrium — the flame, the star, and (were it running) the  
cell — yet only the last is alive. The distinction is not *drive* (all three spend) and not  
*correction* (both the star and the cell restore after a perturbation). It is **what the correction  
is read against**, and it is a structural fact the framework can compute.  
  
Three modes, separated by their restoring structure, not by their substrate:  
  
- **Continue** — no restoring force. The flame is marginally stable: rebuilt each instant from  
 current supply, it is gone the moment supply cuts, with no fight (Section 11). Its semantics are  
 all in drive.  
- **Correct, model-free** — a restoring force whose set-point *is* a fixed point of the  
 constitutive dynamics. The star is the worked case: its negative-specific-heat thermostat pulls  
 the core back when perturbed (Section 11), but nothing in the star *stores* the target  
 hydrostatic state; the target is where the physics sits. There is no separable reference to  
 intervene on. This is the settled distinction between dynamic stability and model-based regulation. What a system of this kind lacks is not stability but a *stored, separately-manipulable reference*: the internal-model requirement of regulation [Francis & Wonham 1976] and the organizational account of biological regulation [Bich, Mossio, Ruiz-Mirazo & Moreno 2015] both locate genuine regulation in a control variable that is dynamically decoupled from the process it steers — precisely what a bare fixed point of the constitutive dynamics is not. (The good-regulator theorem [Conant & Ashby 1970] is sometimes read as forcing every regulator to carry a model; it establishes only that the optimal-and-maximally-simple regulator is a *homomorphic image* of the regulated system — a notion under which even a bare fixed point qualifies, so it does not supply the decoupled-reference distinction needed here.) The star has a set-point but no separable reference to intervene on; that is a Francis–Wonham / Bich point, not a good-regulator-theorem one.  
- **Correct, model-based** — a restoring force driven against a **decoupled internal reference**  
 that stores the system's own viable set (its *essential variables*, in Ashby's sense [Ashby  
 1960]) and is dynamically separable from the regulated variable it controls [Bich, Mossio,  
 Ruiz-Mirazo & Moreno 2015; the internal-model requirement of Francis & Wonham 1976]. The cell is  
 the case: setpoints are held in a regulatory sub-network, read out onto the fast physiology, and  
 can be corrupted independently of the physiology itself.  
  
We take **alive** to name the third mode: *a persister whose maintenance corrects the regulated  
axes against a decoupled, separately-interventable internal model of its own viability.* This is a  
threshold on the existing four dimensions and the mask, not a fifth axis — the model is a  
load-bearing internal coupling from a slow reference onto the fast regulated dynamics, exactly the  
object the semantic mask (Section 3) is built to detect. It is substrate-independent by  
construction, and it does not relabel neighbouring accounts: autopoiesis names self-production of  
components [Varela, Maturana & Uribe 1974] and is silent on a stored viability model; the semantic  
mask's parent result concerns information a system holds about its *environment* [2], whereas the  
model here is about the system's *own* viable set. The conjunction — a decoupled model of one's own  
essential variables, maintained and read out — is this framework's synthesis, with the cybernetic  
and organizational-autonomy results [Ashby 1960; Francis & Wonham 1976; Bich  
et al. 2015; Moreno & Mossio 2015 — with Conant & Ashby 1970 as background, not the load-bearing result (see the body paragraph above)] as its settled antecedents. As stated in the note on "life" at  
the head of the paper, we advance this as a **new, frontier definition** measured against the  
settled NASA reference, not as established science. **[The definition of *alive* is FRONTIER — a new  
proposal; its control-theory components are SETTLED; its internal consistency is SYNTHESIS, computed  
(Figure LT).]**  
  
**Computed, not only described (Figure LT).** The discriminator is made concrete on two minimal

corrects-but-not-alive (Section 11) — outside it. Like Figure MW, this is a demonstration of

**Spore, placed — and the two tiers it forces apart.** The predicate has to be split into two
present-tense tiers, and the spore is the case that forces the split — exactly as it is the case that

- **Alive** = the model edge is *load-bearing now* — active correction against the decoupled reference,

  the same move the canon already makes for the spore's stored complexity Cμ being large at E ≈ 0 and
  drive ≈ 0 (Section 11). Spore: yes (architecture present, quiescent). Crystal, naked virion: no (no

is *life paused*: alive-tier no (edge not load-bearing now), viable/pausable-tier yes (architecture
present). The virion is its false twin: also engine-off, but even its architecture is a propagation

### 13Limitations and outlook

The framework’s honest exposure is concentrated in three places. First, the two soft dimensions—Boundary and Integration—are partition-relative, and Integration in particular has at least six inequivalent published measures that agree on rank but disagree on values, trends, and even sign [12]; any claim about the non-energy triangle inherits that ambiguity. Second, the semantic mask, though well defined as a construction, has its weights determined only relative to a declared viability functional; on any concrete substrate the couplings must be separately manipulable for per-edge weights to be defined, and on strongly integrated systems they may not be—which is not a defect to be removed but a fact about integrated systems to be characterized. That characterization is now partly in hand: the failure decomposes into an inferential (estimation) and an interventional (isolation) part, and their ratio is fixed by the coupling topology (Figure TF), so the limit is a specified family rather than a single unquantified caveat — with the star (Figure R★) its worked, structure-derived instance. What remains open is the non-energy triangle below, not the resolvability limit's form. Third, the observer-as-viability containment and Principle T are this framework’s inferences, secure only to the extent that the present-tense principle is airtight.

A fourth exposure, newly bounded rather than newly opened: the coupling classification treats Boundary, Drive, and Memory as distinct spokes, and a recent external reframing proposed instead that they are three interchangeable "currencies" of one persistence budget. Put through the gate ledger's discipline (Table 4), that stronger claim fails: on a minimal Markov model two of the three mechanisms showed a hard persistence ceiling while only the equilibrium wall did not. That ceiling ordering was then itself pre-registered and tested on a real driven chemical system (the Schlögl bistable birth–death network) — and did **not** survive: on real mass-action kinetics both the wall and the stored-copy reset drive persistence arbitrarily high, and a pure dissipative current has *zero* leverage on persistence at all. The ceilings were artifacts of the toy's small fixed state space, and the "strict ordering" reading is retracted (deposited verdicts `aop_realsystem`, `aop_workedcase`). What survives is cleaner and stronger for being model-independent: in a one-dimensional birth–death system a persister's stationary *occupancy* of its viable set is a functional of the stationary distribution alone, so only mechanisms that reshape that distribution (an equilibrium wall, or a reset into a metastable well) can raise occupancy, while a pure current cannot — but the paper's chosen primitive is *lifetime* (§1), and lifetime is current-sensitive even at fixed stationary distribution (a divergence-free current cuts mean escape time ~5.7× on a driven ring, deposited gate), so under the primitive the framework actually uses, Drive does have direct leverage — which is exactly the forced-edge structure the coupling graph already asserts (Drive acts through Memory and Boundary, not on persistence directly). The one genuinely new, surviving object is the framework's first computed iso-persistence tradeoff surface with finite exchange rates; its generalization beyond the minimal model is open.

The productive step here was not to add a dimension but to make the semantic mask compute on a minimal, deliberately resolvable model with an explicit, present-tense viability functional, and to characterize not a single weight but the range a weight sweeps as the declared viability is varied. That has now been done: the mask is computed on the Figure DM ring (Figure MW, Section 3), returning graded weights on the load-bearing edges, exactly zero on a present-but-inert spectator, and — because semantic weight is a quantity that Section 5 says moves with the grain — an interval rather than a point for each load-bearing weight. That range is the framework's characteristic measurable; it is a shape the synthesis commits to, not a forecast that could adjudicate the framework against a rival. A clean single number would hide exactly what the account says is there. What remains genuinely open is to compute the same mask on a system whose part-partition is well posed, so that the resolvability limit of Section 6 can be shown acting on the mask's own weights (parts blurring while an aggregate weight stays sharp) rather than on a separate Gaussian model. This does not leave the framework wholly unfalsifiable, and it is worth saying plainly what would embarrass it. The resolvability claim commits to a specific coupling between two things that can be measured independently: how strongly coupled a system is and how wide its semantic weights sweep as the partition is varied. The test has to be stated carefully, because this same paper insists (Section 6, Figure TF, and the star) that width is set by coupling *topology*, not by a scalar amount of integration — a weakly integrated chain can out-blur a strongly integrated mean-field system, and that is the topology family behaving as predicted, not a failure. So the embarrassment condition is not "width tracks a scalar integration score across all systems"; that would be confounded by the very refinement the paper is proud of. It is the narrower, sharper claim: *within a fixed coupling topology*, increasing the global coupling must monotonically widen the weight sweep, and *at matched mean coupling* the topology with the more graded spectrum (the chain) must show the wider inferential sweep than the degenerate one (mean-field). Both are computed here (Figure TF; the star vs. its equicorrelation caricature) and either could have come out otherwise: a topology whose sweep did not widen with coupling, or a graded spectrum that did not out-blur the degenerate one at matched coupling, would falsify the resolvability limit. That is a weak test, internal to the synthesis rather than adjudicating it against a named rival, but it is a test the topology result sharpens rather than undercuts, and the account is not idle against it. The energy hub is coherent and defensible; the non-energy triangle is where the work remains; and the account now marks precisely where its own domain ends — a bound system persists, briefly and really, behind a screening skin and inside the causal boundary of its own worldline, while an unbound, delocalized excitation falls outside the domain entirely. The individuation axis added in v1.12 (Φ_MIP, Section 4) sits at the same frontier in two directions that the next work targets: it resolves one-vs-many only for a fixed partition, so selecting which level of a nested system (cell, organism, society) is the individual is open and is the natural next Gaussian gate; and its non-stationary and critical payoffs — the properties that would let it speak to developing systems and to the integration-blur limit — require a model class with a phase transition, which the static Gaussian setting does not provide.

The computed results in this paper — the entropy-production/excess-entropy sweep of the driven three-state Markov ring (Figure DM), the invariant-mass check underlying Section 10, the non-energy-triangle reachability scan (Figure T, Section 4), the equicorrelation resolvability sweep (Figure R, Section 6), the topology-indexed resolvability family (Figure TF, Section 6), the stellar-structure-derived resolvability signature of the star (Figure R★, Section 11), and the worked semantic mask on the Figure DM ring (Figure MW, Section 3) — were produced by short, self-contained scripts. The code and the generated data are deposited at [repository DOI — to be assigned at submission] under an open licence, sufficient to regenerate every one of them: Figures DM, T, R, TF, R★, and MW and the Section 10 numerical statements. The Figure MW mask applies the Kolchinsky–Wolpert scramble-and-re-run procedure [2] to the ring's internal couplings under three present-tense viability functionals (steady-state current, entropy production, relaxation rate), reading each edge's semantic weight as the fractional viability drop when that edge is scrambled to detailed balance; a detailed-balance negative control returns zero weight on every edge, and an inert spectator coupling returns exactly zero under every functional. The resolvability engine takes any coupling Laplacian to its signature (eigenspectrum, per-component variance-inflation factor, interventional do-edge drag, and aggregate collective-mode variance); it is validated against the equicorrelation closed forms (per-component uncertainty (1−ρ)^(−1/2), aggregate 1/√λ_max) to machine precision, and the Figure R★ operator is built from a Lane–Emden n=3 integration reproducing the tabulated first zero ξ₁ = 6.897 to five digits. The individuation gate (Section 4, the Φ_MIP axis) computes Gaussian integrated information across the exhaustively-searched minimum information partition of Σ=(I+gL)⁻¹ on module-weld and matched-coupling-budget systems, returning exactly zero on block-decomposable systems and graded positive values as parts weld; the five pre-registered criteria and deposited code regenerate it.
