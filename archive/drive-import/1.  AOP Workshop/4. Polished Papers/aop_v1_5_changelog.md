# AOP v1.4 → v1.5 changelog

Version 1.5 answers a referee-style critique whose core finding was that the paper read as
"an excellent synthesis wearing the costume of a predictive theory." v1.5 takes the costume off.
No claim that survives is weakened; the deflationary edits make the paper harder to reject, not
softer. Five changes.

## C1 — Prediction posture removed (abstract, introduction, limitations).
The paper no longer claims "a concrete first prediction." Because semantic weight is
observer-relative, the range it sweeps is analytically guaranteed to be non-trivial — so it is a
*shape the synthesis commits to*, not a forecast that could adjudicate AOP against a rival. The
abstract, intro, and limitations now say exactly that. Genre is owned: this is a Perspective.

## C2 — Section 10 collapsed to its sober core.
The binding criterion (persistence presupposes a bound, timelike worldline; binding, not rest mass,
draws the domain wall; binding buys the clock, and — for screenable interactions — the boundary too,
inheriting the §8 gravity distinction) is kept in three tight paragraphs. Removed: the free-photon
set-piece, the "rest mass as the price" framing dressed as a near-theorem, and the de Sitter /
Gibbons–Hawking / Chandrasekaran–Witten horizon-holography reach. A referee following the careful
thermodynamics would read that reach as a lurch toward a theory of everything; it is deferred to the
framework's downstream applications (see the Ladder parking-lot note).

## C3 — de Sitter horizon material removed from Section 8 and the status table.
Figure 4 (one persister, two boundaries) is retained but the causal boundary is now simply the light
cone of the system's worldline, not a de Sitter patch whose entropy the atom must witness. The
"observer-defined horizon entropy (frontier)" row is removed from the status table.

## C4 — Resolvability limit reframed (Section 6).
The (1−ρ)^(−1/2) per-component divergence is now owned as *inherited* multicollinearity / Fisher
sloppiness / PID attribution ambiguity — a limitation (Section 13), not convergent evidence that AOP
discovered something. What is specific to persistence is preserved and foregrounded: the
*dissociation* — the whole sharpens at the same rate the parts become individually unresolvable —
which is the mathematical shadow of the "no ownership" refusal. Stated as an organizing observation,
not a falsifiable result.

## C5 — Soft axes marked in the figures (Section 11 / Figure 5).
Drive and Memory are computable without a declared cut; Boundary and Integration are
partition-dependent. The four-bar profile figure and the surrounding text now state the two tiers
explicitly and mark which axis is which, so the figure stops presenting four bars of unequal footing
as commensurable.

## References
Two removed with the horizon material: Gibbons–Hawking 1977 and Chandrasekaran et al. 2023. In-text
citations renumbered (Mediano 14→12, Crutchfield 15→13). Thirteen of the fifteen original references
retained, all verified. Five gap-additions (Transtrum ×2, Williams & Beer, Watanabe, Faes) and five
v1.4 additions (Rosas, Comolatti–Hoel, Bouchard, Lenton, Boyle) unchanged. The econometrics VIF
citation remains the one open item, load-bearing on nothing.

## Worked cases
Five → four (crystal, flame, spore, bound atom). The photon moves to the Ladder. The bound atom is
reframed as the minimal admitted case rather than a de Sitter witness.
