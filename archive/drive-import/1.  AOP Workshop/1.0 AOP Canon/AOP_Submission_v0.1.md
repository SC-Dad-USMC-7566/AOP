### The Architecture of Persistence

A classified dependency structure among Boundary, Drive, Memory, and Integration, with a semantic mask read out through a declared viability functional on the system’s viable set (the V slot of the declaration tuple; "own" denotes the set the functional is evaluated on, not a viability the system possesses or supplies — the framework remains ownership-free, §9a)

Abstract

Persistence is a thing resisting erasure along several distinct directions at once. We fix the primitive at the outset: persistence means *lifetime* — led by the mean first-passage time out of the set of states from which a system can still recover its identity — not stationary *occupancy* of that set, a distinction that is load-bearing because a pure energy current changes lifetime severalfold while leaving occupancy exactly fixed. Mean first-passage time is the lead member of a persistence-functional family, not the essence of persistence: where the mean is infinite or undefined, where the first-passage law is heavy-tailed, or where the system ages, develops, or modifies its own viable set, the declared functional should instead be a survival curve, a hazard function, a finite-horizon survival probability, or a recovery probability (§1). This paper names four structural directions along which lifetime can be altered or stabilized—Boundary (space), Drive (energy), Memory (time), and Integration (parts)—each expressed as a relative-entropy difference along its own axis (three static cuts and one, Drive, a trajectory asymmetry), and asks how they depend on one another. Their signs are intervention- and regime-dependent: most sharply, a measure-preserving current at fixed stationary distribution can only shorten or leave unchanged the small-noise mean first-passage time. The four are non-commensurable operations on that single target, not exchangeable currencies of one budget (no shared price survives testing), and exhibiting them is not a proof that they are complete or minimal. The separation is graded, not blanket: Memory is the most distinct axis — its raw correlation with the other two is shared coupling strength only, vanishing to a mild tradeoff once coupling is controlled, and it carries 0.59 of its variance uniquely — while Boundary and Integration dissociate throughout the declared Gaussian ensemble — both dissociation corners (sealed-yet-integrated and leaky-yet-unintegrated) are populated throughout it and Boundary’s own content still spreads when Integration is held high (deposited `phaseE1`) — even though the cross-cut slice B5 = I(inside;outside) remains an algebraic component of Integration, so the honest claim is four distinguishable axes, not four independent ones. The result is a two-layer classification. The first layer is a coupling graph whose edges are sorted by modal status: some couplings are compelled by physical law, some are conditional, some are free. The second layer is a semantic mask that separates couplings whose declared viability is intervention-sensitive from those merely present. Its default object is a *coalition*, not an edge: minimal failure cut sets, minimal viability-preserving sets, and equivalence classes of viable mechanisms are what the mask reports, with per-edge weights retained only in the narrow band where additivity and identifiability tests pass. It is computed here in two deliberately scoped demonstrations: Figure MW is a proxy-ablation diagnostic using current, entropy production, and relaxation rate, whereas §11b is the viability-grounded finite-horizon competence check, recovering a designed inert edge, load-bearing edge, redundancy, and synergy. Semantic claims are explicitly relative to a **declared persistence criterion** — the analyst names the viable set and the functional evaluated on it — and the framework applies wherever a subsystem, state representation, persistence functional, horizon, and admissible intervention class can all be declared. In the present ledger Energy is the principal organizing hub [SYNTHESIS], but its spokes are uneven: two are forced and scoped to a stated regime—the thermodynamic uncertainty relation prices reliability, and dissipation forces a floor on predictive memory (excess entropy, a corollary of trajectory irreversibility) but reaches no further—while the rest are conditional or free. Pre-registered gates confirm the reach: the Drive floor forces no stored time-asymmetry (the forward/reverse complexity difference Ξ = Cμ⁺−Cμ⁻ is exactly zero at every drive) and no floor on stored complexity, which covaries with the drive parameter only through the generator's time-symmetric sector, not as a forced consequence of the cost (Section 12). The framework also gains a graded, ownership-free irreducibility diagnostic — **minimum-cut dependence**, the mutual information across the least-disrupting bipartition — which is exactly zero on block-decomposable systems and rises as parts become jointly irreducible, and which reports something the Integration and resolvability axes do not, scoped to static Gaussian models (Section 4). It is one coordinate in an individuation panel, not a verdict: positivity does not by itself establish that a collection is a single individual (Section 4). A structural consequence sits alongside the hub: mask-resolvability and Integration trade off, so the framework reads most cleanly on weakly integrated systems and blurs on the strongly integrated ones it was built to describe. That blur is two distinct failures — parts unestimable and edges un-isolable — whose ratio is set by the system's coupling topology, making the limit a family rather than a single curve. We locate the observer precisely enough to contain the homunculus problem — by naming the analyst rather than hiding one inside the system — bound the theory’s domain by an operational scope condition rather than a physical one, and illustrate the abstractions on five motivating cases: four spanning full persistence to a bare minimum (crystal, flame, spore, a bound atom), and a star, which occupies a high-drive, high-integration, gravitationally-bound corner where the framework's own resolvability limit is driven from an assertion into a fact computed from stellar structure, and where the time-grain relativity of Memory becomes concrete (a star's excess entropy is defined on its thermal clock but not its nuclear one). Because semantic weight is an observer-relative quantity, the framework's characteristic measurable is not a single weight but the range a weight sweeps as the declared partition, resolution, and viability are varied — the shape a synthesis of this kind commits to, rather than a forecast that could adjudicate it against a rival theory. The organizing notion — that what survives can be selected by differential persistence rather than differential reproduction — is itself established across scales: it has been argued at the level of lineages and processes, and demonstrated for self-perpetuating feedback systems from autocatalytic networks to ecosystems, belief systems, and economies. This is a synthesis of established results, not new physics; claims are tagged throughout as settled, as this framework’s synthesis, or as frontier.

Keywords: persistence · stochastic thermodynamics · semantic information · biological individuality · entropy production · excess entropy · Markov blanket

### 1Introduction and scope

The question of what makes a thing a persisting thing—an individual, a system that holds together—recurs across nonequilibrium physics, computational mechanics, information theory, and theoretical biology, and each field answers it with a different quantity. This paper does not add a quantity. It proposes an organizing structure over the quantities the field already has, and asks a question those literatures rarely pose to one another: how do the distinct dimensions of persistence depend on each other, and which of those dependencies are compelled by physical law?

The claim, in one paragraph, is this. Persistence is resistance to erasure along four directions: a maintained separation of interior from exterior (Boundary), sustained dissipation — trajectory-level irreversibility — that holds the system off equilibrium (Drive, measured as entropy-production rate, not free-energy throughput), information the present carries that constrains its own future (Memory), and the degree to which many parts act as one irreducible whole (Integration). We fix the primitive at the outset, because two readings of "resistance to erasure" give opposite answers and the difference is load-bearing. The quantity we mean is *lifetime* — how long the system remains instantiated in the set of states from which it can still recover its identity (its viable set) before a specified perturbation spectrum knocks it out, formally the mean first-passage time to erasure — not *occupancy*, the stationary probability mass sitting in that viable set. The two dissociate sharply: a pure divergence-free current leaves the stationary distribution, and hence occupancy, exactly unchanged while changing lifetime severalfold (deposited gate; §12), so a persistence built on occupancy would declare Drive inert where a flame plainly is not. Every primitive phrase this paper uses — resists erasure, how long until it decays, the spore *lasts* — is lifetime language, and lifetime is what the four axes are operations upon. **Mean first-passage time leads a family; it is not the essence of persistence.** The lifetime-versus-occupancy distinction above is load-bearing and is not weakened here: it is supported by a deposited gate (§12) in which a divergence-free current moves the mean first-passage time ~5.7× while leaving occupancy invariant to 1e-14. What is scoped is the choice of *functional*, not the choice of primitive. Mean first-passage time is the lead member of a **persistence-functional family** whose other members — the survival curve S(t), the hazard function h(t), finite-horizon survival probability P(τ), and recovery probability after a declared perturbation — are the appropriate readings wherever the mean is not. It is inappropriate as the lead in four named regimes: (i) when the mean first-passage time is infinite or undefined; (ii) when the first-passage law is heavy-tailed, so the mean is dominated by rare trajectories and misrepresents the typical system; (iii) when the system is aging, developing, or otherwise path-dependent, so no single time-homogeneous escape rate exists; and (iv) when the system modifies its own viable set, so the target the first passage is measured against is itself moving. In those regimes the declared functional is named in **D** (§12″) and the family member is stated with it; §11b, for instance, declares finite-horizon survival rather than a mean. Two guardrails travel with that framing and are not optional. First, the four are *operations on* lifetime, not *currencies of* it: they are non-commensurable and cannot be traded off at a common price — a pre-registered test found no shared cost, with dissipation varying 157% across an iso-persistence surface (§12), so "four ways to act on the same quantity" must never be read as "four exchangeable amounts of one budget." Nor is every intervention pro-persistence: the sign must be stated for the declared regime. Second, exhibiting the four as operations on lifetime is not a proof that they are *the* operations: completeness (that no fifth axis is needed) and minimality (that none is redundant) remain open, and the primitive does not close them. These four are not independent decorations; they are coupled, and the couplings admit a classification—forced, conditional, or free. Over that classification sits a second layer that distinguishes couplings carrying causal weight for continued existence from couplings that are merely present. The deliverable is that two-layer object: a coupling graph with a semantic mask over it, read out relative to the system’s own viability, and defined only for systems that have a present tense to persist in.

Three commitments bound the scope from the outset, and are kept deliberately because the source literatures repeatedly press against them.

Three load-bearing refusals

No ownership. Which aggregate is the “true” individual, or which self does the inferring, is never the question. The framework classifies couplings and admits a graded, third-person measure of how far a coupled system fails to factor across its least-disrupting cut (**minimum-cut dependence**, Section 4) — a diagnostic, not a verdict that the system is one individual; what it refuses is ownership — individuating by a self-maintenance scalar, the fraction of its own upkeep a system performs on its own behalf. That narrower refusal is not merely cautious: independent attempts to measure ownership directly — to quantify the fraction of its own maintenance a system performs on its own behalf — have failed at exactly this point. A candidate observational measure of self-performed maintenance was found to collapse into an existing quantity (non-trivial informational closure), unable to separate “the system maintains itself while drawing on inputs” from “the environment supplies the structure,” with three independent lines of evidence converging on the conclusion that observational measures cannot make this separation for open systems. The proposed remedy in that literature is to abandon observation for intervention. We take both lessons: ownership is not observationally recoverable, so we decline to individuate by an ownership scalar; and where a directional judgment about coupling is needed, we intervene (the semantic mask, Section 3) rather than correlate. No metaphysics of selfhood or worth. It does not decide what a self is or what a persisting thing is worth; it describes structure. No master quantity. The four dimensions share one mathematical form—a relative entropy—but each is pointed at a different partition: inside versus outside, past versus future, part versus part, forward versus reverse trajectory. The common form makes them expressible in one vocabulary; it does **not** make their raw magnitudes commensurable. Comparing rates with amounts, or panels with different horizons and normalizations, requires the declarations in §12″ and does not produce a shared price. The different targets are what keep the operations distinct, though not generally independent. It is a mistake to read the shared form as the cause of the dissociation—the form would, if anything, invite collapse; the distinct partitions are what motivate four axes rather than one. The unification is also thinner than it first appears, since nearly every quantity in information theory is a relative entropy: the shared operation is a common language, not a hidden master quantity. It licenses pluralism; it does not license reduction.

Nothing below claims a new law of physics. Every forced edge is tied to a named, published result; every inference this framework makes on its own is flagged as such (Section 12). The contribution, if there is one, is the assembly and its classification.

This document is maintained as a *living review*: a single, versioned statement of the framework that its authors update as the science moves, rather than a one-time publication. The version number and compilation date on the masthead are load-bearing, and every change is recorded in a running change log. A reader should therefore take the claims below as current to the stated version, and the grade attached to each (settled, this framework's synthesis, or frontier) as the honest status of that claim at that version — not as a finished verdict.

Why four axes, and not the field's one. A synthesis of this kind has to answer for its carving: the incumbent accounts organize persistence along a single axis. The leading information-theoretic account of individuality identifies the individual as the partition that maximizes a memory-like quantity (autonomy) [6], and free-energy / Markov-blanket accounts read persistence off a single inference boundary [11]. A one-axis carving is not wrong; it is lossy, and the loss is visible in concrete persisters that the single axis is forced to mis-score. A flame is a real persister — a sharp, actively maintained boundary — carrying essentially no memory; an axis that defines the boundary by maximizing memory cannot represent it without either denying it a boundary or importing a memory it does not have (Section 11). A spore is a real persister with its drive switched off and deep structure locked in escrow; any account that scores persistence on a single memory-as-E axis reads it as nearly inert, and any drive-centric account reads it as dead. Neither is true. The spore motivates the choice of Memory numerator as a *thought experiment* — a persister whose stored organization plainly outruns what its past–future channel reports — and it is used here in that role only. It is not offered as a computed witness of high Cμ at low E: statistical complexity is a property of a declared observation process, not of a material object, and no observation model for a spore has been declared or measured here (§4, §5). The four-fold carving earns its keep in exactly this negative sense: each axis is separated from the others because collapsing that pair mis-describes some system that persists. This is the framework's synthesis-level argument, not a theorem — the evidence for it is the worked diagnostic cases (Section 11), each of which is a persister that forces two axes apart. It also fixes the count from below rather than above: at least these four, because each is needed to keep one diagnostic case honest, and the count is held open for the same reason (Section 2).


A structural category is not a metaphysics of selfhood, and the refusals are not relaxed anywhere below. The framework locates one structural feature that separates classes of persisters — active self-maintenance corrected against a regulatory subsystem that is dynamically decoupled from the process it regulates, and separately interventable. That architecture is a coupling fact, read out by the same third-person mask and generator spectrum the rest of the paper uses, and it individuates nothing by an ownership scalar. Its development as a criterion for *life* — the tiers it would need, the diachronic tracking relation it presupposes, and the control-theoretic support it can and cannot claim — is treated in a separate follow-on paper and is deliberately not carried here. What the core retains of the diachronic question is one sentence: **comparing a system at two times requires a declared tracking relation**, and no such comparison is well posed until that relation is named (it enters as part of the declaration **D**, §12″).

**The ontology, fixed once.** Because the four axes are used in several registers below — as targets, as measured quantities, as figure panels, as objects of intervention — the ontology is stated here once and held to throughout. It is the front-of-paper form of the declaration apparatus of §12″.

- **Nodes are conceptual targets.** Boundary, Drive, Memory, and Integration are the four things the framework points at. A node is not a scalar, not a panel, and not a mechanism.
- **Proxies are attached measurement families.** Each node carries a *panel* of proxies (B1, B2, B4, B5; σ and its housekeeping term; E and local active information storage; total correlation and its companions). A proxy is a way of measuring toward a node; it is never the node's identity, and no single proxy is the node.
- **Edges are propositions.** An edge in the coupling graph is a claim relating specified proxy *aspects* of two nodes, under a declaration **D** and a model class **M**. It is not a pipe, a flow, or a causal arrow unless it says so and carries the warrant.
- **Reliability is a downstream outcome, not a fifth node.** It appears as the target of a forced spoke (the thermodynamic uncertainty relation) because it is a consequence, not because it is an axis.
- **Lifetime is the declared outcome primitive, not another axis.** The four nodes are operations *on* lifetime; lifetime is what they operate on.

Two consequences are worth stating so they are not read as slips. First, **cross-loading is expected and is not node confusion**: the maintenance burden B4 is the Drive panel's housekeeping term read at the interface, and B5 = I(inside;outside) is an algebraic component of Integration read at the same interface. Both are stated as cross-loadings, and neither redefines the node it is read on. Second, where a later passage displays an axis as a bar height or a scalar, that is a *panel reading under a stated declaration*, never a claim that the node is that number.

### 2The object: four dimensions as differences

A persisting thing holds a difference-that-could-be-otherwise along four directions. Crucially, none of the four is a substance one can point at. Each is a computed difference across a cut—a mutual information—that is never observed directly. One observes residues, bent flows, and locked shells, and infers the difference behind them. The event horizon makes this unavoidable: a horizon casts only a shadow—the dark disk in a horizon image is the critical photon ring, not the horizon itself—and the horizon is inferred from what it does to surrounding light. Every dimension is like this. One gets the shadow, never the thing.

Drive is the odd one out. It is not a mutual information across a static cut but the time-asymmetry of the dynamics: entropy production equals the relative entropy (Kullback–Leibler divergence) between the forward and time-reversed trajectory distributions [1]. So all four are relative-entropy quantities pointed along independent axes—forward-versus-reverse trajectory (Drive, directional), past-versus-future state (Memory, time-symmetric), inside-versus-outside (Boundary), and part-versus-part (Integration). The count is held open—at least these four, not a claim that exactly four exhaust the phenomenon. The distinction among them is not hard-versus-soft. **All four axes are declaration-relative.** None is computable with no choices to make. Drive requires a declared set of state variables, a declared time grain, and — decisively — a declared time-reversal convention, since the entropy production of a trajectory ensemble is defined only once one has said what the time-reverse of a state *is* (whether each variable is even or odd under reversal); change that convention and σ changes, in some cases to zero. Memory requires a declared observable, a declared time grain, and a stationarity claim at that grain — and §5 shows that E does not merely change value across grains but can cease to be defined. What separates the axes is a *second, stronger* requirement layered on top: **Boundary and Integration additionally require an explicit spatial or component partition** — which states are “inside,” which parts are “the parts” — and the answer moves with that cut as well as with the representation. The honest contrast is therefore *representation-dependence (all four)* versus *partition-dependence (Boundary and Integration additionally)*. Both are permanent scope limits, stated at the outset rather than patched, and the reversal convention that Drive and Memory rest on is carried as slot **R** of the declaration tuple **D** (§12″), where it does real work in the D→M theorem's fourth scope condition (§4).

Integration carries a second, subtler ambiguity beyond the partition: there is no single canonical measure of it. Six distinct candidate measures of integrated information have been reviewed and compared, and on Gaussian network models no two show consistent agreement across all analyses; some can even go negative [12]. This is an instance of a broader regularity: across more than a dozen independently developed measures of causation, the measures turn out to be near-rediscoveries of a small set of shared primitives, agreeing on structural conclusions (such as causal emergence) while differing in the values they assign [Comolatti & Hoel 2025] — direction survives the choice of measure; magnitude does not. We confirmed the qualitative pattern directly — computing two of the closed-form measures on thousands of coupled Gaussian systems, they agree in rank (Spearman ≈ 0.87) but disagree in value, and whole-minus-sum integrated information is negative for a small fraction of systems. AOP does not attempt to resolve this; it inherits the ambiguity but not its stakes, because it uses Integration as one descriptive axis among four, not as a measure of consciousness. For all worked computations we therefore declare an operational default: total correlation, TC = Σ H(Xᵢ) − H(X) [Watanabe 1960], the member of the family that is non-negative by construction, monotone in coupling, and closed-form in the Gaussian case. Where the synergy/redundancy character of the interdependence matters — not just its magnitude — the O-information [Rosas, Mediano, Gastpar & Jensen 2019] is the natural signed companion, negative for synergy-dominated and positive for redundancy-dominated structure; we note it as the measure to reach for when that distinction is load-bearing. This is a choice of operational proxy, not a claim that it is the uniquely correct measure. Accordingly, every Integration-dependent claim in this paper is stated in its directional form — all reasonable measures rise with coupling — and no exact Integration value is load-bearing. Where "simultaneous segregation and integration" is specifically what one wants to capture, causal density is the measure to prefer [12].

### 3Two layers: syntactic correlation and semantic mask

The four dimensions as usually measured are syntactic—plain Shannon correlations, which by themselves carry no meaning. Persistence turns on the semantic subset: the correlations causally necessary for the system to maintain its own existence. The distinction, and the method for drawing it, are due to Kolchinsky and Wolpert [2], who define semantic information as the syntactic information a system holds about its environment that is causally necessary for it to keep itself in a low-entropy state, and operationalize “causally necessary” by counterfactual intervention—scramble a correlation, re-run the dynamics, and measure whether viability drops. What survives scrambling was decorative; what reduces viability when scrambled was load-bearing. What this paper does with that method is **not** what Kolchinsky and Wolpert did with it, and the difference must be stated before anything is computed. Their intervention acts on the *system–environment channel*: it perturbs the correlation carrying information from the environment into the system, and their results are established for that operation. AOP instead intervenes on couplings *internal to the system*. That is an **extension**, and nothing in the parent result licenses it — the parent method is cited here as inspiration and as the special case recovered when the scrambled coupling happens to be the system–environment channel, never as a warrant for internal-edge deletion. Because the extension is ours, it carries its own name and its own specification, given next. Separately, the test presupposes that the scrambled coupling can be intervened on in isolation—an assumption that fails on strongly integrated systems (Section 6), which is why the default reported object is a coalition rather than an edge.

This is the central organizing move: a syntactic coupling graph, with a semantic mask over it. Classification then means locating each coupling on both axes at once—its modal status (forced, conditional, or free) and its semantic weight (does scrambling it drop viability?). An edge can be present but semantically inert, or thin but load-bearing.

**The internal-edge intervention protocol.** AOP's intervention is named here so that it can be argued with, and specified so that it can be reproduced. It is an extension of the Kolchinsky–Wolpert construction to couplings interior to a system, and it is not validated by that construction. Every intervention reported anywhere in this paper must declare six things, and an intervention that cannot declare them is not admissible:

1. **The structural mechanism changed** — which coupling, in which representation, is being altered, stated on the declared object (for coupling/precision quantities, the partition K of §6).
2. **A physical operation that could implement it** — what one would actually do to a system to effect that change. An intervention with no physically statable implementation is a formal manipulation, not a do-operation, and its output is not a semantic weight.
3. **The variables and laws held fixed** — what is *not* allowed to move while the mechanism changes, since the answer depends entirely on this and is otherwise unstated.
4. **Whether resource flow is fixed or free** — whether the intervention is performed at fixed energy/matter throughput or allows it to re-equilibrate. These give different weights for the same edge.
5. **Which structural properties are preserved** — specifically whether detailed balance, conservation laws, and network topology survive the alteration, each stated separately.
6. **Whether the altered model remains in the admissible model class M** — an intervention that leaves the declared class has not measured the system's semantics; it has measured a different system.

Scrambling to detailed balance, the operation used in Figure MW, is one admissible member of this protocol and is declared as such where it is used. **[SYNTHESIS; the protocol is this framework's specification of its own extension, not a result imported from [2].]**

**Coalitions are the default object; per-edge weights are the exception.** The per-edge reading is the special case, admissible only where it is licensed, and it is not the layer the framework leads with. The default semantic report is coalition-level: **minimal failure cut sets** (which sets of mechanisms, removed together, end viability), **minimal viability-preserving sets** (which sets suffice on their own), **equivalence classes of viable mechanisms** (which substitutions leave the declared functional unchanged), and, where no unique edge decomposition exists, a **semantic hypergraph** whose hyperedges are those sets. Allocation summaries that distribute weight across individual mechanisms — Shapley values and their relatives — are reported only where additivity is separately justified, and are labelled as summaries of a coalition structure rather than as the structure itself. Per-edge weights are retained only where additivity and identifiability tests pass; §13 states the measured band in which they do, and it is narrow. The reason for the demotion is stated plainly rather than buried: the per-edge mask does not reach the strongly integrated regime it was built for, and the coalition object is the framework's response to that failure, not a refinement of a working instrument. **[SYNTHESIS.]**

One point must be stated plainly, because it governs how the second layer may legitimately be read. Semantic weight is a quantity, not a law. Like every dimension magnitude in Table 1, it is observer-relative: its value depends on the declared partition, the resolution, and the viability functional chosen, exactly as Integration’s value does (Section 6). The mask is well defined as a construction; its numbers are not invariants. Treating a semantic weight as a fixed, observer-independent coordinate would import a determinacy the framework does not possess and does not claim.

The mask is computed here in two deliberately different demonstrations, and they must not be conflated. **Figure MW is an internal-edge proxy-ablation diagnostic**, not a finite-horizon persistence test. On the driven three-state ring, each internal coupling is scrambled to detailed balance in isolation and the changes in steady-state current, entropy production, and relaxation rate are reported. Those three quantities are dynamical proxies; none is the paper’s primitive mean first-passage lifetime. The exact zero on an already-balanced spectator is therefore a useful negative control, and the graded intervals show that an edge-sensitivity calculation is well defined and observer-relative, but they do not establish that the affected edges are causally necessary for continued existence. Across random driven rates even the edge ordering is not invariant.

The **viability-grounded mask demonstration is §11b**. There the declared functional is finite-horizon survival probability on a fully specified 36-state continuous-time Markov chain, the full coalition mask is evaluated, and the procedure recovers the model’s designed inert edge, load-bearing edge, redundant pair, and synergistic pair. Because that answer key is built into the gate topology and rates, §11b is a competence check rather than external validation or discovery. Together the two demonstrations establish computability, negative-control behavior, and recovery of a declared survival structure. Empirical validation against an answer key not manufactured by AOP remains open.

### 4The coupling graph and its classification

**[SYNTHESIS / organizing description of the current ledger.]** Energy is the hub in the present classification, but its spokes are not alike. This is a compact description of the assembled ledger—two forced floor-type spokes, one conditional maintenance edge, and one model-scoped feasibility constraint—not a theorem that energy is structurally privileged in every persister.

Drive → Memory: dissipation forces a memory floor. Any sustained dissipation leaves some trace: a strictly positive entropy production rate implies a strictly positive excess entropy (σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0). Here excess entropy is E = I(past; future), the past–future mutual information of computational mechanics [13]; it should not be confused with the "excess entropy production" of steady-state stochastic thermodynamics, an unrelated quantity that lives on the Drive axis. Unlike the brittle earlier reading, this edge is not a fresh directional guess but a scoped corollary of the trajectory-irreversibility identity we already invoke for Drive [1]. For a stationary process described by a single complete state, E = 0 holds if and only if past and future are independent, i.e. the process is i.i.d.; an i.i.d. process equals its own time-reverse in distribution, so its entropy production vanishes. The contrapositive is exactly σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0. Figure DM confirms this on a driven Markov ring: every dissipating configuration carries E > 0, while the single detailed-balance configuration has σ = 0 with E > 0 — the converse fails, a reversible oscillator remembers for free, so the necessity runs one way only.

The implication is secure but scoped, and four conditions must be stated or it is either false or vacuous. First, σ and E must be read on the same complete description: coarse-graining can hide a nonequilibrium current so that a reduced observable looks reversible (its E reads near zero) while the full dynamics dissipate — the Memory-axis twin of the observer-relativity of σ itself (Section 5). Second, the process must be stationary, the regime in which σ = D(forward ∥ reverse) is the clean object used here. **Third — a time-reversal parity condition.** The step from "E = 0 ⟺ i.i.d." to "i.i.d. ⇒ σ = 0" runs through the claim that an i.i.d. process equals its own time-reverse *in distribution*, and that claim is convention-dependent: it holds when **all state variables are even under the declared time-reversal**, or, more generally, **when the stationary one-point distribution is invariant under the reversal involution**. Where those hold, the contrapositive σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0 goes through as stated. The condition is satisfied trivially by every model computed on in this paper, all of which are configuration-space Markov processes in even variables; the driven ring of Figure DM is one. Where it is **not** satisfied — systems whose state carries odd variables (momenta, currents, spins), for which the stationary distribution need not be invariant under the parity involution — the theorem is **explicitly silent**, and this paper makes no claim about them until the reversal convention is declared for that case. The distinction is not a technicality invented here: the decomposition of entropy production for stochastic dynamics with odd as well as even variables carries a component beyond the familiar two, and the two-component treatment is recovered as the special case in which the stationary distribution *is* symmetric in the odd variables [Spinney & Ford 2012; Ford & Spinney 2012]. This is what makes the reversal convention **R** load-bearing rather than decorative in the declaration tuple **D** (§12″): an entropy production reported without **R** has not said which involution it is a divergence against, and a memory floor derived from it inherits that gap. **[Scope addition only. The theorem's grade is unchanged: forced × theorem/corollary. Nothing here reproves or weakens it.]** Fourth, and as before, what Drive forces is a memory floor (E > 0), not memory depth: it guarantees that some past–future correlation exists, not that the load-bearing memory is large. That the retained past has a thermodynamic price at all, and that the price falls on its wasteful part, is the content of the thermodynamics of prediction: the dissipated work is proportional to the *nonpredictive* retained information — the memory a system keeps that does not help predict its future — so efficiency is what drives stored memory to be predictive, and only predictive memory is free [3]. **Three notions must be kept apart here, permanently, and this paper does not re-fuse them.** (i) **Predictive dependence**, the excess entropy E = I(past; future): how much the past of a declared process constrains its future. (ii) **Predictive-state complexity**, the statistical complexity Cμ: the entropy of the causal states of the ε-machine *of a declared observation process* — a property of a measurement channel and the stochastic process it defines, never of an object's molecules [13]. (iii) **Stored physical organization**: the material structure a system actually holds. The first two are related by the crypticity decomposition Cμ = E + χ, with the gap χ ≥ 0 diagnosing when predictive dependence understates predictive-state complexity [13]. The third is *not* a member of that decomposition and cannot be substituted into it. Reading Cμ off molecular or material complexity, with no observation process declared, is a category error. E is the *forced* numerator, because a law touches it — the D→M floor. Cμ is the *candidate persistence-relevant* numerator for stored-structure persisters, and it becomes an actual quantity only once an observation process is declared and the ε-machine is measured on it. The spore is the case that **motivates** this choice of numerator — a persister whose stored organization plainly outruns what its past–future channel reports at near-zero dissipation (Section 11) — and it is used in that motivating role only. It is **not** a computed witness of high Cμ at low E: no observation process has been declared for a spore here, so no Cμ has been measured for one, and none is claimed. The periodic crystal is offered on the same footing, as the illustrative opposite pole (a fully predictable declared process, where the gap closes), not as a measurement. A pre-registered gate makes this coming-apart quantitative rather than illustrative: on a driven Markov ring, sweeping the cycle current leaves the causal irreversibility Ξ = Cμ⁺ − Cμ⁻ (the forward/reverse statistical-complexity asymmetry [13]) exactly zero at every dissipation rate — the forward and reverse causal-state reconstructions stay equal in complexity however hard the ring is driven. The stored complexity Cμ itself is not fixed — it covaries with the drive parameter — but that covariation carries no floor (it is weak, and it tracks the parameter's reshaping of the generator, not the dissipation rate: driving at a fixed reversible skeleton moves Cμ by ~1% while σ̇ moves twentyfold) and, decisively, it imposes no stored time-asymmetry at all. The drive writes its arrow into E; the stored-structure quantities either ignore the current (Ξ exactly) or merely drift alongside it with no forced floor (Cμ magnitude). So the floor Drive forces reaches predictive memory (E) and stops there, forcing neither a floor on stored complexity nor any stored time-asymmetry. The one edge that bites is the predictive-memory floor (E > 0); the D → M spoke does not reach the stored-structure quantities at all. Within these conditions the edge is a forced, scoped consequence of a cited law, and it joins the thermodynamic uncertainty relation as the hub's second clean forced spoke rather than a hedged inference. **The spoke is a scoped theorem, and it is not a pillar of "energy as hub."** It forces a floor on predictive memory — E > 0 — and nothing further: no floor on stored complexity, no stored time-asymmetry (Ξ = 0 at every drive), and no statement whatever about stored physical organization. Read it as a box with four scope conditions and one conclusion, not as evidence that energy organizes persistence.

That the stored-structure quantities escape the Drive floor is not an accident of this one edge; it reflects a structural fact about the whole hub, which a series of pre-registered gates on candidate Drive–Memory and Drive–resolvability joints made explicit (Section 12, the pre-registered cross-brick tests). In both model classes examined — Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck dynamics and finite Markov chains — the thermodynamic cost σ̇ is a functional of the generator's *antisymmetric* current part, while the memory- and structure-side quantities are governed by its *time-symmetric* part. The split is exact for the two quantities that carry the framework's forced claims: in the OU case the equilibrium covariance Σ that sets resolvability is Lyapunov-invariant to the driving circulation Q, and in the Markov case the causal asymmetry Ξ = Cμ⁺ − Cμ⁻ is identically zero at every drive. It is looser for the magnitudes: the stored complexity Cμ does covary with a drive parameter, because that parameter reshapes the symmetric part of the generator too (and Cμ retains a weak residual sensitivity to the current itself). But that covariation is shared-input — the pseudo-joint the gates were built to exclude — not a forced coupling through the current, and it carries no floor: no intervention on the dissipation rate forces a stored-structure quantity through a shared mechanism. This is why the hub's only forced spokes are the two floor-type edges (E > 0 and the TUR reliability bound) and no more: a forced cross-sector coupling would require a single quantity that depends on the antisymmetric sector as its *mechanism*, and among the tested quantities only σ̇ and E do — E being exactly the one the D → M floor already names. This is why the hub's forced spokes are exactly the two floor-type edges (E > 0 and the TUR reliability bound) and no more — a forced cross-sector coupling would require one quantity to depend on both sectors at once, which none of the tested quantities does. The claim is offered as framework synthesis, but its first half is no longer model-specific: that the thermodynamic cost σ̇ is a functional of the generator’s antisymmetric (irreversible) sector holds for general — including non-Gaussian and degenerate — diffusions [Da Costa et al. 2023], and is the general instance of the time-antisymmetric (entropy-flux) versus time-symmetric (frenesy / dynamical activity) split of the path-space action for Markov jump and diffusion processes [Maes 2020; Schnakenberg 1976]. What remains this framework’s synthesis is the short step that AOP’s own Memory/structure functionals — the equal-time covariance that sets resolvability, and the causal asymmetry Ξ — are time-symmetric observables; given that, no forced cross-sector coupling follows in general. That step is a lemma to write, not a frontier. Any quantity that appears to couple the two sectors should be checked for the failure mode those tests record: one sector mislabeled as the other (an entropy-production rate read as a memory asymmetry, or a drift-map scale factor read as a resolvability trade).

Drive → Reliability is forced and regime-bounded. The precision of any current a system runs is bounded by its dissipation: the thermodynamic uncertainty relation prices steadiness [4], [5]. Within the classical Markov regime in which it is proven, this is a law.

Drive → Boundary is conditional, and the condition is now computed. Holding a gradient costs nothing at equilibrium—a potential well is free—and costs drive only when the boundary must be maintained against a leak, with the price set by how leaky it is. On a minimal pump-and-leak nonequilibrium steady state, the maintenance burden (Boundary panel B4) equals the housekeeping entropy-production rate σ_hk = f·J (drive force × futile-cycle current): exactly zero at equilibrium, and, when a contrast Δ is held against a leak of conductance g, rising as σ_hk ≈ ½·Δ²·g(g+w)/w — linear in the leak, quadratic in the contrast held (an I²R-type cost) — verified against the Schnakenberg cycle decomposition to 1e-13 [Oono–Paniconi 1998; Hatano–Sasa 2001; Speck–Seifert 2005; deposited]. B4 is thus the Drive panel’s housekeeping term (D3) read on the interface — the concrete sense in which Drive→Boundary is one edge — and its empirical face is the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase’s ~20–45% share of resting-cell ATP.

Drive → Integration is free at equilibrium and, over time, a **feasibility (necessary-condition) constraint — not a causal or tendency edge in either direction, and in particular not a D→I tendency**. Parts can be correlated at zero dissipation; integration exists statically for free. The one dynamical result established is narrow: for classical discrete-state units on a permutation-invariant network, *robust extensive scaling* of stationary multipartite correlation — where "robust" means the extensive scaling persists throughout an open neighbourhood of the dynamics (it is not destroyed by any sufficiently small permitted perturbation) — cannot occur in thermal equilibrium, and arises only when the system relaxes to a time-dependent attractor (e.g. a limit cycle, or certain chaotic attractors), which exists only far from equilibrium [Ptaszyński & Esposito, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 135, 057401 (2025); arXiv:2410.13375]. This is a feasibility constraint on that specific quantity in that model class: robust extensive multipartite correlation is *infeasible without* dissipation. It does **not** establish that drive tends to build integration, nor a general "integration requires drive" — the paper explicitly leaves generalization beyond permutation-invariant systems open, and its quantity is stationary multipartite mutual information, not AOP Integration at large. Separately, creating, transforming, or continuously stabilizing correlations against erasure carries a thermodynamic cost *under the relevant protocol* [Parrondo, Horowitz & Sagawa 2015] — protocol-dependent bookkeeping, not a claim that every correlation must be continuously paid for; equilibrium correlations are the standing counterexample. "Requires" is not "produces," "must be paid for" is not "promoted by payment," and dissipation can equally destroy correlation. Claims that drive *maximizes* integration (maximum entropy production, not a settled principle) or generically *builds* it (strong readings of dissipative adaptation) are not relied on here. A genuine D→I tendency would require separate directional-intervention evidence — a declared ensemble in which raising drive raises integration — which neither the feasibility constraint nor the cost bound supplies. Within the paper's permutation-invariant model class, robust extensive scaling of stationary multipartite mutual information is infeasible without dissipation; "integration exists for free," that scoped infeasibility result, and "drive made this integration" are three distinct claims, and conflating any two is an error.

**Minimum-cut dependence: an irreducibility diagnostic, not an individuality verdict.** Integration measured as total correlation reports how much shared variance a system carries, but it does not distinguish a system whose parts are jointly irreducible from one whose parts are merely correlated: two independent, internally rich modules score high total correlation and high per-component resolvability blur, yet nothing about that score reports their separateness. The quantity that reports irreducibility is the mutual information across the least-disrupting bipartition — the **minimum-cut dependence**, written Φ_MIP where the symbol is convenient, but read throughout as a *minimum-cut irreducibility diagnostic* and not as integrated information in the sense of a consciousness measure. It is exactly zero on any block-decomposable system (a pile of separate parts) and rises, graded, as the parts become jointly irreducible. It is a third-person coupling measure carrying no self-ownership scalar, and it is the irreducibility flavor of interdependence, distinct from the redundancy flavor total correlation captures. Its ordering is invariant under per-component rescaling. **Provenance.** The construction used here — a minimum-cut search over the static Gaussian covariance Σ = (I + gL)⁻¹ — is AOP's, built in the lineage of Aguilera & Di Paolo [2019], whose treatment of integrated information across candidate partitions is its inspiration. It is not their measure imported unchanged, and it should not be cited as though their results transfer to it: their object is defined on a different model class and a different construction, and the static-Gaussian covariance version carries only such warrant as the deposited gate here supplies. **[SYNTHESIS; the construction is this framework's, the lineage is cited as inspiration.]** **What positivity does not license.** A positive minimum-cut dependence establishes that the system does not factor across its least-disrupting bipartition. It does **not** establish that the system is *one individual*, and no such inference is drawn anywhere in this paper. Irreducibility across a cut is one coordinate in an **individuation panel**, and a verdict requires the others: an autonomy reading, a causal-closure reading, an intervention-stability reading (does the candidate whole behave as a unit under the declared intervention class?), and common-cause controls (a shared driver produces irreducibility across every cut without producing an individual). No single coordinate in that panel is a verdict, and this paper reports the coordinate rather than the verdict. It is **not** robust across minimum-partition normalizations where the question is which partition is minimal: on a two-module Gaussian the raw selector relabels to a single-node cut at b ≈ 0.33, while size- and entropy-normalized selectors hold the module boundary to b = 1 and then move to a balanced cut. The normalizer is therefore a required declaration wherever partition identity carries weight, although magnitude ordering at a fixed partition is unaffected. It also inherits the integration-measure family's scope, being the minimum-cut member rather than a canonical Φ. This coordinate is established here only in the static Gaussian setting and only at a fixed partition; selecting which level of a nested system to read it on, and the non-stationary and critical extensions, are frontier (Section 13). It is graded framework synthesis — computed by a pre-registered five-criterion gate (deposited), not asserted — and it leaves the framework's refusal to individuate by an ownership scalar exactly where it was.

### 5Laws versus quantities: the scale claim, done carefully

A brittle early form of this framework held that “forced means scale-invariant”—that a forced coupling, being a law, holds unchanged from molecules to institutions. That reading breaks the moment one notices that measured irreversibility is itself scale-dependent, and that coarse-graining can create or destroy apparent causal structure [7].

The repaired claim is sharper and survives: the laws are observer-independent; the magnitudes are observer-relative. A forced coupling transfers across scale because it is a law—the second-law inequality σ ≥ 0 does not care about one’s grain. But how much boundary, how much memory, how much σ a system “has” depends entirely on the cut and resolution at which it is measured. The relation is invariant; the number is not. Figure 1 shows this directly: coarse-grained entropy production swings by tens of fold with the observer’s grain (roughly seventy-fold in the model of Figure 1), while the law σ ≥ 0 holds at every grain. That entropy production is measured at all in real systems—in living cells [8] and in the human brain [9]—is what lets the forced, law-tied quantities touch reality; the observer-relative magnitudes should stay scoped to a declared cut.

This relativity has a second face on the time axis, and there it cuts deeper, because one of the four numerators is not merely rescaled by the grain but *defined* relative to it. Boundary, Integration, and σ shift in magnitude with the spatial cut; Memory's excess-entropy numerator E shifts with the temporal one — and can cease to be defined — because E is defined only for a stationary process [Crutchfield & Feldman 2003], and stationarity is a claim about a chosen time-scale. A process stationary at one temporal resolution need not be stationary at another; cross a non-stationarity and E does not merely change value, it loses its definition. This is the time-axis instance of the same claim, not a new one: the law (the memory floor E ≥ 0, within its regime) is grain-independent, while the magnitude E is grain-relative — and on the time axis its very definedness is grain-relative. That complexity is accordingly quoted at a declared temporal grain is routine in the one field that measures it at cosmic scale: the statistical complexity of the cosmic web is reported over a "≈ 200 Myr timescale" [Vazza 2020]. The worked consequence is drawn out in Section 11 — a star is stationary on its thermal clock and non-stationary on its nuclear one, so its Memory has no single value until the clock is named. That E requires stationarity and that complexity is grain-relative are settled results; reading the time-grain as the exact twin of the spatial-grain limit is this framework's synthesis.

Where a persister is genuinely non-stationary — a developing organism, an aging system, the star on its nuclear clock — the time-resolved cousin of E survives: local (pointwise) active information storage is defined per time step and does not require global stationarity [Lizier, Prokopenko & Zomaya 2012], estimated off-stationarity from an ensemble of realizations rather than a time-average [Wibral, Lizier et al. 2014], with the honest caveat that the continuous-time formulation yields a clean memory-*use rate* but no clean instantaneous stored-*amount* off-stationarity [Spinney, Prokopenko & Lizier 2018]. E remains the stationary lead proxy (M1); local active information storage is the scoped proxy where stationarity fails.

Retention depth is a curve, not a bit-count. The predictive-information convergence E(T) = I(past_T ; future_T), computed closed-form on two OU systems, saturates at a depth set by the slowest *observed* pole: T₉₉ ≈ 19 steps for a cell-type system with a slow reference (20× separation) versus ≈ 1 for a star-type system of fast shells — a 12-fold difference in depth from timescale structure alone. Both lie in the bounded (finite-memory) regime of predictive information [Bialek, Nemenman & Tishby 2001]: a finite-dimensional stable linear-Gaussian process has a spectral gap and cannot reach the logarithmic or power-law regimes, which require a continuum of timescales. Depth is thus a property of the declared observable (part of the declaration tuple D) and its slowest pole, distinct from the single-number excess entropy [deposited].

Memory's own semantic weight is computable in the same idiom, on the same two systems. Scrambling the cell-type system's slow-reference readout — the memory-bearing edge — in isolation drops a present-tense viability functional by a fraction spanning [0.45, 0.80] across three viability choices, versus exactly 0 for an inert downstream spectator. The weight is regime-relative: the reference edge is load-bearing only when it holds the target more tightly than the declared tolerance, and where the remembered set-point is noisier than the tolerance the weight inverts — making concrete that a memory edge earns semantic weight only when what it stores is viability-relevant [deposited]. (This is a Memory-axis result and is retained in the core on that basis; the regulatory-architecture reading of the same two systems is developed in the follow-on paper, not here.)

### 6The two-axis view

Every coupling carries two independent tags: a **modal class** (free / conditional / forced) and a **semantic weight** (inert → load-bearing). The modal tag alone was never enough, because an edge can be present but semantically inert, or thin but load-bearing. Two tags, not one.

**These do not make a plane.** Plotting the two against each other as orthogonal axes invites a direction to be read off the picture — "persistence lives up-and-to-the-right" — and that reading is not available. It would require the modal classes to be *ordinal*, so that "more forced" is a direction one can travel in, and they are not: forced, conditional, and free are **categorical**. They name what kind of warrant an edge has — a law, a stated condition, or nothing — and there is no defensible sense in which conditional is a fixed quantity of modality between free and forced, nor in which a coupling being forced makes it more persistence-relevant than a conditional one. A forced edge can be semantically inert; a free edge can be load-bearing. Nothing about the modal tag orders anything.

The two tags are not of the same kind, and the replacement object — a **matrix, read as a tagged ledger** (Table 2′) — is built so that the asymmetry cannot be smoothed over by a picture. The modal tag is a *category*: forced/conditional/free is observer-independent but unordered, so it indexes rows and nothing more. The semantic tag is a *quantity*: as argued in Section 3, semantic weight is observer-relative, so an edge's semantic entry is not a single invariant coordinate but an interval that shifts with the declared partition, resolution, and viability functional. Each cell of the ledger therefore holds edges sharing a modal category, and each edge within a cell carries a semantic **range** rather than a value — the width of that range is itself the diagnostic (how far the weight moves as one varies the declaration), and measuring it is the natural first computational task the framework invites. The semantic tag is an interval throughout; the modal tag carries no ordering at all.

### A domain map for the edge-attribution estimator

**What follows is a domain map, not a law of persistence.** It says where the per-edge attribution *estimator* can be trusted on a declared family of synthetic models. It is not a statement about persisting systems in general. The per-edge mask is defined by intervening on one coupling at a time—scramble an edge in isolation, re-run, and read the viability drop—and that procedure presupposes the edges are separately manipulable. Within the declared family below, raising the integration knob degrades both halves of that presupposition, and mapping where it degrades is the useful content.

Two implications must be blocked before the map is read, because both are false in general. First, **high total correlation does not imply that mechanisms cannot be separately intervened on.** A common input produces arbitrarily high total correlation among components that remain individually and independently manipulable — correlation among *readings* is not entanglement of *mechanisms*, and the do-operation acts on the latter. Second, the converse fails too: **low total correlation does not guarantee that per-edge attribution is well posed**, since synergistic structure can leave components nearly uncorrelated pairwise while making no single edge separately meaningful. The estimator's domain is therefore not a function of total correlation, and any reading of this section that treats "integrated" and "un-intervenable" as synonyms is a misreading. What the map reports is the behaviour of a specific estimator on a specific declared family, and §13 states the measured band in which it is both well defined and informative.

Within that family the degradation is real and worth characterizing. When the declared coupling rises, no single coupling in the synthetic model can be scrambled without dragging the others with it, and the per-edge weight the estimator returns stops being well defined; simultaneously the aggregate, whole-system weight sharpens. This is not an uncertainty relation in any but a decorative sense: there are no conjugate variables, no quantitative bound, and no generality beyond the declared family. The honest, measurable form is the one given in Section 13: the width a semantic weight sweeps as the declared coupling rises, and the band outside which the per-edge reading should not be used at all.

This width is quantified in a minimal Gaussian model of n interdependent components with a single integration knob (equicorrelation ρ, Integration measured as total correlation). Two things happen together as ρ rises. The uncertainty in a per-component semantic weight diverges as (1 − ρ)^(−1/2) — equivalently as 1/√λ_min of the coupling matrix — while the aggregate, whole-system weight simultaneously *sharpens*. The individual parts become unresolvable at exactly the rate the whole becomes better determined.

The per-component divergence, taken by itself, is not a discovery about persistence: it is the ordinary statistics of estimating individual parameters in a correlated system, and it appears under three names already — multicollinearity in econometrics (the variance-inflation factor is the same 1/(1−ρ) form [Marquardt 1970]), parameter sloppiness in physics and systems biology (the Fisher-information spectrum spans orders of magnitude; sloppy directions are unconstrained while stiff directions and predictions are tight [Transtrum, Machta & Sethna, Phys. Rev. Lett. 104, 060201 (2010); Transtrum et al., J. Chem. Phys. 143, 010901 (2015)]), and attribution non-uniqueness in partial information decomposition [Williams & Beer, arXiv:1004.2515 (2010)]. The semantic mask inherits this limit rather than discovering it; that it does so is recorded as a limitation (Section 13), not claimed as evidence for the framework. Nor is the *pairing* — the whole becoming sharply resolvable at the same rate its parts become individually unresolvable — a second fact that survives the deflation. It is the same fact: the stiff/sloppy structure of the sloppiness picture, in which stiff, collective directions stay tightly constrained while sloppy, individual directions blow up [Transtrum, Machta & Sethna 2010; Transtrum et al. 2015]. "Whole sharpens as parts blur" is a restatement of "stiff stays tight while sloppy diverges," not an independent discovery about persistence. What AOP adds is therefore not a phenomenon but a reading of one: the interpretation that this already-known collective-vs-component dissociation is the mathematical shadow of the framework's refusal to individuate (the "no ownership" refusal). That interpretive link — sloppiness's stiff/sloppy split seen as the quantitative face of no-ownership — is the organizing observation, and it is a lens the framework puts on a fact sloppiness found, not a falsifiable forecast. Two refinements sharpen this limit without adding to it. The first is that the blur the mask suffers is not one failure but two, and the opening sentences of this section name both without separating them. There is an *inferential* failure — per-component attribution variance diverges (the variance-inflation factor), so no single edge's weight is separately *estimable* — and a distinct *interventional* failure — because coupled parts share a generator, an intervention on one drags the others, so an edge cannot be *isolated* even in principle by a do-operation. These are the two halves of the mask's own procedure (isolate an edge, re-run, read the drop): the second failure blocks the isolation, the first blocks the reading. They do not travel together. The second refinement is that, *within the declared synthetic family*, their ratio is fixed by the model's coupling *topology*, so the domain map is not one curve but a family of curves indexed by wiring. Both halves of that sentence are scoped: "both blur mechanisms are functions of the integration knob, and topology fixes their ratio" is a statement about the declared family below, not a general result about persisters. The equicorrelation model above is only its mean-field member — every component coupled equally to every other — and it is the degenerate case, with a two-level spectrum (one stiff collective mode, the rest identical). Give the same n components a different wiring and the signature moves in a specified way (Figure TF): a nearest-neighbour chain gives a graded stiff-to-sloppy band with high inferential blur and low interventional drag; a sparse hub-dominated network (a food web) gives the reverse, severe drag but mild inferential blur; a symmetric intervention on the collective mode blurs neither. In every topology the aggregate, whole-system mode stays sharply defined (its variance is conserved to numerical precision) while the parts blur — so the pairing of Section 6 is a property of the family, not of the one model. That the blur decomposes into an estimation failure and an isolation failure is a standard reading of the covariance; that their mix is set by coupling topology, with the aggregate mode invariant across topologies, is this framework's synthesis, computed here (Figure TF) and not asserted — and it is established for the five declared wirings of that figure, not for coupled systems at large.

### 7The observer, located: semantic claims are declaration-relative

“Observer” does real work here, so it must be pinned down rather than left as a black box. At every grain an observer does exactly three things: it declares a cut; it reaches the far side only by intervention (information comes only by posing a question); and it has a stake. What physically plays the observer changes by grain—a coarse-graining at the thermodynamic grain, a model partition at the statistical grain, an intervener at the causal grain. **One position is fixed and held everywhere: every semantic claim in this paper is explicitly relative to a declared persistence criterion.** It is tempting to say instead that at the grain that matters the observer is nothing external, being the system's *own* viability function. That formulation does two jobs at once and only one of them is legitimate. The legitimate job is to record that semantic information is meaningful *to* a system relative to *its* viability [2]: the functional is evaluated on the system's viable set, not on the analyst's preferences. The word "own" therefore denotes the set the functional is evaluated on, not a viability the system possesses or supplies, at every occurrence. The illegitimate job is to suggest that the system *performs* the evaluation, which no system in this paper does and which the framework's own refusals forbid. The corrected statement is this. A semantic weight is a three-place quantity: an edge, a declared viable set, and a declared functional V evaluated on that set. Change V and the weight changes; report a weight without V and nothing has been reported. "The system is its own observer" is therefore not a formulation this paper uses. What replaces it is not weaker but more explicit: **the analyst declares the criterion, and says so.**

This is where a homunculus is thought to threaten, and the declaration-relative formulation answers the objection directly. The worry runs: if the system's viability fixes which differences matter, who evaluates the viability function? If the answer is "we do, from outside," an external mind walks back in. Under the corrected formulation the answer is simply *yes, and it is named*. The analyst declares V. That declaration is written into the tuple **D** (§12″), travels with every reported number, and is available to be disputed. A homunculus is dangerous when it is *hidden* — when an evaluative act is smuggled inside the system and described as a physical fact. Naming the analyst as the declarer removes the hiding place entirely. Nothing is now doing evaluative work anywhere in the framework that is not either (a) a declared functional with a named declarer, or (b) a structural fact about the current state. The remaining chain still matters, because it is what keeps the *structural* half honest. (i) By the present-tense principle (Section 9), a legitimate viability criterion is checkable now. (ii) A property checkable now is a structural fact about the current state, not an assessment performed by anyone—the configuration either is or is not in the declared viable set, whether or not a witness scores it. (iii) What plays the selector over such states is selection, which is agentless: the frequency of good environments pays out the wager, with no one at the wheel [10]. The distinction that earns its keep: the bare membership fact — this state is or is not in the declared set — is analyst-independent *once the set is declared*; the magnitude or ranking of viability is relative to V throughout. The framework needs only the former to avoid a hidden evaluator, and states the latter's relativity explicitly. Where the present-tense principle is soft, the structural half is soft, and we mark it accordingly. This is containment by disclosure, not dissolution.

### 8Interfaces: passive and actively maintained

**What survives here is narrow, and v1.26 states only that.** A physical interface can be **passive** — it holds without ongoing expenditure — or **actively maintained** — it exists only because something is continuously paid to keep it. Screenable interactions make passive interfaces cheap: where an interaction can be interrupted (opposite charges, a Faraday cage, a lipid bilayer), a statistical separation of inside from outside can sit at a potential minimum and cost no drive to hold. That is the whole of the general claim, and it is enough for everything the paper does with Boundary.

**The screening reading is computed, and it needs none of the removed taxonomy.** The two notions of boundary—the informational Markov blanket and the thermodynamic gradient-maintenance cost—are genuinely distinct, and conflating them is a known error. The information-theoretic blanket is observer-relative by nature: one must first choose which node is “inside,” after which the blanket appears, and the instrumental and realist readings of that construction should not be run together [11]. On a static Gaussian model with a declared interface F, the screening residual B2 = I(X_in ; X_out | X_F) — the Markov-blanket condition written as a number — vanishes exactly (0.000 versus a cross-boundary dependence B5 = I(X_in ; X_out) = 0.896 nats) when inside and outside interact only *through* the interface, and stays positive (B2 = 0.292, B5 = 1.685) when a coupling bypasses it. So B2, not the dependence B5, distinguishes a cut *sealed by an interface* from one merely *coupled across* — the distinction a lone I(in;out) cannot make, and the computed basis for leading the Boundary panel with B1/B2/B4 rather than B5 (Table 1, and the lead-scalar correction) [Pearl 1988; Faes, Marinazzo & Stramaglia 2017; deposited]. This result is a property of the declared Gaussian model and its interface. It does not depend on which physical interaction realizes the interface, and it did not depend on the force taxonomy when that taxonomy was still present.

**One persister can carry both kinds of interface, and the star is the case that shows it** (developed in Section 11). A star holds an **actively maintained** radiative interface — the photosphere, whose opacity sets the leak the drive works against; scramble that opacity and it stops being a star — sitting on top of a **passive** gravitational one that costs nothing to hold. The pairing is the point: the two interfaces are of different kinds and only one of them is paid for.

### 9Present-tense viability

Any legitimate viability function must be checkable at the present instant. A viability that requires foresight—“this will eventually reproduce”—is illegitimate; it is hindsight worn as a goal. Persistence maintains; it never aims. Apparent foresight, such as a spore seeming to wait for spring, is a present capacity that past environments happened to favor; the wager is structural and retrospective, placed by no one and paid out by the frequency of good environments [10]. This kills teleology cleanly without denying that things can be poised for a future, and it is the principle on which the anti-homunculus chain of Section 7 depends. Present-tense viability splits into three checkable types: staying in a shape (configuration), keeping a process above threshold (process), and staying able to act (capacity)—the three exemplified by the crystal, the flame, and the spore respectively (Section 11).


Lineages do not breach the present-tense principle; they enter it at two admitted points and no  
third. First, *existence now*: a lineage exists now iff it is instantiated now — at least one  
member presently viable — a pure present-tense fact requiring no foresight, and one that fails  
exactly at the instant the last member dies. Second, as a *higher-order individual*: where a  
lineage is itself irreducible across its least-disrupting cut (positive minimum-cut dependence at the lineage partition,  
Section 4), its net reproduction is a present-tense flux — a Drive in this paper's sense —  
maintaining the lineage-form off its extinction-equilibrium on the lineage's own clock (Section  
5), and the lineage carries its own four-axis profile as any persister does. This second route is
available *in principle only*: it leans on minimum-cut dependence at a cross-generational, non-stationary partition —
exactly the nested level-selection extension Section 4 scopes out of the settled static-Gaussian axis
and marks **frontier**. It is offered as a direction, not an in-hand criterion. What the principle  
continues to refuse is the third move: reading a lineage's *future continuation* ("this lineage  
will persist") as a present-tense fact. That claim is not recoverable now and is the province of

### 9a Individuality at the collective scale: the two-level reading

Section 4 supplies one coordinate on whether a set of parts is jointly irreducible rather than a cloud — positive minimum-cut dependence at the
part-partition, the cut into parts stops being clean — and Section 9 fixes what a persister may
assert about itself in the present tense, placing reproduction and lineage-continuation *above* the
framework. Put the two together and a recurring question in the study of biological individuality
falls into place with a sharp division of labour: when a loose collection of persisters becomes a
single higher persister, which part of that event is inside AOP and which part is above it?

The ordinary word for a part's "self-interest" fuses two things that come apart cleanly on the scope
wall, and separating them is this section's synthesis. The first is **self-maintenance** — a part
running its own boundary, drive, and repair. This is nothing but the present-tense viability of
Section 9 read at the part scale: a part must keep it, because nothing else can metabolize for it,
and a part that loses it is a puddle with nothing for the collective to integrate. It is *inside*
AOP. The second is a **private reproductive stake** — an evolutionary interest in the part's own
propagation, separate from the whole's. That is a claim about differential continuation across
generations, and it sits *above* the wall, in the province of selection (Section 9). The two are
independent precisely because they live on two different levels, and the durable collectives are the
ones in which the parts keep the first and surrender the second: a somatic cell that fully forgoes
its own reproduction, a mitochondrion that has shipped most of its genome to the nucleus and cannot
reproduce outside the cell. A part that *retains* a private stake inside a whole that had suppressed
it is the failure mode the medical literature calls cancer — a breakdown of exactly the cooperation
that made the whole one thing [Aktipis et al. 2015, verified in body]. **[SYNTHESIS; it re-reads the
Section 9 scope wall at the part scale — maintenance below the wall, stake above it — and is the
cleaner statement of why the wall sits where it does.]**

On the near side of the wall, the framework's own machinery applies, and one inference does not.
A collective whose minimum-cut dependence is positive at the part-partition is **not** thereby a
single higher persister: that is the inference from positivity to individuality which §4 refuses in
general, and it is refused here too. It would also sit badly beside §13a, on which a minimum-cut
result at one fixed partition establishes nothing about which level is the individual. What the
coordinate reports is exactly this much: minimum-cut dependence at
the part-partition reports how far the collective fails to factor across its least-disrupting cut,
it is exactly zero on a block-decomposable pile, and it is one entry in an individuation panel
(autonomy, causal closure, intervention stability, common-cause controls — §4) that this paper does
not complete for any collective. Where a collective's four-axis profile is discussed below, it is
discussed under an explicitly declared collective partition, and the declaration is what makes the
discussion well posed. **[SYNTHESIS; no individuality verdict is claimed.]**

On the far side of the wall sits the *mechanism* by which a collective drives the private-stake dial
toward zero and holds it there — and this is cited context, not an AOP claim, because it is a
selection process operating across generations, which the framework does not adjudicate. Two devices
recur across the independent origins of multicellularity. Across generations, passing each new
collective through a **single-cell bottleneck** makes offspring-collectives genetically uniform, so
a defector lineage cannot ride between generations and organism-level selection can act on whole
individuals [Grosberg & Strathmann 2007; Michod 2007 — "How do
groups become individuals? This is the central question," resolved through fitness tradeoffs and
reproductive altruism]. Within a generation, **policing** — the collective actively repressing
competition among its parts — suppresses defectors before the next bottleneck arrives [Frank 1995], and where a life cycle evolves, the collective's
fitness can decouple from its cells' [Hammerschmidt, Rose, Kerr & Rainey 2014]. The organismality this produces is well captured by the definition of
an organism as an entity of high cooperation and near-absent conflict among its parts [Queller &
Strassmann 2009], and the whole staircase — replicating molecules to chromosome,
free bacterium to organelle, single cells to body, solitary insects to colony — is the settled
major-transitions picture [Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995]. **[SETTLED external biology; recorded
here as the above-wall process that motivates the scope wall, and flagged as a cross-project
(Ladder) propagation-bus item, not a persistence claim of this paper.]**

The collective scale also poses one structural question that the core paper does not take up. The
regulatory architecture of §11a — correction against a dynamically decoupled, separately
interventable reference — can in principle be asked about at the collective scale as well as the
part scale: does a collective ever hold such a reference for the *collective's* own viable set,
one that is not simply the sum of its members'? An ant colony regulating its nest toward a
set-point no single ant represents is the standing example of why the question is not idle. That
question, and everything that would be needed to answer it, is developed in the follow-on paper
rather than here, for the same reason the rest of the life material moved: it is bottlenecked on
the nested-level, non-stationary extension of minimum-cut dependence that Section 4 scopes out
(the F2 seam, open in both halves — §13a), and answering it would additionally require the
individuation panel Section 4 declines to complete. **[Pointer only; the question and its
frontier grade live in the follow-on.]**

### 10The domain and its edge: an operational scope condition

Every one of the four dimensions presupposes a now. Boundary is an interior held over time; Drive is a rate per time; Memory is past constraining future across time; Integration is parts cohering over time. The question is what it takes for a system to be *in scope* for the framework at all, and v1.26 answers it operationally rather than physically.

**The scope condition.** AOP applies wherever the following five can be declared: a **subsystem** S; a **state representation** for it (including the time grain δt and the time-reversal convention R under which its variables are even or odd); a **persistence functional** V on a declared viable set; a **horizon** τ over which that functional is evaluated; and an **admissible intervention class** I. These are not new requirements invented for this section — all five are already slots in the declaration tuple **D** (§12″), and every number in this paper is already reported under them. The domain of the framework is exactly the set of systems for which the tuple can be filled in. Where it cannot be filled in, the framework is silent; where it can, the framework applies, and nothing further about the system's physics is required to admit it.

**What the change does and does not decide.** The operational condition is deliberately weaker: it does not adjudicate whether any particular exotic system "really" has a present tense, and it does not need to. It asks only whether the five declarations can be made for that system. Two consequences follow and are stated rather than left implicit. First, admission is now a property of the *description* one can supply, not of the object — which is consistent with the rest of the paper, where every magnitude is declaration-relative (§2, §7). Second, "rest mass as the price of persistence" does not survive in any form, and the bounding-principle inventory is updated accordingly (§13).

### 11Five motivating cases

The abstractions are illustrated on five concrete things. Four form a progression from full persistence to a bare minimum (crystal, flame, spore, bound atom); the fifth — the star — is not a point on that line but a case in a corner none of the others occupy: high Drive, high Integration, gravitationally bound. **These are motivating cases and illustrations. They are not the framework's evidence for anything**, and §11's closing paragraph states what they are and are not. A mode of persistence is a shape across the four dimensions in semantic weight (Figure 5); a transition is a change in that shape. All four axes are declaration-relative (§2); Boundary and Integration additionally require a declared partition, and are the softer of the four on that account. The profiles below show all four together for readability, but the two tiers do not have equal footing, and the figure marks which is which.

A crystal is configuration whose semantics are spent. The living thing was the growth front — drive depositing structure; once grown, drive goes to zero and only the husk remains, memory made solid. Its structure is load-bearing, but **passively**: disrupt the lattice and the crystal ceases, so the structure plainly bears weight — yet holding that lattice costs no drive at all. What was spent is the growth front that built it, not the structure's weight for continued persistence. The crystal rests on a passive floor it pays nothing to maintain. A flame is process with its semantics all in drive: a sharp boundary rebuilt each instant from current conditions, with shallow or short-lived memory. It is the physical counter-witness that keeps Boundary and Memory from being welded by law; it is also the maximally observer-relative boundary (thermal, ionization, and luminous edges all differ). Temporal correlation in a flame's temperature, radical-concentration, flow, and front-position fields is not zero; the claim is that its memory is shallow and short-lived relative to the timescale on which it persists, which is all the counter-witness requires. A spore is capacity with its semantics in escrow: drive switched off, structure locked, viability held in reserve and re-formable when a coupling re-forms. Its coat is a lock, not a reader; the germinant is the key; a dead lock still opens to its key. This is bet-hedging made physical — stored information with zero current viability value and high conditional-future value [10] — and it is what **motivates** the choice of Memory numerator: a persister whose stored organization plainly outruns what a past–future channel would report on it. As §4 sets out, that motivation is a thought experiment and not a measurement: statistical complexity is a property of a declared observation process, and none has been declared for a spore here, so no Cμ is computed or claimed for one.

A bound atom is the minimal illustrative case: a screenable electromagnetic interface gives it an interior, held passively at essentially no drive. It marks the low end of the range the paper illustrates rather than a rich interior of its own. A ground-state hydrogen atom is stable absent perturbation, so it is not a brief persister; and one cheap passive interface is the whole of the illustration.

A star is the case at the opposite corner, and it earns its place for a reason none of the other four supply: it is where the framework's own instrument is driven soft. Its four dimensions are all high on their own panels and all entangled. (The axes carry no common normalization — the paper denies one elsewhere (§1, §2) — so "maximal across all four at once" has no defined meaning and is not asserted.) Drive is the defining axis — fusion is a coherent central entropy source resupplying, against radiative loss, the thermal pressure that balances gravity; kill it and the star dies on a thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) time. Boundary is two interfaces of different kinds (Section 8): an **actively maintained** radiative one — the photosphere, whose opacity sets the leak the drive works against, and which is load-bearing in the strong sense that scrambling it stops the object being a star — sitting on a **passive** gravitational one that costs nothing to hold. Only one of the two is paid for, and that asymmetry is the surviving content of this observation. Integration is very high and non-separable: hydrostatic equilibrium couples every shell to the weight above and the structure below, so no single shell's coupling can be scrambled without the rest hydrostatically readjusting. Memory is left qualitative, as with the spore, but here for a reason the framework can now name precisely: it is the star that makes the time-grain relativity of Section 5 concrete. Mass and composition largely fix the track, yet the excess-entropy numerator E has no single value for a star, because E is defined only for a stationary process and a star's stationarity is clock-dependent — stationary on its thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) clock, manifestly non-stationary on its nuclear-evolution clock, along which composition drifts and the track moves. So the star's Memory is not merely un-asserted between E and stored complexity Cμ [13]; its E is undefined until the clock is named. This is the same observer-relativity Section 5 states for the spatial grain, now on the time axis and sharp enough to remove a magnitude rather than merely rescale it. There is a tension here worth naming rather than leaving for the reader to find: the framework's cleanest, forced claim on the Memory axis — the D→M floor σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0 — holds only for a stationary process, so on the very case the paper spends the most ink on it can be evaluated only on the thermal clock, and goes silent on the nuclear one where the star is non-stationary. The flagship sits precisely in the regime where the Memory-axis law is undefined. We take this as the point rather than an embarrassment: it is the star that shows the floor is a clock-relative statement, not a defect in the floor, and a persister whose Memory is grain-dependent is exactly the object that makes the time-grain relativity concrete instead of abstract. Two model results are worth stating, and both are reported here as results about the declared stellar model rather than as features of stars in general. First, the star's **self-regulation** rests on a settled property of self-gravitating systems: negative specific heat — radiate energy and the system gets hotter [Lynden-Bell & Wood 1968] — which is the microcanonical signature of the non-additivity of long-range interactions [Campa, Dauxois & Ruffo 2009]. That gives the star a genuine restoring mechanism, and it is retained. The thermostat is a restoring mechanism and is claimed as nothing more; in particular it is not offered as evidence that gravitational non-additivity also fixes a floor on Integration, which this paper does not claim. Second, the star supplies a **resolvability signature computed from stellar structure** rather than assumed: because its shells are locked by hydrostatic equilibrium, the declared shell model sits in the region of the §6 domain map where per-edge attribution is not well posed — the mask flags the coupled opacity–gradient–generation–pressure loop as load-bearing at the coalition level but cannot assign per-edge weights, and the gravitational coupling admits no separate intervention within the declared intervention class. Here the star's coupling is not imposed but read off its structure, which is what lets it stand as more than an illustration of Section 6. Linearizing the adiabatic pulsation equation on a Lane–Emden n=3 polytrope — the settled apparatus of stellar structure — gives a tridiagonal shell-to-shell operator, a chain with a global hydrostatic constraint, whose spectrum is a graded stiff-to-sloppy band (Figure R★). It is therefore not the mean-field equicorrelation caricature (which the family of Figure TF places as the degenerate two-level member); at matched mean inter-shell correlation the derived operator has a stiff/sloppy spread roughly an order of magnitude wider, and its per-shell attribution uncertainty is *larger* than the caricature's — the parts blur harder precisely because the coupling is the real one. Read through this derived coupling the star sits high on both blur mechanisms of Section 6 at once — per-shell weights unresolvable (inferential) and gravity resisting intervention entirely (interventional) — while the aggregate, collective mode stays sharply defined. The star therefore realizes the Section 6 resolvability limit from its own physics rather than being relabeled into it; it does not escape the mask-blur regime the framework most wants to describe, it is driven into it by the same hydrostatic coupling that holds it up. It should be said clearly what this does and does not do: any strongly, non-degenerately coupled linear system has a stiff/sloppy spectrum — that is the general content of the sloppiness picture — so a real integrated system could not have come out *not* blurred, and the star is therefore a demonstration of self-consistency (the framework says integrated systems blur; here is a real one, built from its own physics, that does), not a confirmation that could have failed. What the derivation adds over the equicorrelation caricature is not informativeness about whether the effect occurs but fidelity about its shape — a graded band rather than an imposed two-level spectrum — and the removal of the suspicion that the result was smuggled in by the model choice. Computed on the star’s own shell operator — the linearized adiabatic pulsation chain of a Lane–Emden n=3 polytrope (ξ₁ = 6.897 reproduced) with a global hydrostatic constraint — the **declared polytropic shell covariance is redundancy-dominated in O-information (Ω > 0)**: robust across coupling strength, invariant under per-component rescaling, and shared by every cooperative topology tested (mean-field, chain, sparse hub); only a common-effect/collider structure flips it to synergy. The interdependence *of that declared covariance* is therefore degenerate (shared) rather than conjunctive — a part-ablation loses little — so the content is carried by the sign, not the magnitude, exactly as the directional-only posture requires [Rosas et al. 2019; deposited]. The result attaches to the model, not to the object: it is a property of the polytropic shell covariance AOP declared, and the sentence "the star is redundancy-dominated" is not licensed by it. The Ω > 0 computation itself is unchanged.

The star also sharpens a distinction the flame alone left ambiguous. Both are all-in-drive, boundary-rebuilt-continuously persisters — but the flame has no restoring force (marginally stable, at the mercy of current supply; interrupt it and it is gone with no fight), while the star has a strong one: the negative-specific-heat thermostat is a present structural property, not a bet on the future (core cools ⇒ pressure drops ⇒ contracts ⇒ virial heating ⇒ steeply temperature-sensitive fusion climbs ⇒ restores). Same drive-dominated character, opposite stability class. This also answers a Section 9 worry directly: the star's apparent goal-directedness ("keep burning") is not hindsight worn as a goal but present dynamical stability, checkable now — hydrostatic and thermal balance read on the current state — exactly as the present-tense principle demands.

### 11a A regulatory architecture that separates classes of persisters

The five cases fix the axes; they also expose a structural distinction the axes do not name. Three of the persisters actively hold themselves off equilibrium — the flame, the star, and (were it running) the cell — and they do not all do it the same way. The flame has no restoring force at all. The star has one whose set-point *is* a fixed point of its own constitutive dynamics: it corrects, but nothing in it stores the target, so there is no separate reference to intervene on. The cell has a restoring force driven against a **regulatory subsystem that is dynamically decoupled from the process it regulates** and is separately interventable — a set-point held in one place and read out onto another, corruptible independently of the physiology it steers. AOP locates that third architecture, and it is a coupling fact the mask (§3) and the generator spectrum are built to detect: a load-bearing internal coupling from a slow reference onto fast regulated dynamics. Stated invariantly, the discriminator is the existence of a **proper invariant subspace whose dynamics are autonomous with respect to the regulated coordinates** — a formulation that is preserved under similarity transformation, since invariant subspaces map to invariant subspaces, and that is not defeated by a change of basis because an arbitrary basis change is not a member of the declared physical intervention class **I** (§12″).

This paper takes the architecture no further. It is offered as a **candidate regulatory architecture that separates classes of persisters**, graded as this framework's synthesis, and it is deliberately **not** named *alive* here. Readers will notice where paradigmatic living things fall, and that is the point of stating it; but developing it as a criterion for life — the present-tense tiers it would require, the diachronic tracking relation it presupposes, the control-theoretic results that do and do not support it, and its relation to the field's standard definitions — is treated in a dedicated follow-on paper (`AOP_LifeArchitecture_Followon`) and is not carried in the core. That separation is scope discipline: nothing about the architecture is in doubt here, and this paper simply declines to run the further argument that would turn it into a definition. **[SYNTHESIS; the architecture is a coupling fact. ### 11b A unit test of the semantic intervention protocol

The five cases of §11 are motivating illustrations; their four-axis profiles are illustrative shapes, not measurements (§11 figure caption). This section supplies something different in kind: a **unit test** — an exactly-solvable system with a designed answer key, on which the semantic intervention protocol (§3) is checked for competence. Its job is to establish that the protocol, run on a system whose inert, load-bearing, redundant, and synergistic structure is known by construction, **recovers that structure**, where a strength- or correlation-based reading inverts it. That is a test of the instrument, not a finding about persistence.

**The system.** A leaky autocatalytic compartment as a continuous-time Markov chain on states (n, r, z): interior copy-number n∈0..8 (n=0 extinction, absorbing), a fuel-driven regulator r∈{0,1}, and a downstream readout z∈{0,1} that tracks r but feeds nothing back. Thirty-six states; everything below is closed-form (survival by matrix exponential of the transient subgenerator; the full 2⁷ coalition table; Möbius and Shapley terms), no Monte-Carlo. Seven intervenable mechanisms: a redundant birth pair {A,B} (OR-gated — either alone suffices), a synergy pair {S1,S2} (AND-gated — both needed), a leak-suppressor R, a weak constitutive influx C, and the inert readout coupling Z. Viability V(θ,τ) is finite-horizon survival from the healthy state over τ=15; baseline V=0.93.

**The dissociation a one-axis reading inverts.** The mechanism ranked first by structural coupling strength — both by injected rate and by mutual information — is the inert readout Z, whose viability importance is exactly zero (ΔV(Z)=0, Shapley 0). The leak-suppressor R, low by every structural proxy, carries the largest single-mechanism viability effect (ΔV=0.16). Across the seven mechanisms the rank correlation between structural strength and viability importance is **negative** (Spearman −0.67): a one-axis reading does not merely underperform, it inverts. Single-edge deletion finds the redundant pair {A,B} entirely inert (ΔV=0 each) while their joint deletion is catastrophic (ΔV=0.44); the Möbius interaction is +0.44 for the redundant pair (which a naive reading calls synergy) and −0.12 for the synergy pair (which it calls redundancy) — the signs invert the naive labels. The minimal failure cut-sets are {A,B}, {R,S1}, {R,S2} — no single mechanism is a cut-set — and two disjoint routes, {A,R} and {A,S1,S2}, each preserve viability, an emergent substitutability not built in. All verdicts hold across horizons τ∈{8,12,15,20,30}.

**What is and is not claimed.** The ground truth is built in throughout: {A,B} redundant, {S1,S2} synergistic, Z inert, R load-bearing, and — because these are set by the gate topology and the rate assignments — the strength-vs-viability anti-ranking and the Möbius sign pattern follow from the construction (swapping the OR/AND wiring flips the Möbius signs). The benchmark is therefore a **unit/competence test, not a discovery**: it shows the coalition-aware, viability-anchored protocol *correctly recovers* a designed structure on a system where a strength- or correlation-based reading inverts it. **What it does not show is that the four-target apparatus is *necessary*.** This benchmark cannot establish necessity: it was constructed by the same people who built the apparatus, its answer key is a property of the construction, and no rival method was run against it. Necessity is not recoverable from a self-authored answer key, and no necessity claim is made. Establishing it requires an external benchmark whose ground truth is set outside the modeler, with a pre-registered gate frozen before any run — that is a separate, still-open piece of work and is deferred (see the external-ground-truth benchmark deliverable, §13). What remains, and it is worth having: the protocol is computable in closed form on a fully specified 36-state system; it returns the designed inert edge, load-bearing edge, redundant pair, and synergistic pair; and the coalition layer (minimal cut-sets, disjoint viability-preserving routes, Möbius interactions) is what does the recovering, while single-edge deletion alone reports the redundant pair as entirely inert. That last point is the section's strongest content and is an argument for the coalition default of §3, not for necessity. [analytic-model-result; the designed structure and the dissociation it induces are forced by the construction — the contribution is the correct recovery, not the dissociation.] It does not establish that AOP is uniquely correct or beats any rival.

### 12″ The declaration tuple and the measurement panels

Every quantity in this paper is reported under a common **declaration tuple** D = (S, E, F, P, δt, τ, R, V, I, N): the system variables, environment, interface, partition, time grain, horizon, **reversal convention**, viability functional, intervention class, and normalization. It is also the front-of-paper ontology (§1) in its operational form — nodes are targets, proxies are measurement families attached to them, edges are propositions under **D** and a model class **M**, reliability is a downstream outcome, and lifetime is the declared outcome primitive.

**Three slots are load-bearing and are called out here so they are not skipped.** **R, the reversal convention.** It carries the fourth scope condition of the D→M theorem (§4): entropy production is a divergence between a forward trajectory ensemble and a *reversed* one, and the reversal is not defined until one has said which state variables are even and which are odd under it. A σ reported without R has not said which involution it is a divergence against, and any memory floor derived from it inherits that gap. Declaring R is therefore mandatory wherever Drive or the D→M edge is reported, and the models computed on in this paper all declare even, configuration-space variables. **V, the viability functional.** Following §7, every semantic weight is relative to a declared persistence criterion — a viable set and a functional evaluated on it — and V is where that criterion is written down. There is no semantic reading without V, and the declarer is the analyst. Which member of the persistence-functional family V belongs to (mean first-passage time, survival curve, hazard, finite-horizon survival, recovery probability) is declared with it (§1). **I, the intervention class.** Every intervention reported must satisfy the internal-edge intervention protocol (§3), and I is where the admissible operations are named. I also does load-bearing work in §11a: a change of basis is not a member of any physical intervention class, which is why a coordinate-dependence objection to a subspace-level criterion does not land.

The four dimensions are read out not as single scalars but as **panels** — families of proxies — because a lone proxy silently inherits the ambiguity of the target it stands for (mutual information is dependence, not separation; total correlation is interdependence, not proven unity; entropy production is dissipation, not free-energy throughput). On the benchmark, the panels make the point in closed form: the inert readout Z dominates the Drive and Integration panels (it carries most of the stationary dissipation and cross-component correlation) while contributing nothing to viability — so a panel reading describes where activity and correlation sit, and only the viability-relative layer says what persistence depends on. Consistent with §3, that viability-relative layer reports **coalitions** by default and per-edge weights only where additivity and identifiability tests pass. The panels, their proxy families, and their declaration tuples are deposited (operational-panels deliverable).

### 13Limitations and outlook

The framework's honest exposure is concentrated in three places. First, **all four dimensions are declaration-relative**, and Boundary and Integration additionally require a declared partition (§2); Integration in particular has at least six inequivalent published measures that agree on rank but disagree on values, trends, and even sign [12], and any claim about the non-energy triangle inherits that ambiguity. Second, the semantic mask, though well defined as a construction, has its weights determined only relative to a declared persistence criterion; on any concrete substrate the couplings must be separately manipulable for **per-edge** weights to be defined, and often they are not. The paper responds to this structurally rather than by caveat: the default reported object is a **coalition** (§3), and the per-edge reading is used only inside a measured band. The blur that motivates the demotion decomposes into an inferential (estimation) and an interventional (isolation) part whose ratio is fixed by coupling topology across the five declared wirings of Figure TF — a **domain map for the estimator**, scoped to that synthetic family, and explicitly not a law of persistence (§6). The star (Figure R★) is its worked, structure-derived instance. Third, semantic claims are declaration-relative and the anti-homunculus argument runs by disclosure — the analyst declares V and is named (§7) — which still rests on the present-tense principle being airtight for its structural half.

**The external benchmark remains the framework's principal missing evidence, and it is deferred.** Every computed demonstration in this paper — Figures MW, LT, R★, and the §11b unit test — is a self-consistency or competence check whose answer key AOP wrote. None could have come out otherwise, and §11b makes no necessity claim for exactly that reason. What is needed is a benchmark with **externally-sourced ground truth**, a **pre-registered gate frozen before any run**, and a bind-before-freeze protocol — work that is commissioned separately and deliberately not attempted here.

A fourth exposure, newly bounded rather than newly opened: the coupling classification treats Boundary, Drive, and Memory as distinct spokes, and a recent external reframing proposed instead that they are three interchangeable "currencies" of one persistence budget. Put through the same pre-registration discipline (Section 12), that stronger claim fails: on a minimal Markov model two of the three mechanisms showed a hard persistence ceiling while only the equilibrium wall did not. That ceiling ordering was then itself pre-registered and tested on a real driven chemical system (the Schlögl bistable birth–death network) — and did **not** survive: on real mass-action kinetics both the wall and the stored-copy reset drive persistence arbitrarily high, and a pure dissipative current has *zero* leverage on persistence at all. The ceilings were artifacts of the toy's small fixed state space, and the "strict ordering" reading does not hold. What survives is cleaner and stronger for being model-independent: in a one-dimensional birth–death system a persister's stationary *occupancy* of its viable set is a functional of the stationary distribution alone, so only mechanisms that reshape that distribution (an equilibrium wall, or a reset into a metastable well) can raise occupancy, while a pure current cannot — but the paper's chosen primitive is *lifetime* (§1), and lifetime is current-sensitive even at fixed stationary distribution (a divergence-free current cuts mean escape time ~5.7× on a driven ring, deposited gate), so under the primitive the framework actually uses, Drive has **direct anti-persistence leverage** in this declared regime. That result is not the coupling graph’s D→M or D→B structure and must not be described as indirect action through those edges. A positive pro-persistence role for Drive is conditional or mediated and is not established by this theorem; the stellar case remains open. The one genuinely new, surviving object is the framework's first computed iso-persistence tradeoff surface with finite exchange rates; its generalization beyond the minimal model is open.

The productive step here was not to add a dimension but to make the semantic mask compute on a minimal, deliberately resolvable model with an explicit, present-tense viability functional, and to characterize not a single weight but the range a weight sweeps as the declared viability is varied. That has now been done: the mask is computed on the Figure DM ring (Figure MW, Section 3), returning graded weights on the load-bearing edges, exactly zero on a present-but-inert spectator, and — because semantic weight is a quantity that Section 5 says moves with the grain — an interval rather than a point for each load-bearing weight. That range is the framework's characteristic measurable; it is a shape the synthesis commits to, not a forecast that could adjudicate the framework against a rival. A clean single number would hide exactly what the account says is there. Run per edge on a coupled-Gaussian system with a well-posed two-module part-partition, the scramble-and-rerun mask shows per-edge semantic weights becoming interval-valued and unresolvable as coupling rises — both blur mechanisms climb (inferential variance-inflation factor and interventional edge-drag), and the sloppy attribution 1/√λ_min diverges — while the aggregate collective-mode weight stays load-bearing and far sharper (the stiff direction 1/√λ_max stays bounded), realizing the Section 6 domain map on the mask’s own weights rather than on a separate model, within that declared family [deposited]. A sweep over three topologies (chain, mean-field, sparse-random; deposited `phaseE2`) bounds where the per-edge mask is usable, and the bound is tight: it is simultaneously well-defined (resolvable to within a factor of two — state-correlation condition number κ ≲ 9) and informative (the load-bearing/inert gap exceeds three times the blur) only in a weak-to-moderate integration band — total correlation up to ≈0.5 but with minimum-cut dependence ∈ [0.0003, 0.05], i.e. near-zero irreducibility — and it blurs out before strong integration is reached: the informative gap is swamped first, then the weight becomes unresolvable, so the **per-edge** mask does not reach the strongly integrated regime (the regulatory architectures of §11a, the star) it was built to describe — which is why the coalition object, not the per-edge band, is the default semantic layer (§3). The one thing it is not is trivial: this usable band lies above the near-separable triviality floor (total correlation above the 5%-coupling floor), so the mask is not confined to trivial cases — only to weak-to-moderate integration. Like Figure MW it is a demonstration of self-consistency, not a test that could fail; and stated honestly, the aggregate mode stays *sharp*, not exactly invariant. It is worth being exact about the kind of exposure the framework has. The domain map is not a falsifiable prediction, and should not be presented as one. It asserts a relation between how strongly coupled a system is and how widely its semantic weights sweep as the partition is varied — but within the model class the framework uses, that relation is structural, not contingent: per-component attribution variance is governed by the smallest eigenvalue of the precision, so within a fixed topology raising the coupling widens the inferential sweep, and at matched mean coupling a more graded spectrum out-blurs a degenerate one, both by construction rather than by any fact a measurement could contradict. The computed instances (Figure TF; the declared stellar shell model versus its equicorrelation caricature) are therefore demonstrations of self-consistency — the account says integrated systems blur, and its own worked systems blur in the predicted way — not tests that could have come out otherwise. This is the posture the rest of the paper already takes toward its computations (Figures MW, LT, R★); §13 is brought into line with it here. Where the framework is genuinely exposed is its carving, and that exposure is taxonomic rather than predictive. The claim that these four axes are the right ones can be shown wrong in the ordinary way a taxonomy can: by a persister whose four-axis profile mis-describes it — one that demands a fifth axis the four cannot express, or that forces two the paper holds distinct to collapse into one that always co-moves. Completeness and minimality are held open (Section 2) precisely because this is the live question, and it is the honest form of "this could be wrong." None of this makes the paper a rival theory settled by a crucial experiment, and it does not present itself as one. It is a synthesis of established results, and a synthesis earns adoption on different terms: by unifying quantities the source literatures pose in isolation, and by refusing to lose the distinctions a single-axis account must discard — the flame's maintained boundary at shallow, short-lived memory, and the spore's stored organization at near-zero drive (Section 11). That is the standard the paper asks to be read against, and the one it is built to meet. The energy hub is coherent and defensible; the non-energy triangle is where the work remains; and the account now marks precisely where its own domain ends — at the point where the five declarations of §10 can no longer be supplied. The irreducibility diagnostic of Section 4 (minimum-cut dependence) sits at the same frontier in two directions that the next work targets: it is defined only at a fixed partition, so which level of a nested system (cell, organism, society) one should even read it on is open and is the natural next Gaussian gate; and its non-stationary and critical payoffs — the properties that would let it speak to developing systems and to the integration-blur limit — require a model class with a phase transition, which the static Gaussian setting does not provide.


### 13a Level selection and the moving partition

**Level selection across a nested hierarchy [OPEN].** Minimum-cut dependence is defined at a *fixed* grain; a nested system (nodes within modules within a whole) also poses the *which-grain* question. The literature supplies several related but distinct rules: the Φ^Max "complex" orders candidate systems/grains within IIT [Hoel, Albantakis, Marshall & Tononi 2016; Marshall, Findlay, Albantakis & Tononi 2026], Liu, Yuan & Zhang [2024] give a closed-form treatment of optimal linear coarse-graining for Gaussian-noise dynamics, and Aguilera & Di Paolo [2019, §III.B] compare integrated information across candidate systems. This framework has not ported those rules.

A genuine level-selection result in this setting requires four declarations not yet supplied: (1) a coarse-graining map; (2) a construction of a part as a subsystem — marginal, conditional, and isolated constructions place the module/whole equality at b = 0.3002, 0.2163, and 0.6296 respectively; (3) a convention for comparing the diagnostic across grains of different dimension; and (4) an actual comparison across candidate grains, rather than a changing minimum cut within one fixed graph. The general point is worth stating on its own: **a minimum-cut relabeling demonstrates that the argmin changed; it does not by itself evidence a change in individuality.** The relabeling is also normalization-dependent — size- and entropy-normalized selectors hold the module boundary until inter- and intra-module coupling are equal, then move to a balanced cross-module cut, while the raw selector relabels to a single-node cut at b ≈ 0.33 (eight nodes; b ≈ 0.42 for six).

**Adiabatic moving partition.** The instantaneous spatial minimum-cut dependence on a slowly varying covariance remains well posed: zero-calibration survives off stationarity, and along a ramp merging two modules the minimum cut relabels while the diagnostic stays continuous and its derivative is discontinuous, under the declared adiabatic condition ε = ramp-rate / relaxation-rate ≪ 1. The kink marks an exchange of discrete optimizers, not an individuation event. It identifies what a time-extended partition would have to track; it does not show that the system became one individual. The time-extended diagnostic over a developmental window remains **frontier**, because no single fixed partition scores a window straddling the relabel, and the effects-based temporal alternative inherits the definedness problem of §5.

**Stellar positive-persistence mechanism [FRONTIER].** Whether a star's fusion lengthens its AOP lifetime — and by what mediation — is open. The measure-preserving mean-first-passage-time theorem is out of scope for the star, because fusion reshapes the stationary state; but out-of-scope is not a positive sign. Settling it requires a declared intervention on nuclear energy generation, a declared AOP exit set (loss of hydrostatic equilibrium, main-sequence departure, fuel exhaustion, and cessation of luminous fusion are distinct first-passage events), and a paired stellar-evolution counterfactual exhibiting a longer first-passage time under that intervention.
