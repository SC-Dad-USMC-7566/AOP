## 1. Item-by-item verdicts (work order §6 menu)

### 1.1 The four-pillars framework as a rival definition — **add as a boxed rival lens** (pre-rung ch. 7)

**Seam:** PrimeDraft v0.7, ch. 7 "What can persist before life," at the paragraph: *"These cases defeat a tempting definition: life is not simply whatever lasts, copies a shape, dissipates energy, or looks complicated. Every single-feature definition captures too much. The origin problem concerns a coupling of capacities that separately existed earlier. [Synthesis]"*

**Why here:** this paragraph refuses single-feature definitions; Bartlett–Wong is the strongest published *multi-feature process* definition, and the draft currently argues against a weaker opponent than actually exists. A boxed rival lens strengthens the chapter by steelmanning. (Scope note: "rival" here means rival to the *definition-move* this paragraph refuses — not rival to the Ladder's threshold, which asks a different, diachronic question; see §2.)

**Candidate boxed prose (for the chat seat to grade and place; drafted at [Synthesis] with anchors graded inline):**
> **Box: the four-pillars rival.** The strongest process-based definition on offer is Bartlett & Wong's (2020) "lyfe": any system exhibiting all four of dissipation, autocatalysis, homeostasis, and learning [Settled as to what the paper claims]. It shares this chapter's premise — processes, not components, are the right currency — and its comparative zoo (flames, reaction–diffusion spots, neural networks) overlaps this catalog. It fails where this chapter's discipline bites: its autocatalysis is stipulated by outcome (exponential growth) rather than mechanism, excluding the canonical non-enzymatic autocatalysts in the regimes where they run (parabolic growth from product inhibition) [Settled: growth laws; Synthesis: the definitional adjudication]; its homeostasis is stretched to cover passive relaxation, forfeiting the regulation/stability distinction that separates a flame from a cell [physiology core Settled; the architecture triple is the organizational school's proposal, Synthesis]; and its learning pillar quantifies over survival and proliferation — a diachronic criterion smuggled into a synchronic checklist [Synthesis]. The lesson this draft takes: multi-feature checklists inherit the failures of their weakest feature definitions; the coupling of capacities, not the count of checkboxes, is where the origin problem lives. [Synthesis]

**Counterargument:** a box lengthens an already long chapter, and engaging a 2020 vocabulary proposal may date the manuscript. Mitigation: the box earns its place only if kept under ~200 words; otherwise **retain as source-library material only**.

### 1.2 The sublyfe zoo cases (BZ oscillator, Gray–Scott/Lee self-replicating spots, chemotactic/maze droplets, sheared-suspension memory) — **incorporate now in next numbered draft** (pre-rung ch. 7 catalog), with corrected attributions

**Seam:** ch. 7's nonliving-persister catalog (crystal — flame — vortex — mineral pore — autocatalytic set). The catalog currently has no chemical-pattern case between "flame" and "autocatalytic set," and no material-memory case at all.

**Recommendation:** add two catalog entries, graded and sourced from D1:
> **The self-replicating spot.** In the Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion regime, chemical spots grow to a critical size and divide, and the laboratory version (ferrocyanide–iodate–sulfite) shows "birth through replication and death through overcrowding" [Settled result; primary bodies abstract-verified in this audit (Pearson 1993; Lee et al. 1994) — full-text check required before manuscript use]. The resemblance to dividing cells is visual, not mechanical: there is no heredity beyond the parents' chemical species, and no variation worth the name — replication without a record. In simulations that couple two spot populations thermally, one exothermic and one endothermic, the pair can hold internal temperature against boundary perturbations [Frontier: single-group, simulation-only — Bartlett & Bullock 2016; Bartlett 2014, whose own thesis declines to call it true homeostasis]. What the spot shows is how much of the *look* of life exists with none of the ratchet's three relations closed. [Synthesis]
>
> **The trained material.** A cyclically sheared suspension stores the amplitudes it was driven at and returns them on readout — multiple transient memories, stabilized by noise [Settled: Keim et al. RMP 2019, full-text verified; the 2011 primary abstract-verified]. A droplet of oil in an alkaline maze follows the pH gradient to the exit by the shortest path [Settled: Lagzi et al. 2010]. Both are memory, and neither is learning: the suspension's record is imposed, not used; the droplet's "solution" is gradient-following with no record at all. The pre-rung lesson: memory is cheap, readers of memory are not. [Synthesis]

**Attribution discipline:** if droplets enter, cite **Lagzi et al. 2010 (Grzybowski lab)** for maze-solving; the episode's Hanczyc attribution is incorrect (D2-C15).

**Counterargument:** ch. 7's catalog is deliberately spare; two additions risk zoo-creep. If Prime prefers one, the trained-material entry does more new work (the draft has no material-memory case; it already has flame/vortex dissipative cases).

### 1.3 The "cooperation barrier" / parasite material (episode C04, C06) — **reject or correct**

The episode's claim that parasites cause complexity saturation in digital evolution is **contradicted by the primary literature** (Ray 1991; Zaman et al. 2014 — parasites *promote* complexity; D2-C04). It must not enter the Ladder in the episode's form. The correct import, if any, is D2-C04's properly scoped correction — in the flagship digital-evolution studies, parasites promoted rather than capped complexity — which is Ladder-relevant (the charter demands explicit attention to parasitism) but belongs to a later rung's treatment of coevolution, not the pre-rung. (Guardrail note: this is a report of two specific results, not a directional law about parasitism and complexity; the no-monotonic-complexity guardrail applies to corrections too.) **Verdict: reject the episode version; park the Zaman result as source-library material for the coevolution rung.**

### 1.8 Chemical associative learning (Bartlett & Louapre 2022) — **park as frontier** (Rung 1 v0.5 competence table contact)

Rung 1 v0.5's competence ladder (levels 0–4: reactive coupling → viability-relative responsiveness → regulatory competence → learning → model-based control) currently rules learning "not licensed" for chemotaxis. Bartlett & Louapre 2022 is the first constructive demonstration that a *hand-designed* minimal chemistry can sit at level 3 (associative learning with survival advantage) — simulation-only, non-emergent. That makes it the natural **boundary marker** for the level-2/level-3 line: what it takes, and that it has not been observed to *emerge*. **Verdict: park as frontier; candidate footnote to the competence table, pending the two-stacks ruling** (learning sits adjacent to the PROPOSED competence coordinate, so any promotion inherits that proposal's unresolved status).
