## Part I — Red-team report (verbatim, independent seat, 2026-08-13/14)

**Verification basis:** all three targets read in full; D1/D2 read as reference; B&W 2020 full text, Mindscape transcript, AOP master v1.27-swept, and all Ladder files checked locally; 9 load-bearing external citations spot-verified by fetch (Blokhuis 2020, Sakref & Rivoire 2024, Zaman 2014, USFS FEIS, Mills/Spiegelman 1967, Hwang & Karplus 2019, Bartlett & Louapre 2022, Jackson & Criado-Perez 2024, Schulze-Makuch & Irwin 2001; Lynch 2025 attempted, blocked).

### Findings

**1. MAJOR — D3 §2 Q5 (and D4 §1.1 box prose): the "ideal conditions hedge fails" argument is undercut by its own FULL TEXT source.** D3's strongest sentence against pillar 2 — "The 'ideal conditions' hedge fails because product inhibition is intrinsic to template chemistry, not an environmental imperfection" — is graded on Sakref & Rivoire 2024. I fetched that paper. It says sub-exponential growth is *typical* and originates in product inhibition, but it also says, verbatim: "exponentiality is not an intrinsic property of an autocatalyst, but crucially depends on **extrinsic conditions**," and "simple and generic exponential autocatalysts are designable." A B&W defender quotes those two sentences and the hedge survives: "ideal conditions" is exactly an extrinsic-conditions qualifier. The Blokhuis half of the attack (stoichiometric definition, five cores, no growth-law axiom — all verified) stands; the intrinsicness half, which is what kills the hedge, is contradicted at the source. This sentence is propagated into D4's candidate boxed prose at [Settled]. *Repair:* narrow to "exponential growth is non-generic in template chemistry and the definition classifies by outcome, not mechanism"; delete "the hedge fails," or argue it explicitly against S&R's extrinsic-conditions sentence.

**2. MAJOR — D5 §2.2: "independently draws the same line" is circular corroboration.** D5 writes: "The published regulation literature (Bich et al. 2015…) **independently** draws the same line AOP draws." But the master's own changelog (v1.14 entry) states the living-threshold/§11a architecture "rests on settled control theory (Francis & Wonham 1976; **Bich et al. 2015**)." AOP took the line *from* Bich; citing Bich as independent convergence is the framework grading its own source as its own confirmation — precisely the nobody-grades-their-own-homework failure the charter names. *Repair:* replace "independently" with "is the shared source of"; if independence is wanted, anchor it outside the Bich/Mossio lineage (e.g., the internal-model principle line, or physiology-side treatments of homeostasis-as-active-regulation).

**3. MAJOR — D5 §2.3 / checklist 13(b): "decomposes without remainder" has (at least) three remainders.** (i) B&W's outcome clause quantifies over *the system's actual* probability of surviving/proliferating — un-relativized. AOP v1.26 deliberately re-states all semantic claims "relative to a declared persistence criterion rather than to 'the system's own viability'" (masthead, verified). Mapping B&W's absolute clause onto AOP's declared-V mask is a *substitution* of frameworks, not an expression of one in the other; the remainder is exactly the absolute/declared gap. (ii) B&W's definition has three verbs — record, **process**, act — and D5's decomposition names AOP targets for record (E) and use (mask) only; information *processing* is neither storage nor viability-weighted coupling, and it is silently absorbed. (iii) The master's §13 concedes the per-edge mask "does not reach the strongly integrated regime (the regulatory architectures of §11a…) it was built to describe." Learning systems are strongly integrated; so the "present-tense residue is fully expressible in AOP today" only at coalition-aggregate granularity. D5 carries this caveat in §3.1 but the §2.3 verdict sentence — the one that will be quoted — does not. *Repair:* restate as "decomposes under two declared substitutions (declared-V for absolute survival; coalition for per-edge) with the processing clause absorbed into the mask's causal path — a faithful but lossy translation," and move the §3.1 caveat into §2.3.

**4. MAJOR — D3 executive verdict / checklist 13(a): "asserted, never argued" overreaches, and the sufficiency counterexample is weak.** The paper does argue necessity informally for two pillars: dissipation ("the dissipation of free energy is **certainly the first necessary aspect** of life," from the second law + transduction, ¶ before the definitions) and homeostasis ("In a dynamic world of perturbations, coupled with the exponential growth described above, a lyving system **must have means** to limit the variation…"). What is genuinely absent is any argument for autocatalysis/learning necessity, any independence proof, and any completeness argument — and note the asymmetry D3 misses: *sufficiency for lyfe* is a stipulation of their own coinage (not counterexampleable), while *necessity for life* ("life must be a subset of lyfe") is the substantive exposed claim — which is why D3's best weapon is dormancy (necessity), and why its thermostat-factory sufficiency counterexample is soft (a factory's output is linear, not exponential; "arguably… under generous readings" concedes the point). *Repair:* narrow the verdict to "sufficiency stipulative; necessity informally motivated for two pillars, unargued for two; independence and completeness never addressed" — this is still fatal for a "necessary and sufficient" claim and is now quote-proof.

**5. MINOR — D2/D3 defect-roster inconsistency (checklist 12).** D2's inline numbering calls C27 (rTCA) the "**Fourth clear defect**" and C32 the fifth; but D2 §5's closing list and D3's "wrong in six specific, checkable places" both list {C04, C08, C20, C22, C28, C32} — silently dropping C27 and substituting C22. C15 (misattribution, verdict "misattributed") and C25 ("incorrect spectral detail") are also uncounted "incorrect" verdicts. The six-count is defensible as a flagship list but is not what D2's own body tallies. *Repair:* reconcile ("six flagship errors plus three lesser: C15, C25, C27").

**6. MINOR — access-level discipline breaches in quoted/graded material.** (a) D3 §6 quotes Lynch 2025 as "unsubstantiated speculation" while D1 records FB-7 at ABSTRACT/METADATA only; I also could not retrieve the abstract (PNAS/PubMed blocked) — either the phrase is abstract-recoverable (state so) or the quotation marks violate D1's own no-reporting-above-access-level rule. (b) D4 §1.5's candidate manuscript sentence grades Mizuuchi et al. 2022 **[Settled]**; D1 records it ABSTRACT ONLY — and a single 2022 study is not "Settled" by this project's standards regardless. (c) D4 §1.2's candidate prose grades Pearson 1993, Lee et al. 1994, and Keim & Nagel 2011 [Settled]; all three are ABSTRACT ONLY in D1, with no point-of-use downgrade — the rule D1 §4 states for ▲ sources.

**7. MINOR — one-school reliance on regulation (checklist 8).** The homeostasis critique is graded "Settled counter-evidence in the regulation literature" on essentially one primary: Bich/Mossio/Ruiz-Mirazo/Moreno 2015, the organizational-autonomy school. The coarse claim (physiological homeostasis is active) is safely settled; the specific machinery doing D3/D5's work — the constitutive-stability vs *dynamically decoupled second-order* regulation triple — is that school's proposal and belongs at Synthesis. (Kin selection and Gaia, probed for the same failure: held — both-sides sources are present, and D2-C08's corrected formulation is robust under the Nowak side too.)

**8. MINOR — D5 §5's headline "no conflicts — none found" is near-tautological (checklist 13c).** "Read at its verified strength" down-weights exactly the B&W claims most capable of conflicting (necessary-and-sufficient; the region-8 exponential-growth+homeostasis claim D1 itself flags as unverified in B&W's own refs) before the comparison runs. And pressure point (a) — B&W's dissipation-as-pro-life vs the master's computed direct anti-persistence leverage of a pure current (§13, verified verbatim) — is as close to a substantive disagreement as a rival vocabulary can produce; filing it as "framing" softens it. *Repair:* retitle "no contradictions at verified strength; one substantive sign disagreement; two open-problem collisions."

**9. MINOR — D5 benchmark case 1 conflates temporal windows (checklists 4, 6, 7).** The case is named "Ideal gas **relaxing**," yet the AOP expectation is σ=0 and the B&W expectation region 2. The suite's declaration discipline should catch this in its own flagship anti-B&W case.

Both are defensible (the box rivals the refused definition-move, not the Ladder's threshold), but the memo should say so or a hostile reader will play the two sentences against each other.

### Overall verdict

This package would substantially survive hostile expert review: its factual reporting of the B&W paper is the most accurate part of it (every quote I pulled was verbatim), its citation hygiene is far above field norm, and its two structural theses — the pillar/axis non-equivalence map and the scope-wall diagnosis of the learning pillar — are sound. All four are repairable by narrowing sentences, not by redoing work; none of the underlying results falls. Fix findings 1–4 and reconcile 5–6 before anything from D3–D5 is promoted into canon, boxed prose, or the LifeDef matrix; the remaining items are polish.
