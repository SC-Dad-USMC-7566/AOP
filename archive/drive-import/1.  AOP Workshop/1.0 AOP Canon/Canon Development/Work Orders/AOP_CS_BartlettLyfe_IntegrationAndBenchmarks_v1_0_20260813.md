## 1. What is being compared

B&W classify systems by a four-item checklist of *processes* (dissipation, autocatalysis, homeostasis, learning), evaluated at a system boundary the analyst chooses, with lyfe = all four. AOP profiles persisters along four *measured, declaration-relative axes* (Boundary, Drive, Memory, Integration — each a relative-entropy quantity, §2), adds a semantic layer (which couplings causally support viability under a declared functional V, §3/§7), and refuses to define life in the core (§11a: the candidate regulatory architecture is "deliberately not named alive here"). The deep formal kinship: **both frameworks are boundary/declaration-relative, and B&W say so informally while AOP formalizes it** (declaration tuple **D**, §12″). The deep formal difference: B&W's pillars are *binary predicates bundling mechanism and outcome*; AOP's axes are *graded quantities with the outcome (viability) held in a separate, declared slot*.

## 2. The non-equivalence map (work order §7, tested contact by contact)

### 2.1 Dissipation ↔ Drive — closest contact; fails in three places

Contact: B&W's dissipation ("cannot exist at equilibrium" + transduction mechanisms) and AOP's Drive (entropy-production rate σ as forward/reverse trajectory divergence) point at the same physics. Failures: **(a) Binary vs graded and convention-relative.** B&W's pillar is a yes/no on the state; AOP's σ is a rate whose value (including whether it is zero) depends on the declared variables, grain, and time-reversal convention R (§2). A system B&W call "dissipative" simpliciter can have σ = 0 under one admissible R — the pillar is not well-defined until B&W's missing declaration apparatus is supplied. **(b) Sign discipline.** B&W treat dissipation as unambiguously pro-life. AOP's computed results cut the other way in the declared regime: a pure divergence-free current *reduces* mean first-passage lifetime (~5.7× faster escape on the driven ring), and a positive pro-persistence role for Drive is "conditional or mediated and is not established" (§13). Any import of "dissipation as pillar of persistence" into AOP would contradict a deposited result. **(c) Direction of the D-contingency.** B&W make autocatalysis and learning contingent on dissipation (Venn topology). AOP's only forced theorem-grade edge runs the other way: σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0 — Drive forces strict positivity of predictive memory under four scope conditions (§4; the v1.27 sweep's own language). These are different claims with different quantifiers; neither implies the other. **Verdict: analogous target, non-equivalent formalization; the mapping is a motivation, not a translation.**

### 2.2 Homeostasis ↔ active regulation — B&W's single pillar spans three AOP-distinguished architectures

AOP §11a separates: (i) no restoring force (flame); (ii) restoring force whose set-point *is* a fixed point of the constitutive dynamics — corrects, but stores no target (star); (iii) a **dynamically decoupled, separately interventable regulatory reference** — a set-point held in one place, read out onto another, corruptible independently (cell); invariantly, a proper invariant subspace autonomous with respect to the regulated coordinates. [SYNTHESIS in canon.] B&W's homeostasis pillar covers (ii) and (iii) indiscriminately and — via the equilibrium-gas region — extends below (i) to *passive Lyapunov stability with no drive at all*, a fourth, weaker class AOP does not even call maintenance. The regulation literature (Bich et al. 2015, FULL TEXT, D1 §4.2) draws the same line — and is **the shared source lineage, not independent convergence**: AOP's §11a architecture rests on the control-theory line that includes Bich et al. (per the canon's own changelog). Independent corroboration would have to come from outside the organizational school (e.g., the internal-model principle in control theory); that check has not been run here and is flagged as open. **Consequence:** B&W's pillar cannot express the one distinction (§11a's class iii) that AOP's mask is built to detect — a load-bearing internal coupling from a slow reference onto fast regulated dynamics. **Verdict: homeostasis maps onto no AOP axis; it decomposes into an architecture fact about couplings, which AOP holds in the semantic layer, not on an axis. B&W's own caveat paragraph (Landauer) concedes the instability of their extension.**

### 2.3 Learning ↔ Memory + control + outcome — the pillar dismembers along AOP's three-layer split

B&W's learning = record + process + use-such-that-survival/proliferation-benefits. AOP separates: **(a) record** = Memory, E = I(past;future) — pure syntax, no semantics (§2); **(b) use for viability** = the semantic mask — a correlation whose scrambling drops the declared viability functional V (§3, §7; three-place: edge, viable set, V). This half of B&W's definition is *literally* Kolchinsky–Wolpert semantic information — the same construction AOP §3 extends — so on this clause the two frameworks share an ancestor, and the comparison is exact rather than analogical; **(c) survival/*proliferation*** — proliferation is differential continuation across generations, which §9 places **above the scope wall**: "propagation as differential continuation is thereby placed above AOP… a cross-project (Ladder) propagation-bus item, not a fifth dimension." And B&W's naming of *Darwinian evolution as a learning process* is wholly diachronic. **Answer to the work order's scope-wall question: yes — B&W's learning pillar crosses the present-tense wall**, in its outcome clause and in its flagship example. **Verdict: decomposes into Memory (syntactic) + semantic mask (present-tense) + an above-wall evolutionary clause AOP refuses by design — a faithful but lossy translation, under two declared substitutions and one absorption.** The substitutions: (i) B&W's outcome clause quantifies over the system's *actual* survival probability, un-relativized; AOP re-states every semantic claim relative to a *declared* persistence criterion (v1.26 remediation) — the absolute/declared gap is a real remainder, and mapping across it substitutes AOP's framework for B&W's rather than expressing one in the other. (ii) The mask's per-edge reading does not reach the strongly integrated regime learning systems occupy (§13); the present-tense residue is expressible today only at coalition granularity. The absorption: B&W's middle verb, *process*, is neither storage nor viability-weighted coupling; it is absorbed into the mask's causal path rather than separately represented.

### 2.4 Autocatalysis ↔ no single axis — confirmed, with a sharper statement than the work order anticipated

Autocatalysis is a *mechanism-class* (network stoichiometry admitting collective self-amplification — Blokhuis et al. 2020), not an axis value. Three AOP readings, none an equivalence: (a) at the **individual-system declaration**, an autocatalytic network inside the boundary appears as couplings whose semantic weight the mask can score — mechanism, not axis; (b) at the **population declaration**, exponential growth is a property of a *lineage-level* object; §9's "higher-order individual" route would read net reproduction as a lineage-scale Drive, but canon marks that route as available in principle only and grades it FRONTIER (§9) — so B&W's pillar 2, read at the scale they themselves recommend (system/ecosystem), lands on an AOP axis reading that canon has deliberately not licensed yet; (c) via the persistence primitive: §13's occupancy result — only mechanisms that reshape the stationary distribution (an equilibrium wall, or a **reset into a metastable well**) raise occupancy — is the closest AOP-native formalization of what replication *does* for persistence, and it is indifferent to whether the reset mechanism is autocatalytic. **Hidden fifth dimension? No** — but with a caveat canon should keep visible: if one *did* want growth-of-instances as a first-class quantity, AOP's architecture forces it to enter as a new declared object (a lineage-level persister), not as a new axis; the wall, not the axis count, is what does the refusing. **Verdict: no single-axis mapping; the pillar is either a mechanism (inside the wall) or a lineage fact (above it).**

## 3. The required AOP questions (work order §7)

**3.1 Can current AOP distinguish a readable historical trace from information used in counterfactual-sensitive control?** Yes — this is precisely the syntactic/semantic split. Trace: E > 0 (Memory axis). Used-in-control: nonzero semantic weight under the declared V, via the internal-edge intervention protocol (§3; six admissibility declarations). The trained sheared suspension is the clean benchmark case: E > 0 by construction, expected semantic weight ≈ 0 (no coupling from the stored amplitudes onto anything that maintains viability under the declared persistence criterion for the suspension). Caveat honestly: per-edge weights are defined only in the measured weak-integration band; the coalition object is the default (§3, §13).

**3.2 Can it distinguish passive return from actively maintained regulation without importing biological purpose?** Yes — twice over: σ-cost at the interface (passive interfaces cost nothing, active ones are paid; §8), and the §11a architecture triple, stated as coupling facts and an invariant-subspace criterion, no teleology needed. This is a place where AOP is strictly finer-grained than B&W (D3 §2 Q6).

**3.3 Does Bartlett's learning definition cross the scope wall?** Yes (§2.3 above). The audit point for canon: AOP's refusal is *load-bearing* here, and the B&W collision is a good external illustration of why the wall exists — bundling proliferation into a synchronic-sounding predicate is exactly the failure mode §9 is built to prevent.

**3.4 Does autocatalysis introduce a hidden fifth dimension?** No (§2.4). It remains an external causal mechanism (or an above-wall lineage fact).

**3.5 How do nested declarations change the profile for virion/host, worker/colony, organism/ecosystem?** This is where B&W and AOP meet on AOP's own open ground. B&W's virus resolution — a virion alone performs no pillar; virus+host performs two; biosphere-embedded performs more — is, in AOP terms, the observation that **profiles are functions of the declaration**, which AOP states exactly (§2, §7) and B&W state informally. But B&W then *recommend* the ecosystem/planetary declaration as the right one, and AOP currently **cannot adjudicate that recommendation**: level selection across a nested hierarchy is an OPEN item — the v1.22 retraction stands, "the level-selection half of the F2 seam is open, not closed" (§13a), with four required declarations not yet supplied. Honest statement for the record: *AOP formalizes the dependence B&W gesture at, and inherits an open problem exactly where B&W place their strongest claim.* The nested cases (virion/host, worker/colony, organism/ecosystem) are therefore **benchmark declarations, not adjudications** — each level gets its own declared profile; no level is crowned. The §9a two-level reading (self-maintenance inside the wall; private reproductive stake above it) supplies the worker/colony split without group-selection metaphysics — and note D2-C08: the episode's "genes get nothing" framing is exactly what §9a's separation avoids.

**3.6 Do AOP life-threshold claims overlap or conflict with Bartlett's functional criteria?** No conflict, by construction: the core canon *refuses* the life predicate (§11a: "deliberately not named alive here"), so there is no AOP life-definition for the four pillars to contradict. Overlap exists in the follow-on arc: the LifeDef adjudication work order's rival-criteria matrix (NASA/Joyce, autopoiesis, chemoton, FEP, assembly theory per the CW verification report) **does not currently include Bartlett–Wong 2020 as a rival criterion — it should** (proposal §6.1). Note also the convergent finding: the LifeDef report's NASA-wording caveat and this audit's D2-C01 arrived at the same two-variant provenance problem independently.

**3.7 Can the hostile-case panel become an AOP benchmark suite with preregistered declarations and expected failure modes?** Yes — §4 below drafts it in the intervention-contract v0.2.1 idiom (every benchmark a complete declared world; typed contrasts; identifiability separated from admissibility).

**3.8 Does any apparent support for AOP here depend on a Ladder inference?** Checked: no. Every AOP-side statement above rests on canon sections or on externally verified literature (Bich et al.; Kolchinsky–Wolpert lineage). The one place a Ladder object was tempting — using the three-switch ratchet to explain what B&W's learning pillar is missing — was excluded; the scope-wall argument is made entirely from §9. **Nothing removed because nothing was admitted.**
