B* 2019" does not exist.** Confirmed independently by this seat: PubMed `Durant F[au] AND Levin M[au]` returns 7 records, **none** in that journal in any year; the sole Levin paper in *Phil. Trans. 4. **The Gánti priority claim's second half misreads the potential criteria** — they concern "the surviving of the living world," not founding a lineage, and include mortality.
5. **Abrahão et al. is given as "1:1–20"**; the journal's own locator is **e0000014**. Marshall et al. is given as "et al." where the full 11-author byline is available and was verified.

## 7 · A process defect in this seat's own work

On the bioelectric locus, this seat's retrieval track **constructed a DOI by pattern** from the order's journal-and-year string and fetched it. It resolved to a real but unrelated paper on mosquito-borne disease. Had the returned title not been checked, a fabricated-in-effect citation could have entered a deliverable under Durant's name.

**This is the same mechanism behind the two fabricated bylines this project has already caught, and it originates in identifier construction, not in prose invention.**

**Recommended standing rule:** DOIs enter this project's documents only from a resolver query (CrossRef / PubMed / Europe PMC) keyed on author and title, **and the title returned by the resolver is checked against the title sought.** Applied for the remainder of this arc.

A second defect, in the deliverables themselves: **the verdict matrix's §6 summary table contradicted the per-case data in its own document** — two tally errors, found by review rather than by the author, corrected in Corrigendum 1. The failure mode is worth naming for CW: **the totals summed correctly while the individual counts were wrong.** A verification pass that checks only whether a distribution sums to the row count will pass this error; only re-parsing the column catches it.
