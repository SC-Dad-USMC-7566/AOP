## What is being tested, and what is not

The six-part conjunction as written is a definition. Definitions do not fail. This arc therefore does **not** test whether AOP has the right definition of life. It tests **consequences the decoupled-reference architecture must have if the criterion tracks something real.**

---

## P1 — Competent misregulation exists

**The prediction.** A system holding a genuinely decoupled reference has a failure mode a model-free corrector cannot have: corrupt the stored set-point without damaging the regulatory machinery, and the system regulates *competently toward the wrong target*. A star can only be degraded — its target is not stored anywhere separable, so there is nothing to move. Prediction: for any system the criterion calls alive, such an intervention exists within the declared intervention class.

| Outcome | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **KILLS** | A bounded, pre-declared search on a paradigm living system finds **no** intervention producing intact, precise regulation toward a wrong target. **Or:** a system the criterion excludes is shown to have one. |
| **NARROWS** | Competent misregulation is found, but only via operations **outside** the declared intervention class **I**. This shrinks third-person detectability without touching the architectural claim, and enters as a scope condition on **I**. |
| **UNINFORMATIVE** | The declared search bound was too tight to have found an intervention if one existed; **or** *competent* and *degraded* regulation cannot be cleanly separated on the available data. |

## P2 — No knee

**The prediction.** The discrimination is claimed to be architectural, not a magnitude of timescale separation. On a real system, the verdict holds flat across orders of magnitude of the slow/fast ratio, with no threshold.

| Outcome | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **KILLS** | A reproducible threshold in the slow/fast ratio at which the verdict flips. **This is fatal on contact.** It would establish that the discrimination is a timescale magnitude wearing architectural clothes — precisely what the follow-on denies, and what Figure LT-T was run to exclude. |
| **NARROWS** | Flat, but over a smaller range than claimed. Enters as a declared scope condition on the ratio. |
| **UNINFORMATIVE** | The achievable range on the chosen system is too narrow for a knee to have been visible had one existed. |

## P3 — The architecture buys lifetime

**The prediction.** AOP's persistence primitive is lifetime — mean first-passage time out of the viable set. Under a declared perturbation class, systems holding the architecture show longer lifetime than matched systems without it.

**This prediction cannot kill the criterion, and that is stated here so it is not misread later.** It tests the architecture's *relevance to AOP*, not its correctness.

| Outcome | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **NULL** | The architecture is severed from AOP's primitive. The life material stays in the follow-on **permanently** and never folds into the core. The criterion survives; its claim on AOP does not. |
| **WRONG SIGN** | Decoupled regulation systematically *shortening* lifetime under the declared perturbations is a distinct and more interesting result. It gets its own ruling at the time, and is not pre-dispositioned here. |
| **CONFIRMS** | Grounds to reopen the question of folding the architecture into the core. Grounds only — not authorization. |
| **UNINFORMATIVE** | No matched comparison class could be constructed on the chosen system. |
