## 2. AOP's position, as currently defensible

The functional turn, substrate generality, gradedness, refusal of a bright line, four-fold decomposition, interventional measurement, the refusal to individuate, and a viability anchor are **all published.** Bartlett has executed a single-axis measurement discipline with a negative control and reported sensitivities. K&W have executed a single-axis interventional test anchored to a viability function. Smith & Morowitz have the refusal and a planetary viability domain.

**What remains undone:** run an interventional, viability-anchored measurement across four axes on one declaration-bounded system over one declared interval, and report how much the reading moves when the declaration changes.

Three components, none individually novel, in a combination not yet assembled: **four axes at once, intervention rather than observation, and a declaration-sensitivity audit.** This is a methods contribution. It requires no novel law and no novel prediction.

**Do not undersell it.** Bartlett could not separate biology from cloud physics *because he had no intervention* — a named failure in a 2022 *Nature Astronomy* paper that this method addresses directly. **Do not oversell it either.** Whether the combination survives §7's prior-art sweep is unknown, and the sweep runs before anything else for that reason.

**The cost, stated up front:** cross-system comparison now requires harmonised protocol, grain, declaration slots, and normaliser. The reach that made "a cell, a flame, and a star" appealing is reduced. That trade is the paper's central argument and must be made explicitly.

## 3. The scope wall: typological, not phylogenetic

**3.1 Framing, binding on all seats.** Retire *synchronic.* Retire *no lineage* as the primary framing. The wall is now stated in the field's own vocabulary, from Smith & Morowitz §2.3.1: **AOP is a typological framework, not a phylogenetic one.** It classifies by convergence and constraint rather than by divergence under descent. Smith & Morowitz establish that typological schemes are required for systems that do not record their divergences in branching trees. AOP inherits that justification rather than building one.

Operational formulation, unchanged in substance: *AOP assesses one declaration-bounded system over one declared interval, without appeal to its ancestors or its descendants.* The wall is against ancestry and descent, **not against time** — K&W's protocol carries a timescale τ and P3 predicts lifetime. Never write "no time."

**3.2 Four grounds, kept distinct because their strengths differ.**

- **Evidential.** A lineage criterion cannot be evaluated on one specimen over one interval; it converts a test into a bet. **Source:** B&W separate a pillar's significance from ease of measurement and concede exoplanet biosignatures will not reveal autocatalysis except over very long periods. The framework that *keeps* the criterion admits it cannot be run.
- **Underdetermination.** Even given a lineage, the history is not recoverable. **Source:** B&W's event horizon — top-down inference reaches only LUCA; the extant tree is compatible with multiple geneses, extinct lineages, a shadow biosphere, rampant horizontal transfer, LUCA-as-a-set.
- **Individuation dependency — the internal argument.** A Darwinian criterion presupposes countable individuals with heritable variation in fitness. AOP declines to individuate. Importing a lineage criterion contradicts a prior commitment; the exclusion is *forced*. **Sources, two fields:** Smith & Morowitz — evolution depends on the prior organisation of matter into modes of individuality, a hard problem, so evolution sits inside a wider class of order-forming processes rather than at the foundation. Bouchard — individuation breaks down for evolutionary theory on its own terms (integrated clonal genets, colonies, ecosystems); he calls it metaphysical and unsolved.
- **Category.** Persistence and reproduction come apart. **Sources:** Smith & Morowitz §2.2.2 — ecosystems do not persist through replication or anything reasonably called reproduction, and variant configurations do not undergo Darwinian selection for want of a population. Bouchard — Pando as worked case. Van Valen 1989.

**3.3 What Bouchard does NOT support.** He is not excluding the diachronic; his title is differential persistence *of lineages*, and he is repairing evolutionary theory from inside. Citing him as an ally in the exclusion is a free hit for any philosophy-of-biology referee. Cite him for: reproduction is the wrong currency, persistence is the right one, individuation is where the machinery breaks.

**3.4 A Boundary bonus.** Via Bouchard's use of Turner's *The Extended Organism*: an evolving individual may be partly non-biological and physiology extends beyond any material envelope (termite mound as external organ). Cuts against material-interface definitions of Boundary. Belongs in §8 of the draft as well as here.

**3.5 The cost of the wall, in the same paragraph as the benefit.** AOP cannot speak to open-endedness, cannot track complexity growth, cannot distinguish architecture from luck across generations. That is Bartlett's territory. The wall buys a test runnable on one specimen and pays in reach.

**3.6 Framing prohibitions.** Do not write that AOP "independently confirmed" any of this — arriving before finding the literature buys confidence, not credit, and in a paper it reads as a priority claim. Do not claim the refusal to individuate as AOP's own; Smith & Morowitz published it in 2016 in a stronger form. Do not issue bright-line verdicts ("the pre-biotic planet was not alive"); AOP produces axis readings under a declaration, never verdicts. **This last is the most likely way the framework gets undermined by its own author.**

**3.7 Metabolism-first disclaimer, mandatory.** Smith & Morowitz's prebiotic chemistry is contested. Orgel 2000 (*PNAS* 97:12503) argues the Beilstein data claimed to support a self-organised rTCA cycle admits another reading and places the burden of proof on the proposers — aimed at Morowitz's own work. Orgel 2008 (*PLoS Biol.* 6:e18) argues the rTCA needs at least six distinct catalytic activities with substrate specificity unlikely to arise abiotically. Ross 2007 argues from kinetics and thermochemistry that it cannot run. The field has since demonstrated some non-enzymatic Krebs-like intermediates, so it is unsettled rather than refuted. **AOP cites Smith & Morowitz for the conceptual and classificatory claims only, and says so in one clause.** Without that clause a referee assumes the whole package has been swallowed and AOP inherits a fight it has no stake in. *Verification note: Orgel and Ross known to prime from search results only. Retrieve and read before the clause is written.*

## 5. Chunking

**The constraint.** A chat-style seat holds roughly four to six full papers per thread while also writing. Beyond that, quality degrades silently: later papers get skimmed, earlier findings recalled from memory rather than re-read. That is how v0.1 happened. Assume the same ceiling for Claude Science and Aster. Cowork runs more tool calls but reads under the same limit.

**The unit is one axis: three to four papers in, one section out.** One thread each.

**5.1 Read and write are separate seats.** Each axis is read by one thread producing reading notes, and written by a *different* thread working from the notes plus the sources. Reading and arguing simultaneously is how "the source supports this" becomes "the source says this" — the specific defect that produced v0.1. Slower; non-negotiable.

**5.2 The Axis Brief (blocking).** Chat seat produces a short binding document every axis thread reads first. Contains: the §3.1 typological framing; the §2 position statement; the section template (structure, headings, verification-tier notation, grading vocabulary); the commitments no axis may contradict; and these explicit prohibitions — **an axis section may not claim the four-axis protocol generalises** (that is §8's determination), and **may not claim the refusal to individuate as AOP's own** (Smith & Morowitz 2016). **Without this, chunking produces four incompatible sections and is worse than one overloaded thread.**

## 8. Two questions that must be settled

**8.1 Lineage or convergence?** v0.1 §2 claimed the functional turn was reached independently at least four times and every framework fails the same way. But Di Paolo's architecture *is* structural-criterion-fails-so-go-interventional; K&W cite Di Paolo and Ashby; Bartlett cites both. Papers citing each other in sequence will share a move. **Task (Cowork, mechanical):** extract and cross-tabulate reference lists across Maturana & Varela → Di Paolo → K&W → B&W → Bartlett 2019/2022. Until discharged, §3 of the draft says "a lineage," not "independent convergence."

**8.2 Does the intervention generalise?** K&W's scramble is well-posed because the object is a *channel* — correlation can be destroyed while marginals are preserved, giving a natural null. Ablating a Boundary or Drive mechanism changes the dynamics, not a coupling. That is knockout logic — standard and defensible in biology — but not the K&W protocol generalised. **Task (chat, Phase 5):** state per axis what the intervention is, what is held fixed, and what the null is. **Expected outcome, not a risk:** if the four are not the same operation, say so and defend a *family* of interventions united by what they report rather than by what they do. Still a real contribution; a quieter one.
