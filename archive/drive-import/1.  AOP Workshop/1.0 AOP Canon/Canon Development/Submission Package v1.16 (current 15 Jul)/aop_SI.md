## S1. The five worked cases (full)

The abstractions earn their keep on five concrete things. Four form a progression from full persistence to a bare minimum (crystal, flame, spore, bound atom); the fifth — the star — is not a point on that line but a case in a corner none of the others occupy: high Drive, high Integration, gravitationally bound. A mode of persistence is a shape across the four dimensions in semantic weight (Figure 5); a transition is a change in that shape. Two of the four axes — Drive and Memory — are computable without a declared partition; the other two — Boundary and Integration — are partition-dependent and are the softer of the four. The profiles below show all four together for readability, but the two tiers do not have equal footing, and the figure marks which is which.

Figure 5. Three ways to persist, as semantic profiles over the same four dimensions (the crystal, flame, and spore; the bound atom and the star are treated in the text and in Figure R★ rather than as bar profiles). The crystal's semantics are spent (frozen structure, no live drive); the flame's are all in drive (sharp boundary, negligible memory); the spore's are held in escrow (structure and integration stored, drive switched off). Bar heights are illustrative shape, not measured values. Boundary and Integration are partition-dependent axes and are marked as such; Drive and Memory are computable without a declared cut.

A crystal is configuration whose semantics are spent. The living thing was the growth front — drive depositing structure; once grown, drive goes to zero and only the husk remains, memory made solid. Nothing it holds bears weight for continued persistence; it is terminal. A flame is process with its semantics all in drive: a sharp boundary rebuilt each instant from current conditions, with essentially no memory. It is the physical counter-witness that keeps Boundary and Memory from being welded by law; it is also the maximally observer-relative boundary (thermal, ionization, and luminous edges all differ). A spore is capacity with its semantics in escrow: drive switched off, structure locked, viability held in reserve and re-formable when a coupling re-forms. Its coat is a lock, not a reader; the germinant is the key; a dead lock still opens to its key. This is bet-hedging made physical — stored information with zero current viability value and high conditional-future value [10] — and it is what forces the choice of Memory numerator: the excess entropy E can read near zero on a richly structured process whose stored complexity Cμ is large [13], so two systems with identical E can differ arbitrarily in stored structure, and the spore's semantics live in the latter.

A bound atom (the hydrogen atom of Figure 4) is the minimal admitted case: a screenable electromagnetic skin gives it an interior, and its binding gives it a rest frame and a present tense. It persists — briefly, modestly, really — and it marks the low end of the domain rather than a rich interior of its own.

A star is the case at the opposite corner, and it earns its place for a reason none of the other four supply: it is where the framework's own instrument is driven soft. Its four dimensions are all near maximal and all entangled. Drive is the defining axis — fusion is a coherent central entropy source resupplying, against radiative loss, the thermal pressure that balances gravity; kill it and the star dies on a thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) time. Boundary is two boundaries of different force-types, both load-bearing (Section 8): the unscreenable causal boundary from gravitational binding, and the screenable photospheric skin whose opacity sets the leak the drive works against. Integration is very high and non-separable: hydrostatic equilibrium couples every shell to the weight above and the structure below, so no single shell's coupling can be scrambled without the rest hydrostatically readjusting. Memory is left qualitative, as with the spore, but here for a reason the framework can now name precisely: it is the star that makes the time-grain relativity of Section 5 concrete. Mass and composition largely fix the track, yet the excess-entropy numerator E has no single value for a star, because E is defined only for a stationary process and a star's stationarity is clock-dependent — stationary on its thermal (Kelvin–Helmholtz) clock, manifestly non-stationary on its nuclear-evolution clock, along which composition drifts and the track moves. So the star's Memory is not merely un-asserted between E and stored complexity Cμ [13]; its E is undefined until the clock is named. This is the same observer-relativity Section 5 states for the spatial grain, now on the time axis and sharp enough to remove a magnitude rather than merely rescale it. There is a tension here worth naming rather than leaving for the reader to find: the framework's cleanest, forced claim on the Memory axis — the D→M floor σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0 — holds only for a stationary process, so on the very case the paper spends the most ink on it can be evaluated only on the thermal clock, and goes silent on the nuclear one where the star is non-stationary. The flagship sits precisely in the regime where the Memory-axis law is undefined. We take this as the point rather than an embarrassment: it is the star that shows the floor is a clock-relative statement, not a defect in the floor, and a persister whose Memory is grain-dependent is exactly the object that makes the time-grain relativity concrete instead of abstract. Two things make the star more than a fifth illustration. First, one physical property — gravitational non-additivity, the settled long-range-interaction property of Section 8 [Campa, Dauxois & Ruffo 2009] — is the common root of three of the star's features at once: gravity's anti-boundary character (no negative mass ⇒ unscreenable ⇒ no statistical boundary), the Integration floor (subsystems never fully decouple), and the star's self-regulation, because a self-gravitating system has negative specific heat (radiate energy and it gets hotter), which is the microcanonical signature of that same non-additivity. The property that denies the star a boundary is the property that stabilizes its drive; this is the framework's synthesis, built on settled components, and it is the reason the star is worth working. Second, the star makes the Section 6 resolvability limit physical rather than illustrative: because its shells are locked by hydrostatic equilibrium, it sits high on the integration axis where per-edge semantic weights blur — the mask flags the coupled opacity–gradient–generation–pressure loop as load-bearing but cannot assign per-edge weights, and gravity resists intervention entirely. Here the star's coupling is not imposed but read off its structure, which is what lets it stand as more than an illustration of Section 6. Linearizing the adiabatic pulsation equation on a Lane–Emden n=3 polytrope — the settled apparatus of stellar structure — gives a tridiagonal shell-to-shell operator, a chain with a global hydrostatic constraint, whose spectrum is a graded stiff-to-sloppy band (Figure R★). It is therefore not the mean-field equicorrelation caricature (which the family of Figure TF places as the degenerate two-level member); at matched mean inter-shell correlation the derived operator has a stiff/sloppy spread roughly an order of magnitude wider, and its per-shell attribution uncertainty is *larger* than the caricature's — the parts blur harder precisely because the coupling is the real one. Read through this derived coupling the star sits high on both blur mechanisms of Section 6 at once — per-shell weights unresolvable (inferential) and gravity resisting intervention entirely (interventional) — while the aggregate, collective mode stays sharply defined. The star therefore realizes the Section 6 resolvability limit from its own physics rather than being relabeled into it; it does not escape the mask-blur regime the framework most wants to describe, it is driven into it by the same hydrostatic coupling that holds it up. It should be said clearly what this does and does not do: any strongly, non-degenerately coupled linear system has a stiff/sloppy spectrum — that is the general content of the sloppiness picture — so a real integrated system could not have come out *not* blurred, and the star is therefore a demonstration of self-consistency (the framework says integrated systems blur; here is a real one, built from its own physics, that does), not a confirmation that could have failed. What the derivation adds over the equicorrelation caricature is not informativeness about whether the effect occurs but fidelity about its shape — a graded band rather than an imposed two-level spectrum — and the removal of the suspicion that the result was smuggled in by the model choice.

The star also sharpens a distinction the flame alone left ambiguous. Both are all-in-drive, boundary-rebuilt-continuously persisters — but the flame has no restoring force (marginally stable, at the mercy of current supply; interrupt it and it is gone with no fight), while the star has a strong one: the negative-specific-heat thermostat is a present structural property, not a bet on the future (core cools ⇒ pressure drops ⇒ contracts ⇒ virial heating ⇒ steeply temperature-sensitive fusion climbs ⇒ restores). Same drive-dominated character, opposite stability class. This also answers a Section 9 worry directly: the star's apparent goal-directedness ("keep burning") is not hindsight worn as a goal but present dynamical stability, checkable now — hydrostatic and thermal balance read on the current state — exactly as the present-tense principle demands.

These five cases are not decoration; they are the framework's primary evidence that the four-fold carving is the right one (Section 1). Each is a persister that forces two axes apart and so refutes a collapse the single-axis incumbents would make: the flame breaks the Boundary–Memory weld, exhibiting a maintained boundary with negligible memory that a memory-maximizing individuality axis [6] cannot score honestly; the spore forces the choice of Memory numerator (Cμ over E), exhibiting deep stored structure at near-zero drive that a drive- or E-centric axis reads as inert; the crystal fixes the spent-semantics pole, a structure whose semantics are all in the past; the bound atom marks the minimal admitted case at the domain's low edge; and the star occupies the high-Drive, high-Integration, gravitationally-bound corner, where it demonstrates the one thing an illustration cannot — the framework's instrument going soft in precisely the regime it was built to describe. Where the prediction posture is gone, this is what carries the paper's weight: a carving is justified by the distinctions it refuses to lose, and each case above is one such distinction made physical. A broader gallery of edge cases — a map of the ways a system can fail at persistence, including objects that fall outside the domain wall entirely (a free photon, a black hole, a meme), together with the binding/rest-frame analysis that locates that wall — is developed separately and is not required for the paper's argument.

## S2. Diachronic individuation (§4a in full)

Section 4 added a *synchronic* individuation coordinate — Φ_MIP, telling one individual from many at  
a fixed time. Persistence also poses the orthogonal, *diachronic* question: when do two time-slices  
belong to **one** persisting process? The framework's answer is not material and not descriptive. A  
persisting thing is a process — a form continuously instantiated through changing material — and it  
is individuated over time by **continuity of instantiation** — causal continuity of the process itself (*genidentity* — the term originates in early-20th-century process metaphysics, from Lewin and later Reichenbach, and long predates its use here; it is applied to biological individuals as processes by DiFrisco 2018, in Nicholson & Dupré 2018, whose in-chapter terminology is paraphrased pending full-text verification) — not by retained substance and not by a preserved description. This is a settled position imported, not a  
new invention; what is this framework's is the mapping onto the four axes and the present-tense  
principle. **[SETTLED named view; the mapping is SYNTHESIS.]**  
  
Two contrasting thought experiments fix the criterion, and their difference *is* the result. In the  
**transporter/de-extinction** case the process stops and a *description* is re-enacted elsewhere;  
the result is a look-alike, a cover version, not the resumed original — the recreated thing is a new  
process that happens to match a record. In the **Ship of Theseus** case continuity of instantiation  
holds *through total material turnover*, because the pattern is never interrupted; it is the same  
ship though it keeps no original plank. The two cases involve the *same* total substance swap; the  
only difference is whether instantiation was unbroken (Theseus, same process) or  
interrupted-and-re-enacted-from-a-record (transporter, new process). Continuity is unbroken  
instantiation, not preserved material. The verdict rests on the *physical* fact, which is
determinate: was the causal chain of instantiation interrupted? The transporter interrupts it; Theseus
does not. "Same process ≡ unbroken instantiation" is then this framework's stipulated definition, and
"the transporter makes a new process" follows *by that definition*, not by a discovery about identity.
This is consistent with the deflationary reading of personal identity on which there is "no further
fact" beyond continuity itself [Parfit 1984] — no identity fact is claimed *beyond* continuity;
continuity is defined as the fact. (Parfit's thesis is precisely that in such cases there is no
determinate further identity fact to find; the framework does not read a determinate metaphysical
verdict out of his view, it stipulates a physical criterion and reports its consequence.) This is
exactly what the genidentity criterion asserts.  
  
This gives death and its edge cases a clean reading, consistent with the present-tense principle  
(Section 9). **Death = interruption of instantiation** — not loss of material (Theseus keeps none)  
and not loss of the description (a frozen genome is intact). The mammoth species stopped being  
sailed when the last individual died; its recoverable genome is a description, not a paused process,  
so de-extinction would begin a new process, not resume the old. The operational test is present-  
tense and needs no foresight: does the system restart *itself* from its own held state (spore →  
paused, same process) or must something external rebuild it from a record (mammoth → new process)?  
This is why *paused ≠ stopped* and *held-state ≠ description*. It also secures why death must be  
real: if "the blueprint still exists, so it persists" were admitted, nothing would ever die and  
persistence would mean nothing. **[SYNTHESIS.]**  
  
**Fission is the honest hard case, and it is not new either.** One continuously-instantiated lineage  
that splits into two leaves both descendants genidentity-legitimate; asking "which is the original?"  
has no unique answer, exactly as in the fission cases of the personal-identity literature [Parfit  
1984]. Diachronic identity is therefore not always one-to-one, and the framework does not force it to  
be: speciation is a branching of one process into two, both continuations, neither uniquely the  
original. This is a property of the criterion, marked, not a defect to be patched. **[SYNTHESIS;  
FRONTIER at the formal treatment of branching.]**

**The gate ledger: pre-registered cross-brick tests.** The framework's forced spokes are few because the search for more was run explicitly and came back negative. Four pre-registered cross-brick gates asked whether an intervention on one brick's regime provably moves another brick's admissible output *through a shared mechanism* (a genuine joint) rather than a shared input (a coincidence). All four of these returned negative, and the shared reason — the reversible/irreversible sector split — is the positive finding recorded above. The negatives are results, not gaps: each is a scoped, pre-registered boundary with a deposited verdict, and one records a spurious positive caught and retracted, which is the discipline working as intended. The ledger also records the one gate run in the opposite direction — not to break a claimed independence but to establish a proposed new axis: the individuation gate (Section 4) asked whether Φ_MIP is a genuine coordinate distinct from resolvability and Integration, and returned the register's first GO. Testing in both directions — for couplings that should not exist and for an axis that should — is the same pre-registration discipline applied symmetrically.
