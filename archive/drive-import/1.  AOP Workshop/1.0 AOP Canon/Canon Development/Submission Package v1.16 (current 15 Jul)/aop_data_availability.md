# Data Availability Statement — *The Architecture of Persistence*

All computed results and figures in this paper are reproducible from a single deposited
package, `aop_repro_package.tar.gz`, archived at **[repository DOI — to be assigned at
submission]** under an open licence (code under the MIT Licence, text and data under CC BY 4.0
unless the journal requires otherwise). The package contains short, self-contained scripts —
every computed claim is closed-form or cheap-numerical, requiring only Python (≥3.10) with
`numpy`, `scipy`, `pandas`, and `matplotlib`; no proprietary software, GPU, or network access
is needed, and each script runs in seconds.

## What the package regenerates

The package is organised into `engine/` (the resolvability-signature engine), `gates/` (the
five pre-registered cross-brick gate modules), `figures/` (one script per display item), and
`data/` (deposited verdict files and precomputed sweep arrays). Its `README.md` and `MANIFEST`
map each figure and numerical statement to the script that regenerates it and the file it
produces.

- **Resolvability engine.** `engine/aop_resolvability_engine.py` takes any coupling Laplacian
  to its signature — eigenspectrum, per-component variance-inflation factor (VIF),
  interventional do-edge drag, and aggregate collective-mode variance. It is validated against
  the equicorrelation closed forms (per-component uncertainty (1−ρ)^(−1/2), aggregate
  1/√λ_max) to machine precision. The **Figure R★** operator is built from a Lane–Emden *n*=3
  integration reproducing the tabulated first zero ξ₁ = 6.897 to five digits.

- **Resolvability figures.** The equicorrelation resolvability sweep (**Figure R**), the
  topology-indexed resolvability family (**Figure TF**), the stellar-structure-derived
  resolvability signature of the star (**Figure R★**), and the mask-resolvability × Integration
  trade-off are regenerated by the corresponding scripts in `figures/`.

- **Semantic mask (Figure MW).** `figures/figure_MW_worked_mask.py` applies the
  Kolchinsky–Wolpert scramble-and-re-run procedure to the internal couplings of the driven
  Markov ring of **Figure DM**, under three present-tense viability functionals (steady-state
  current, entropy production, relaxation rate), reading each edge's semantic weight as the
  fractional viability drop when that edge is scrambled to detailed balance. A detailed-balance
  negative control returns zero weight on every edge, and an inert spectator coupling returns
  exactly zero under every functional.

- **Thermodynamic figures.** The entropy-production / excess-entropy sweep of the driven
  three-state Markov ring (**Figure DM**) and the non-energy-triangle reachability scan
  (**Figure T**) are regenerated by their scripts in `figures/`; the invariant-mass check
  underlying Section 10 is an analytic statement reproduced in the same numerical setting.

- **The living threshold (Figures LT, LT-T).** `figures/figure_LT.py` and
  `figures/figure_LT_threshold.py` compute the load-bearing-∧-decoupled discriminator on two
  closed-form OU systems, correctly placing the star (corrects, model-free) outside the living
  threshold, and show the discriminator to be architectural rather than a timescale-separation
  magnitude.

- **The gate ledger (Table 4).** The four pre-registered cross-brick NULLs and the individuation
  GO are regenerated by the modules in `gates/`, each printing its pre-registered verdict.
  `gates/aop_individuation_gate.py` computes Gaussian integrated information (Φ_MIP) across the
  exhaustively-searched minimum information partition of Σ=(I+gL)⁻¹ on module-weld and
  matched-coupling-budget systems, returning exactly zero on block-decomposable systems and
  graded positive values as parts weld, and passing all five pre-registered criteria (the GO).
  The trajectory-B, trajectory-K, and E-vs-Cμ dormancy modules regenerate the three driven-regime
  NULLs together with their deposited verdict data files; the snapshot NULL is the Q=0 limit
  proven analytically in the engine.

## Figures not derived from a named system

A small number of display items are schematic or illustrative rather than measurements of a named
system, and are identified as such in their captions: the observer-grain hook figure (a schematic
computation) and the illustrative-shape figures — the four-axis profile-as-a-shape graphical
abstract and the boundary/screenability schematics. These are not the subject of the deposited
computation. The deposited computation covers the driven-ring memory floor (Figure DM), the
non-energy triangle (Figure T), the resolvability plane and star (Figures R, R★), the
topology-indexed resolvability family (Figure TF), the mask-resolvability × Integration trade-off,
the living threshold (Figures LT, LT-T — regenerated as stdout verdicts), the worked semantic mask
(Figure MW), the full gate ledger, and the Section 10 numerical statements.

Note on figure numbering: the labels above (DM, T, R, R★, TF, LT, LT-T, MW) are the canon's
working labels. The submitted manuscript renumbers the computed items into a consecutive main-text
set (Figs 1–6) plus SI figures; the package `MANIFEST` records the crosswalk. Every computed
main-text and SI figure traces to a script in this package.
