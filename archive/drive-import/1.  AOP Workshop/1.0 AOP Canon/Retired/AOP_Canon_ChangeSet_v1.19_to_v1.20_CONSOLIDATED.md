### E7 (UPDATED) · §12, Table 4, "Current → lifetime" outcome cell — replace the NEW text with this fuller, computation-backed version
**OLD (v1.19 tail):** "… Sign is geometry-set (here anti-persistent: the current stirs the system over its barrier); generalization beyond the minimal ring open"
**NEW:** "For a measure-preserving current (divergence-free, at fixed stationary distribution, with ∇U·ℓ = 0) in the small-noise limit the sign is not free but **one-sided**: circulation can only shorten or leave unchanged the mean first-passage time, never lengthen it. The escape barrier is pinned to the fixed quasipotential −ε ln ρ_s, so only the prefactor moves, and the non-reversible Eyring–Kramers analysis shows it moves one way — added circulation raises the saddle's unstable eigenvalue, enlarging the escape prefactor [Lee & Seo 2021, Lemma 3.4 & Cor. 3.9; Bouchet & Reygner 2016]. We confirm this on a genuine metastable barrier (a 2D double well, not merely the ring): the saddle's unstable eigenvalue is μ₊(A) = 1 + √(9 + 8A²), monotone increasing, giving an asymptotic ratio E[τ](A)/E_rev[τ] = 4/(1+√(9+8A²)) ≤ 1, and a deterministic first-passage PDE solve shows escape accelerating (~287×) as the current grows, never slowing (deposited). The ring's anti-persistence (5.7×) is therefore the *expected* behaviour, not a coincidence of geometry. A drive that appears to *lengthen* lifetime — a star's fusion — does not contradict this: fusion is not a measure-preserving current at fixed stationary distribution, it **reshapes the stationary state** (composition, energy content, the hydrostatic profile), so the theorem does not bind it; such a drive lengthens lifetime through the forced Boundary and Memory edges and a present dynamical restoring force (the negative-specific-heat thermostat, §11a), not through the measure-preserving lever. What Drive forces directly under the lifetime primitive is a change in lifetime; for a genuine measure-preserving current the direction is settled and downward. Generalization to finite noise, and the classification of drives that reshape the stationary state, remain open."
**WHY:** the reframe is now a confirmed computation, not just a literature citation. **[SETTLED (the one-sided rule, confirmed) + FRONTIER (finite-noise; stationary-reshaping drives).]**
