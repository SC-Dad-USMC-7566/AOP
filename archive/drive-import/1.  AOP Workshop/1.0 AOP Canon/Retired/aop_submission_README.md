## The contribution, in one place (for the red-team to attack)

**Pitch.** Persistence resolves into four distinguishable entropic axes (Boundary, Drive, Memory,
Integration) whose couplings sort by physical modality into forced, conditional, and free; energy is
the hub but not the dictator.

**Three contributions:** (1) a two-layer classification — a modal coupling graph + an interventional
semantic mask, computed on a fully-specified system; (2) a pre-registered two-exit gate discipline
applied to the framework's own predictions (four NULLs + one GO), with the negatives as first-class
results; (3) closed-form, not estimated, results throughout, shown to converge with independently
established results in other fields.

## Known soft points (stated, not hidden — the honest attack surface)

1. **F2** — the nested-level/non-stationary extension of the integration axis (Φ_MIP) is open; it
   bounds the individuation axis, the collective living-threshold, and the higher-individual lineage
   route. Stated as frontier throughout. The one genuine scientific gap.
2. **Integration is partition-relative** — ≥6 inequivalent published measures agree on rank but not
   value/sign; the non-energy triangle inherits this.
3. **5 record-only references + 8 needs-user-PDF** — NOT submission-ready under the charter standard;
   closing them needs library/PDF access (see punch-list for the exact list).
4. **Bich et al. 2015** volume/pages corrected in canon (now *Biology & Philosophy* 31, 237–265).
5. Minor: Fig 3 panel-a planet/flame label tightness (legible; a typesetting nudge if the journal asks).
