# The Architecture of Persistence

**A four-axis classification of what it takes for a region of reality to resist erasure, with the couplings among the axes sorted by physical modality.**

*Submission draft for Royal Society* Interface Focus *(Perspective). Derived from the AOP canon v1.16; the canon is the complete backing reference and every computed value here traces to a deposited script (see Data Availability).*

---

## Abstract

Persistence — a region of reality resisting erasure — is asked about across nonequilibrium physics, computational mechanics, information theory, and theoretical biology, and each field answers with a different quantity. We do not add a quantity. We propose an organizing structure over the quantities the field already has, and ask a question those literatures rarely pose to one another: how do the distinct dimensions of persistence depend on one another, and which dependencies are compelled by physical law? We name four dimensions — **Boundary** (a maintained interior/exterior separation), **Drive** (free-energy throughput holding the system off equilibrium), **Memory** (information the present carries about its own future), and **Integration** (irreducibility to independent parts) — each a relative-entropy difference along its own axis: three static cuts and one, Drive, a trajectory asymmetry. The separation among them is graded, not blanket: Memory is near-orthogonal to the rest across generic systems, while Boundary and Integration are dissociable only by construction and otherwise share a plane — so the honest claim is four *distinguishable* axes, not four independent ones. From this we build a two-layer classification: a **coupling graph** whose edges are sorted by modal status (some couplings compelled by physical law, some conditional, some free), and a **semantic mask** that separates the couplings causally required for continued existence from those merely present, isolated by intervention on a system's own viability and computed here on the paper's one fully-specified system. The framework's own cross-axis predictions are put through a pre-registered, two-exit (GO/NULL) gate protocol: four candidate forced couplings return NULL, and a graded, ownership-free individuation axis passes its GO. Energy is the hub of the coupling graph but not its dictator. Every computed claim is closed-form, not estimated — deliberately, to avoid the estimator-artifact failure that retired a predecessor framework — and where the framework's predictions recur they are shown to match results independently established in other fields.

## 1. What persists, and along how many directions

The question of what makes a thing a persisting thing — an individual, a system that holds together — recurs across nonequilibrium physics, computational mechanics, information theory, and theoretical biology, and each field answers it with a different quantity. This paper does not add a quantity. It proposes an organizing structure over the quantities the field already has, and asks how the distinct dimensions of persistence depend on one another, and which of those dependencies are compelled by physical law.

The claim, in one paragraph. Persistence is resistance to erasure along four directions: a maintained separation of interior from exterior (**Boundary**), a free-energy throughput that holds the system off equilibrium (**Drive**), information the present carries that constrains its own future (**Memory**), and a failure to reduce to independently-acting parts (**Integration**). Each is a difference-that-could-be-otherwise, measured as a relative entropy along its own axis. The four are not a wish-list; they are what remains after asking, for each candidate dimension, whether a system could persist with it set to zero — and finding that it could not.

A note on what the reader is about to be shown, because it recurs. **None of the four is a substance one can point at** (Fig 1 makes the sharpest version of the point). Each is a computed difference across a cut — a mutual information, or for Drive a trajectory asymmetry — that is never observed directly. One observes residues, bent flows, locked shells, and infers the difference behind them. An event horizon makes this unavoidable: a horizon casts only a shadow — the dark disk in a horizon image is the critical photon ring, not the horizon itself — and the horizon is inferred from what it does to surrounding light. Every dimension is like this: one gets the shadow, never the thing. This is not a limitation to apologize for; it is the reason the framework is built on differences rather than substances.

**Fig 1** anchors the founding move. On a driven ring, the *measured* entropy production swings roughly seventyfold as the observer coarsens the grain at which the system is read (panel a) — the quantity is observation-dependent — yet the second-law floor σ ≥ 0 holds at every grain (panel b). Persistence is read along axes that are observer-relative in magnitude but law-constrained in sign. That combination — quantities that move with the observer, laws that do not — is the paper's organizing tension, and §5 makes it precise.

**Scope.** This is a Perspective: an organizing structure with worked demonstrations, not a theory with a single decisive experiment. The framework is graded throughout — every claim is tagged **settled** (established science we rely on), **synthesis** (this framework's own organizing move), or **frontier** (a claim we believe but have not closed). The reader should always be able to tell which they are reading; §12 collects the grades in one table.

## 2. The object: four dimensions as differences

A persisting thing holds a difference-that-could-be-otherwise along four directions, and each is a mutual information across a cut — inferred from its shadow, never seen. Read together, a system's four values form a *shape* — the object Fig 2 uses to compare persisters — but each value is itself a difference, not a substance.

**Boundary** is the mutual information between a designated interior and its exterior that a maintained separation *suppresses*: a boundary is present to the degree that knowing the outside tells you little about the inside beyond what the boundary enforces. **Memory** is the information the present state carries about its own future — the excess entropy E = I(past; future) of the system's trajectory, the mutual information between its semi-infinite past and future. **Integration** is the failure of the whole to factor into independent parts — a total-correlation-type quantity, the relative entropy between the joint distribution and the product of its marginals.

**Drive is the odd one out.** It is not a mutual information across a static cut but the time-asymmetry of the dynamics: entropy production equals the relative entropy (Kullback–Leibler divergence) between the forward trajectory distribution and its time-reverse [settled; Parrondo, Van den Broeck & Kawai 2009]. Three of the axes are static cuts through a snapshot; Drive is a cut through *time*. This asymmetry — three spatial-style differences and one temporal — is not cosmetic. It is why Drive turns out to be the hub of the coupling graph (§4): it is the one axis defined on trajectories, so it is the one that can force floors on the others.

That the four are *differences* rather than *stuffs* has a consequence the framework leans on. Each requires a declared cut to be defined — an inside/outside for Boundary, a partition into parts for Integration, a past/future split for Memory, a forward/reverse for Drive. Two of these cuts (Drive's and Memory's) are supplied by the dynamics themselves and need no external choice; two (Boundary's and Integration's) require a declared partition, and their values move with that choice. This split — partition-free versus partition-dependent axes — returns in §5 and §6 as the deepest structural fact about the four.

## 3. Two layers: a syntactic graph and a semantic mask

The four dimensions as usually measured are **syntactic** — plain Shannon correlations, which by themselves carry no meaning. Persistence turns on the **semantic** subset: the correlations causally necessary for the system to maintain its own existence.

The distinction, and the method for drawing it, are due to Kolchinsky and Wolpert [settled, 2018]. They define semantic information as the syntactic information a system holds about its environment that is causally necessary for it to keep itself in a low-entropy state, and operationalize "causally necessary" by counterfactual intervention: scramble a correlation to its detailed-balanced form in isolation, re-run the dynamics, and measure whether viability drops. What survives scrambling was decorative; what reduces viability when scrambled was load-bearing. The framework adopts this as its second layer — a **semantic mask** laid over the syntactic coupling graph, keeping the load-bearing edges and zeroing the inert ones.

The mask is not merely described here; it is **computed** on the paper's one fully-specified system (§7.2, where the mask figure is presented). On a driven three-state ring with an attached inert spectator node, the mask returns graded positive weights on the ring's load-bearing internal couplings and **exactly zero** on the spectator edge — the spectator is present in every syntactic correlation but causally necessary for nothing, and the mask says so by construction. The exact zero is the point: a modal analysis of the coupling graph alone cannot discriminate the spectator from the ring; the interventional mask can.

Two layers, then: a syntactic graph (§4) whose edges are sorted by *physical modality* — forced, conditional, free — and a semantic mask (this section, computed in §6/§11a) that sorts the same edges by *causal necessity for viability*. The rest of the paper is the interplay of these two sortings.

## 4. The coupling graph: energy is the hub, not the dictator

Energy is the hub, but its spokes are not alike, and this uneven hub is the spine of the structure. The coupling graph has four nodes (the axes) and edges sorted into three modal classes: **forced** (compelled by physical law), **conditional** (present under stated conditions), and **free** (contingent, weldable open or shut by construction).

**Drive → Memory is forced [synthesis, from a settled identity].** Any sustained dissipation leaves a trace: a strictly positive entropy production rate implies a strictly positive excess entropy, σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0, where E = I(past; future) is the past–future mutual information of computational mechanics [settled; Crutchfield & Feldman 2003]. This is not a fresh directional guess but a scoped corollary of the trajectory-irreversibility identity already invoked for Drive: for a stationary process on a single complete state, E = 0 iff past and future are independent (the process is i.i.d.), an i.i.d. process equals its own time-reverse, so its entropy production vanishes — and the contrapositive is exactly σ > 0 ⇒ E > 0. **Fig S4** confirms it on a driven Markov ring: every dissipating configuration carries E > 0, while the single detailed-balance configuration has σ = 0 with E > 0 — the converse fails, a reversible oscillator remembers for free, so the necessity runs one way only. The edge is secure but **scoped**: it forces a floor on *predictive* memory (E), not on stored complexity, and it is a floor, not a depth (§4a and the gate results below sharpen exactly where it stops).

**The other forced edge is Drive → reliability** via the thermodynamic uncertainty relation [settled; Barato & Seifert 2015]: any precise, reliable current must pay a dissipation cost. Both forced edges run *from* Drive. This is the sense in which energy is the hub: it is the one axis defined on trajectories, so it is the one that can compel floors on the others.

**But the hub does not dictate.** The edges *among* the non-energy axes — Boundary, Memory, Integration — are not forced. We tested this directly rather than asserting it. Four thousand random stable vector-autoregressive systems, plotted in (Boundary, Integration, Memory) space (**Fig S5**), show Memory spreading freely of the Boundary–Integration plane (pairwise |corr| < 0.05) while Boundary and Integration share a plane (corr ≈ 0.83). Five constructed regimes each reach a distinct corner — memory alone, integration without boundary, boundary without memory, all three together — establishing that **no non-energy edge is welded shut**. This is the origin of the paper's central honesty: the claim is four *distinguishable* axes, not four independent ones. Boundary and Integration are dissociable only by construction; left to generic dynamics they co-vary. Memory is the one axis genuinely near-orthogonal to the rest.

## 5. Laws are observer-independent; magnitudes are not

A brittle early form of this framework held that "forced means scale-invariant" — that a forced coupling, being a law, holds unchanged from molecules to institutions. That reading breaks the moment one notices that measured irreversibility is itself scale-dependent, and that coarse-graining can hide a nonequilibrium current so that a reduced observable looks reversible while the full dynamics dissipate [settled; a standard coarse-graining result, stated in the canon without a single attribution].

The repaired claim is sharper and survives: **the laws are observer-independent; the magnitudes are observer-relative.** A forced coupling transfers across scale because it is a law — σ ≥ 0 does not care about one's grain. But how much boundary, how much memory, how much σ a system "has" depends entirely on the cut and resolution at which it is measured. The relation is invariant; the number is not. This is precisely what **Fig 1** shows, and it is the paper's one set of genuinely computed magnitudes in the figures — the roughly seventyfold swing of σ with grain, against a floor that never breaks. That entropy production is measured at all in real systems — in living cells and in the human brain — is what lets the forced, law-tied quantities touch reality; the observer-relative magnitudes should stay scoped to a declared cut.

This relativity has a second face on the **time** axis, and there it cuts deeper. Memory's numerator E is defined only for a *stationary* process [settled; Crutchfield & Feldman 2003]. A system can be stationary on one clock and not another — a star is stationary on its thermal timescale but not its nuclear one — so its Memory is undefined until the clock is named. Reading the time-grain as the exact twin of the spatial-grain limit of Fig 1 is this framework's synthesis: just as Boundary and Integration require a declared spatial partition, Memory requires a declared temporal grain, and its magnitude moves with that choice while the D→M law does not.

## 6. The two-axis view, and a resolvability limit

Every coupling lives at a point in a plane: modal status (free → conditional → forced) against semantic weight (inert → load-bearing). **Persistence lives up-and-to-the-right** — couplings both forced and load-bearing. The modal axis alone was never enough: an edge can be present but semantically inert, or thin but load-bearing. Two knobs, not one.

The two axes are not of the same kind. The vertical (modal) axis is a *law* axis — forced/conditional/free is observer-independent and single-valued. The horizontal (semantic) axis is a *quantity* axis — as argued in §3, semantic weight is observer-relative, so an edge's horizontal position is not a pinned coordinate but a value that shifts with the declared partition, resolution, and viability functional. Each edge is more honestly a horizontal *range* than a point; **the width of that range is itself a diagnostic** — how far the weight moves as one varies the declaration — and measuring it is the natural first computational task the framework invites (**Fig S1**).

**A resolvability limit follows, and it is the paper's sharpest structural result.** The semantic mask is defined by intervening on one coupling at a time. But as Integration rises — as the parts become more interdependent — intervening on one edge without disturbing the others becomes impossible, and the mask blurs. The blur has two distinct mechanisms: parts become **inferentially** unresolvable (you cannot estimate a part's contribution — the variance-inflation factor, VIF, diverges) and edges become **interventionally** unresolvable (you cannot isolate an edge to scramble it — the do-intervention drag diverges). The ratio of the two is set by the coupling topology, so the resolvability limit is **a family indexed by wiring, not one curve**, not a single boundary.

**Fig 3** quantifies the limit and then shows a physical system realizing it from its own structure. Panel a places eight persisters in the resolvability plane (inferential blur × interventional blur); the star sits alone in the **resolvability trough** at the extreme corner (max per-component VIF 3.733, max do-edge drag 5.338), the largest joint blur of any persister examined. Panel b earns that placement: the star's per-shell VIF is derived by linearizing its own hydrostatic/thermal structure (a Lane–Emden n = 3 adiabatic wave operator), giving a graded sloppiness band peaking at exactly 3.733 — **identical to its plane placement, from stellar physics rather than assumption** — against a mean-field caricature that stays flat near 1.0. The star does not get relabeled into the resolvability trough; it falls there from its own equations. This is the framework's methodological signature: a prediction realized from a system's physics, closed-form, not fitted.

The limit is not one curve but a family (**Fig 4**): mean-field, chain, lattice, modular, and sparse topologies each trace a distinct limit, and in every one the aggregate (the whole) stays load-bearing while the parts blur — which topology a system has sets the ratio of its two blurs.

## 7. What the framework refuses — and the tests it put its refusals through

A classification of persistence is defined as much by what it excludes as by what it admits. AOP makes three refusals, and — unusually — it subjected them to a pre-registered test protocol rather than defending them by argument alone.

### 7.1 The present-tense scope wall

Any legitimate viability function must be **checkable at the present instant**. A viability that requires foresight — "this will eventually reproduce" — is illegitimate; it is hindsight worn as a goal. Persistence maintains; it never aims. Apparent foresight, such as a spore seeming to wait for spring, is a present capacity that past environments happened to favor: the wager is structural and retrospective, placed by no one and paid out by the frequency of good environments [settled; Rivoire & Leibler 2011, on the value of information in varying environments — the canon's [10] for the bet-hedging reading]. This kills teleology cleanly without denying that things can be poised for a future.

The wall has a sharp consequence for lineages and reproduction. A lineage enters the present-tense frame at exactly two admitted points and no third: **existence now** (a lineage exists now iff it has a present carrier — synthesis) and, in principle only, as a **higher individual** with its own lineage-clock Drive where an integration measure exceeds zero at the lineage partition (frontier — it rests on the nested-level extension of §4 that §4a leaves open). *Future* continuation — "this lineage will persist" — is refused and placed **above** AOP, in the domain of evolutionary theory. Reproduction and heredity are above the wall by construction: AOP describes what it takes to persist now, and hands the across-generation question to the theory of evolution. This is not a gap; it is the scope boundary that keeps the framework's present-tense claims honest.

### 7.2 The living threshold: alive = load-bearing ∧ model-based

The worked cases (§11, summarized below) fix the axes; they also expose a threshold the axes do not yet name. Three persisters actively hold themselves off equilibrium — the flame, the star, and (were it running) the cell — yet only the last is alive. The distinction is not *drive* (all three spend) and not *correction* (both star and cell restore after perturbation). **It is what the correction is read against**, and it is a structural fact the framework can compute (**Fig 5**):

1. **Continue** — no restoring force. The flame, rebuilt each instant from current supply, is gone the moment supply cuts, with no fight. Its semantics are all in drive.
2. **Correct, model-free** — a restoring force whose set-point *is* a fixed point of the constitutive dynamics. The star's negative-specific-heat thermostat pulls its core back when perturbed, but nothing in the star holds a *separable representation* of the set-point; the correction and the thing corrected are the same physics.
3. **Correct against a model** — a restoring force read against a *separately-interventable reference*. The cell holds a representation of its own viable set-point that can be perturbed independently of the variable it regulates. This is the alive tier.

**Fig 5** computes the discriminator: cutting the cell's model edge collapses viability (0.667 → 0.20), while cutting the star's coupling barely moves it (0.98 → 0.975) — the cell's regulation is load-bearing against a model, the star's is not. Critically, the discriminator is **architectural, not a timescale magnitude** (**Fig S6**): sweeping the slow/fast separation of reference from regulated variable, the model-edge weight stays high and load-bearing across the whole star–cell window with no threshold knee — what separates the cell from the star is the *presence of a separate, separately-interventable reference node* (a 0/1 architectural fact), not how slow the reference is.

This yields a two-tier, strictly present-tense predicate. A system is **alive** if the model edge is load-bearing *now* (mask-detectable now — the same interventional mask computed on the driven ring in **Fig 6**). It is **viable/pausable** if the decoupled-reference architecture is structurally present now but not currently load-bearing — read off structure without a restart. The dormant spore forces the two tiers apart: to the present-tense mask its drive ≈ 0 gives zero scramble-drop (not alive *now*), yet its decoupled-reference architecture is structurally present (viable). This is a definition, not a forecast — it never says "will reproduce." The framework's own star is correctly **not** promoted to life: it corrects, but model-free. [synthesis for the tier split; the discriminator rests on Francis & Wonham 1976 on the internal-model principle and Bich et al. 2015 on biological regulation, with Conant & Ashby 1970 as historical antecedent, not load-bearing.]

### 7.3 Individuality at the collective scale

The same machinery reads *upward*, to collectives (§9a). Here the framework makes one synthesis move: it splits a part's "self-interest," on the §9 scope wall, into **self-maintenance** (a part running its own boundary/drive/repair — present-tense viability at the part scale, *inside* AOP) and **private reproductive stake** (differential continuation across generations — *above* the wall). The two are independent because they live on two different levels; the durable collectives are those whose parts keep the first and surrender the second; **cancer is the failure mode where a part re-asserts the second**. Synchronically, a collective with positive integration at the part-partition is one higher persister with its own four-axis profile — already the content of §§4/9. The across-generation transition mechanism — single-cell bottleneck, policing, life-cycle fitness-decoupling, the major-transitions staircase — is **settled external biology** [Michod 2007; Queller & Strassmann 2009; Grosberg & Strathmann 2007; Frank 1995; Hammerschmidt et al. 2014; Aktipis et al. 2015; Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995] that *motivates* the scope wall; it is a Ladder-facing propagation item, explicitly **not** a persistence claim of this paper. The one new structural question — does a collective ever hold a decoupled model of the *collective's* own viability (the §11a threshold at collective scale)? — is stated as **frontier**, bottlenecked on the same nested-level integration seam as the §9 higher-individual route.

### 7.4 The refusals, tested: a pre-registered gate protocol

The framework's most distinctive methodological commitment is that it put its own would-be discoveries through a **two-exit gate**: each candidate cross-axis coupling was pre-registered with GO and NULL criteria *before* computation, so a null could not be reinterpreted into a win. Five gates ran.

- **Four candidate forced couplings returned NULL.** (i) A static snapshot test of resolvability against dissipation — resolvability is drive-blind. (ii) A trajectory Fisher-information test (F_traj = T·((2D)⁻¹ ⊗ Σ)) — the resolvability object is q-free by construction, so drive-blind even read dynamically [deposited trajectory-B gate verdict; the trajectory-Fisher form was verified in-body against its primary source during the gate run]. (iii) A coupling-resolvability test with the drive honestly co-estimated (a Schur-complement Fisher object F_K|Q) — once the drive is co-estimated and the true floor taken, coupling-resolvability is drive-blind; the trivial-coupling control rises *most*, exposing the naive version as a drift-map scale artifact [deposited trajectory-K gate verdict; the Schur-complement Fisher identity was verified in-body against its primary sources during the gate run]. (iv) A memory-irreversibility screen (does drive force a stored time-asymmetry Ξ = Cμ⁺ − Cμ⁻?) — an initial GO was **retracted** when the quantity computed turned out to be the entropy-production estimator itself (a Drive object misread as memory); the corrected object is identically zero at every drive, while positive controls confirm the estimator is not blind (it detects Ξ up to 0.90 on genuinely irreversible processes). The meta-finding: memory/structure objects are functionals of the generator's *time-symmetric* sector, dissipation of its *antisymmetric* sector — orthogonal coordinates, so no forced coupling.
- **One gate returned GO.** A graded, ownership-free **individuation axis** — integrated information across the minimum-information partition, Φ_MIP — passed all five pre-registered criteria (zero-calibration exact: Φ = 0 on block-decomposable systems; coordinate well-posedness exact; gradedness; irreducibility to the (VIF, TC) pair by a matched pair; a matched-coupling-budget one-vs-many control). It is adopted as a third-person, ownership-free individuation coordinate **in the static Gaussian setting only**; the nested-level and non-stationary/critical extensions are frontier.

The four NULLs are first-class results, not failures: they mark where the framework does *not* force structure, which is exactly what keeps the forced edges of §4 credible. The retracted-then-corrected GO is reported as such, because the discipline is the contribution.

## 8. Five persisters, and where they sit

The abstractions earn their keep on five concrete systems (developed in full in the Supplementary Information, §S-cases). Four form a progression from full persistence to a bare minimum — **crystal** (a boundary and structure held with almost no drive), **flame** (pure drive, no model, gone when supply cuts), **spore** (capacity held in near-zero-drive dormancy), **bound atom** (the minimal quantum persister) — and the fifth, the **star**, is not a point on that line but a case in a corner none of the others occupy: high Drive, high Integration, gravitationally bound, sitting in the resolvability trough of Fig 3. A mode of persistence is a *shape* across the four dimensions in semantic weight (**Fig 2**); a transition is a change in that shape. Two axes — Drive and Memory — are computable without a declared partition; the other two — Boundary and Integration — are partition-dependent and are the softer of the four, a distinction Fig 2 marks explicitly. The worked cases are where the living threshold (§7.2) becomes physical: the flame *continues*, the star *corrects model-free*, the cell *corrects against a model*, and only the last is alive.

## 9. Status of claims

A framework of this kind is defensible exactly to the degree that the strength of each claim is stated honestly and no synthesis is promoted to a theorem it is not. The full ledger is in the SI (Table S1); its spine:

- **Settled** (tied to named, published results within their regime): the trajectory-irreversibility identity for Drive; the thermodynamic uncertainty relation; E defined for stationary processes; coarse-graining dependence of σ; the gravitational non-additivity that puts the star in its corner; the Kolchinsky–Wolpert semantic-information construction.
- **This framework's synthesis** (defensible as organizing moves, not theorems): the four-axis object; the modal coupling graph; the D→M edge as a scoped corollary; the resolvability limit as a topology-indexed family; the semantic mask computed on the driven ring; the living-threshold tier split; the two-level reading of collective individuality.
- **Frontier** (used as convergence, not foundation): the individuation axis beyond the static Gaussian setting; the collective living-threshold; the recombination/heredity questions above the wall.

The four gate NULLs and the individuation GO sit inside this ledger as *results*, each with a deposited verdict carrying its full pre-registration, computation, and controls.

## 10. Limitations and outlook

The framework's honest exposure is concentrated in three places. **First**, the two soft dimensions — Boundary and Integration — are partition-relative, and Integration in particular has at least six inequivalent published measures that agree on rank but disagree on values, trends, and even sign; any claim about the non-energy triangle inherits that ambiguity. **Second**, the semantic mask's weights are defined only relative to a declared viability functional, and on strongly integrated systems the per-edge weights may not be separately defined at all — which is not a defect to be removed but a fact about integrated systems to be characterized. That characterization is now partly in hand: the failure decomposes into an inferential (estimation) and an interventional (isolation) blur, a topology-indexed family (§6, Fig 4). **Third**, and most importantly, the framework has one principal open problem.

**F2 — the nested-level, non-stationary extension of the integration axis — is the seam that bounds the framework.** The individuation axis is closed only in the static Gaussian setting; the higher-individual route for lineages (§7.1), the collective living-threshold (§7.3), and the critical/Ising individuation regime all wait on extending Φ_MIP to nested levels and non-stationary partitions. This is stated as frontier throughout, and it does not weaken the forced edges of §4 or the present-tense claims — but it is the single result whose closure would most extend the framework's reach, and it is shared with the downstream project that builds on this one.

The larger outlook is the one the introduction promised: the framework adds no quantity, and its value is as an organizing structure that lets fields that measure persistence differently see that they are measuring axes of one object — with the couplings among those axes sorted, honestly, into what physical law compels and what it merely permits. Energy is the hub. It is not the dictator.
