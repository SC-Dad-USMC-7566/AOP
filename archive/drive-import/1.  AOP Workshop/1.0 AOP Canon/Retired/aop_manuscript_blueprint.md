## 1. Contribution statement (reviewer-facing — goes up front, in abstract + intro)

**Pitch (lead sentence).** *Persistence — a region of reality resisting erasure — resolves into four
distinguishable entropic axes (Boundary, Drive, Memory, Integration) whose couplings sort by physical
modality into forced, conditional, and free; energy is the hub but not the dictator.*

**The three contributions, phrased for a reviewer (what is genuinely new / falsifiable vs. synthesis):**

1. **A two-layer classification of persistence** — a modal coupling graph (edges sorted forced /
   conditional / free) over four relative-entropy axes, plus a semantic mask that separates couplings
   *causally required* for continued existence from those merely present, isolated by intervention on
   viability and **computed** on a fully-specified system (graded weights on load-bearing couplings,
   exact zero on an inert one). [SYNTHESIS + computed result]

2. **A pre-registered, two-exit gate discipline applied to the framework's own predictions** — four
   candidate cross-axis couplings tested under GO/NULL pre-registration, returning four NULLs, plus a
   graded ownership-free individuation axis (Φ_MIP) that passed its five-criterion GO. The negative
   results are first-class: the framework states where it does *not* force structure. This methodology
   — and its honesty — is itself a contribution. [the novel methodological spine]

3. **Closed-form, not estimated, results** — every computed claim is analytic (the resolvability limit,
   the sector split, the mask zeros), explicitly to avoid the estimator-artifact failure that sank a
   predecessor framework. Where the framework's "new" predictions recur, they are shown to match
   results independently established in other fields (convergence, not coincidence). [rigor claim]

**Most arresting display item (graphical abstract):** the four-axis profile-as-shape (current Figure 5)
— it geometrizes the whole classification: a persister is a shape, a transition is a change in shape.

**What Figure 1 must do:** hook on the observer-relativity result — entropy production swings ~70-fold
with coarse-graining grain — which licenses the paper's founding move (persistence is read along
distinct, observer-relative axes) before any machinery is introduced.

The canon's section order is already close to a good arc; the manuscript tightens it into six movements:

   once; those directions are observer-relative (Fig 1); name the four axes. [~900w]

   coupling graph + semantic mask). [~900w]

   free); the laws-vs-quantities scale claim. This is the paper's spine. [~1,600w]

   resolvability limit as a topology-indexed family; the star realizing it from its own physics.
   [~1,400w]
5. **What the framework refuses, tested (from §9, §9a, §11a, and the gate results in §12):** the
   present-tense scope wall; the living threshold (alive = load-bearing ∧ decoupled); collective
   individuality two-level reading; the four cross-axis NULLs + individuation GO as pre-registered
   results. [~2,000w]
6. **Status + outlook (from §12 compressed, §13):** the grade summary (settled/synthesis/frontier),
   F2 as the principal open problem, the scope wall to the Ladder. [~1,200w]
