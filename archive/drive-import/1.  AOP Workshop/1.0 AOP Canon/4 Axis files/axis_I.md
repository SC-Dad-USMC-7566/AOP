## 1. The axis in one paragraph

Integration is the **parts** axis: how much a system's components are one interdependent object rather than a stack of independent ones. Its target is *interdependence-that-bears-on-persistence*; it is read out not as a scalar but as a **panel** (I1–I5) under the declaration tuple D — I1 multivariate interdependence (total correlation), I2 redundancy/synergy (O-information / PID), I3 cut-irreducibility (Φ_MIP), I4 dynamic/causal integration, I5 modularity / near-minimum cuts. The operational default proxy is **total correlation, TC = Σ H(Xᵢ) − H(X)** [Watanabe 1960 ⊙], the family member that is non-negative by construction, monotone in coupling, and closed-form Gaussian. Integration's defining problem, stated up front as a permanent scope limit, is that **there is no canonical measure**: at least six published integrated-information measures agree on rank but disagree on value, trend, and even sign on Gaussian networks [Mediano, Seth & Barrett 2019 ✓]. This is a special case of a broader regularity — over a dozen causal measures reduce to a small set of shared primitives (sufficiency/necessity → determinism/degeneracy), agreeing on structural conclusions like causal emergence while differing in the numbers they assign [Comolatti & Hoel 2025 ✓]: **direction survives the choice of measure, magnitude does not.** AOP inherits this ambiguity but *not its stakes*, because it uses Integration as one descriptive axis among four, **not** as a measure of consciousness. Consequently **every Integration-dependent claim is stated in its directional form and no exact Integration value is load-bearing**. What the axis does NOT measure: TC is *interdependence / shared variance, not proven irreducible wholeness*; it is *sign-blind for persistence* until the viability-alignment second coordinate is added (§6); and it does not tell one individual from many — that is a separate coordinate, Φ_MIP.

Two further coordinates live on this axis and must be kept distinct from TC. **Φ_MIP** — Gaussian integrated information across the minimum information partition [Aguilera & Di Paolo 2019 ✓] — is the *one-vs-many* individuation coordinate: exactly zero on any block-decomposable system, graded up as parts become jointly irreducible into one whole. **Resolvability blur** (§6) is the mask's own failure on strongly integrated systems and is *not* an integration measure at all but a statement about what integration does to measurement. Confusing these three — TC (how much shared variance), Φ_MIP (one thing or many), resolvability (can the parts still be separately weighed) — is the axis's most common internal error.

## 2. The persister, per example

The mandate: name the process, not the object. Integration's job is to say whether a set of parts *is* one persister at all, and, where it is, to be the coupling that both holds it together and blurs the instrument.

| Example | The persister (process) | The husk | Present-tense type | What Integration contributes to *its* persistence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Star** | the self-regulating **hydrostatic + fusion process** (fusion resupplies thermal pressure against gravity and radiative loss) | the ball of gas; the cooling remnant once fusion stops (white dwarf / the KH-time corpse) | process (§9): hydrostatic + thermal balance checkable *now* | Integration very high and **non-separable** — hydrostatic equilibrium couples every shell to the weight above and structure below; no shell's coupling can be scrambled without the rest readjusting. Same hydrostatic coupling holds it up *and* drives it into the resolvability trough |
| **Multicellular organism / integrated colony** | the **cooperating physiological whole** (the metazoan's integrated maintenance, or a colony with Φ_MIP > 0 at the part-partition) | the pile of cells — a corpse is exactly the parts that no longer cohere | process; and *one* only if Φ_MIP > 0 (§9a) | Φ_MIP > 0 is what makes it **one higher persister** carrying its own four-axis profile on its own clock, rather than a cloud of members. A Φ_MIP ≈ 0 aggregate is a pile with no collective present tense, however much its members cooperate |
| **Two independent internally-rich modules** | **two** persisters, each its own process | — (neither is a husk of the other) | two configurations/processes, not one | The decisive negative case: TC and per-component resolvability blur are both **high**, yet Φ_MIP = **0**, so Integration-as-irreducibility correctly reads them as *two things*. This is why TC cannot be the individuation coordinate |
| **Spore** (Integration reading) | a **paused** integrated architecture — coupling *structurally present*, drive off | a mere blueprint/description would be the dead twin (§4a) | capacity; viable/pausable-tier yes, alive-tier no (§11a) | Integration held **in escrow**: the interdependence is stored structure (large Cμ), not live TC-bearing current; the coupling can re-form when a germinant re-forms it |
| **Crystal** (Integration reading) | the **growth front** (past tense) — drive that deposited the structured lattice | the grown lattice you hold | configuration, terminal | Integration **frozen/spent**: high static structural order, zero live coupling doing persistence work; semantics all in the past |

Diachronic test (§4a): the star and the organism restart nothing from a record — they are continuously instantiated processes individuated by *continuity of instantiation*; interrupt the process (kill fusion; kill the physiology) and there is no self-reboot. The spore is the one case that restarts *itself* from its own held architecture (paused, not dead). The two independent modules never were one process to restart.

**The load-bearing line for this axis:** *the persister is the integrated process; the husk is the set of parts once they stop cohering.* Integration is the axis that decides whether "the parts" even name one persister.

## 3. The axis independently

**Settled core.** The one settled, closed-form object is total correlation, TC = Σ H(Xᵢ) − H(X) [Watanabe 1960 ⊙], non-negative, monotone in coupling, Gaussian-closed-form (TC = ½[Σ log Σᵢᵢ − log|Σ|]; this is exactly `stats_from` in `aop_depmap.py`). Its signed companion is the **O-information** Ω [Rosas, Mediano, Gastpar & Jensen 2019 ⊙], positive for redundancy-dominated and negative for synergy-dominated structure — the measure to reach for when the *character* of the interdependence, not just its magnitude, is load-bearing. For "simultaneous segregation and integration," causal density is preferred [Mediano–Seth–Barrett 2019 ✓, which finds only some measures capture that dynamical complexity]. **[SETTLED, within the Gaussian model class; TC is a definitional citation.]**

**The no-canonical-measure fact, honestly.** Six candidate measures of integrated information show "a striking diversity in behaviour — no two show consistent agreement across all analyses" on Gaussian networks [Mediano, Seth & Barrett 2019 ✓, verified this session]. Whole-minus-sum integrated information can go negative. The canon's own recomputation confirms the pattern: two closed-form measures on thousands of coupled Gaussian systems agree in rank (Spearman ≈ 0.87) but disagree in value. The correct posture is **directional-only**: all reasonable measures rise with coupling; no exact value is invariant. **[SETTLED that the measures disagree; SYNTHESIS that AOP can live with it because it uses Integration descriptively, not as consciousness.]**

**The individuation coordinate, Φ_MIP.** Gaussian integrated information across the minimum information partition of Σ = (I + gL)⁻¹ [Aguilera & Di Paolo 2019 ✓]. Exactly **zero on any block-decomposable system**, graded to one whole as parts become jointly irreducible. Established by a pre-registered five-criterion gate (canon Table 4, the register's first GO): zero-calibration and coordinate well-posedness exact; gradedness, irreducibility-to-(VIF, TC) via a matched-(VIF,TC)-different-Φ pair, and a matched-coupling-budget one-vs-many control all passed. Its ordering is invariant under per-component rescaling and robust across minimum-partition normalizations (so it needs no analogue of the resolvability K-convention), but it inherits the integration-family's scope — it is the *minimum-cut* member, not a canonical Φ. It **narrows the framework's "no individuation" refusal to "no ownership"**: AOP still refuses to individuate by a self-maintenance/ownership scalar (the PIC lesson), but now admits third-person, coupling-based, graded individuation. **[SYNTHESIS, conditionally-forced within static Gaussian; analytic-model-result. Nested level-selection and non-stationary/critical extensions FRONTIER — see §5, the F2 seam.]**

**The panel, and the cleanest computable proxy.** I1 = TC (compute this). I2 = O-information (reach for when synergy/redundancy matters). I3 = Φ_MIP (compute for one-vs-many, static Gaussian only). I4 dynamic/causal integration and I5 modularity are specified but not the default. The acceptance rule (ADR-001): no numerical Integration claim without naming which of I1–I5 and why; "highly integrated" is meaningless unbadged. **The Integration DEFECT the panel fixes:** old Table 1 lets a lone TC scalar silently stand for "unity"; the panel forbids it (TC is interdependence, not proven wholeness).

**Worked numbers that exist.** TC identity max error 1.8e-15 over 4000 systems (§4 below). Φ_MIP = 0 exact on block-decomposable, positive on module-weld. Resolvability: per-component uncertainty diverges as (1−ρ)^(−1/2) with aggregate 1/√λ_max, validated to machine precision; star operator built from Lane–Emden n=3 reproducing ξ₁ = 6.897 to five digits (§6, canon Data Accessibility).

## 4. Interactions with the other three axes

Typed against the measured dependence map (`aop_depmap.py`, 4000 random stable VAR(1) systems + constructed corners; report rev.2 §5). Reminder: correlation is a **finding to type**, not a failure to fix.

### Integration ↔ Boundary — **nested** (the B–I nesting identity is as much mine as Boundary's)

The exact identity, machine-precision (max err **1.8e-15** over 4000 systems):

> **TC = I(in;out) + TC_in + TC_out**

Boundary's lead proxy I(in;out) is the **cross-cut slice** of Integration; TC_in + TC_out is the within-side structure Boundary throws away. So the raw **B–I Spearman ≈ 0.83** is not a coincidence and not a collapse — it is this identity showing through: both B and TC are instantaneous mutual-informations read off *the same covariance matrix*, so they co-move. Controlling for Memory the partial **B–I|M = 0.73** stays high — direct, shared-substrate, exactly because removing the time-lagged axis (Memory) does nothing to two static cuts of one Σ. **Dissociable but not free:** every combination is reachable by construction — sealed modules give (B, I, E) = (0, 1.53, 0), Integration with *zero* Boundary; a cross-cut-only pair gives (1.01, 1.01, 0), Boundary = Integration with no internal structure. Boundary is the piece of Integration that happens to fall across a declared cut. **[Identity: SETTLED, analytic (theorem-level, machine-verified). B–I substrate-sharing: SYNTHESIS, random-ensemble regularity. Dependency status: dissociable, substrate-sharing.]**

### Integration ↔ Drive — **free (static) / open (dynamic)**

Parts can be correlated at **zero dissipation**: integration exists statically for free (a passive equicorrelated Gaussian has high TC and σ = 0). So D→I is **free at equilibrium**. Whether drive *builds or maintains* integration over time — selection assembling and holding a coupled whole — is a separate, genuinely unsettled question, and conflating "integration exists for free" with "drive made this integration" is a named error. **[Free-static: SETTLED. Dynamic-open: FRONTIER. Do not claim drive forces integration.]** One asymmetry worth noting: the gravitational Integration *floor* (§8, below) is a case where a force imposes integration with no drive at all — non-additivity, not dissipation, does the coupling.

### Integration ↔ Memory — **shared-driver; mild tradeoff at fixed coupling (suggestive)**

Raw **I–M Spearman ≈ 0.61**, but this is mostly shared coupling strength. Partial out Boundary: **I–M|B = 0.22**. Partial out raw coupling: **I–M|coupling = −0.62** — a mild *tradeoff* at fixed coupling budget, consistent with memory and integration appearing as **substitutable strategies** in bet-hedging theory [Rivoire & Leibler 2011 ⊙ / canon ref 10]. Memory carries the most unique rank-variance (0.59) of the three non-energy axes; Integration carries only **0.29** — Integration is the *least* independent axis, sharing most of its variance with Boundary and Memory. That is honest and it is fine: dissociability, not orthogonality, is the bar, and Integration dissociates (sealed-modules and pure-memory corners both exist). **[Shared-driver + suggestive tradeoff: SYNTHESIS, random-ensemble regularity. Dependency: dissociable.]** **DEFECT flag:** canon Table 2's M–I row and Fig T say "|corr| < 0.05 / near-orthogonal" — that is the *same overclaim* the report flags for Fig T. The measured raw I–M is 0.61, not <0.05. The near-orthogonality is only true *after* partialling coupling. **Reword Table 2 M–I and Fig T to the measured dependence** (raw 0.61; free only conditional on coupling).

## 5. Holes, traps, and DEFECTs

**The principal open problem — the F2 seam (make this vivid).** Φ_MIP is settled *only* in the static-Gaussian setting for the one-vs-many question at a **fixed** partition. Two extensions are FRONTIER and they are the **same seam**: (i) **nested level-selection** — which level of a nested system (cell / organism / society) is *the* individual; and (ii) the **non-stationary and critical** extension — the same construction defined without stationarity, and its behaviour at criticality. This one missing capability **bottlenecks two of the framework's biggest downstream claims at once**:
- the **§9 higher-individual lineage route** (a lineage as a Φ_MIP-integrated higher individual with a lineage-clock Drive) is "available in principle only" — it leans on Φ_MIP at a cross-generational, *non-stationary* partition;
- the **§9a collective-*alive* question** (a decoupled model of the *collective's own* viability — the §11a living threshold at collective scale) is bottlenecked on the **same** nested-level Φ_MIP seam.

So the framework's route to "a superorganism is one individual" and its route to "a collective can be alive" both stall on one unbuilt piece of machinery. That is the single highest-value open problem on this axis. **Partial literature anchor:** Aguilera & Di Paolo 2019 ✓ already show integrated information **diverges at critical points in the thermodynamic limit** (kinetic Ising / mean-field), and that the divergent behaviour of different blocks marks the boundary between an integrated unit and its environment — so the *critical* half has a real anchor to build on; the *minimum-information-partition + non-stationary + level-selection* combination is what is not in hand. **[FRONTIER; needs new computation in a model class with a phase transition, plus a level-selection criterion.]**

**The resolvability tension — central and honest.** The semantic mask scrambles one coupling at a time; that presupposes edges are *separately manipulable*. High Integration is precisely the regime where they are not. So Integration reads **most cleanly on weakly-integrated systems and blurs on the strongly-integrated ones the framework was built to describe.** The blur is **two** distinct mechanisms, not one: an **inferential** failure (per-component attribution variance diverges — variance-inflation factor, so no edge weight is separately *estimable*) and an **interventional** failure (coupled parts share a generator, so a do-operation on one drags the others — no edge is *isolable* even in principle). They do not travel together; their **ratio is set by coupling topology**, so the resolvability limit is a *family* indexed by wiring, not one curve (Fig TF: chain highest in inferential blur, sparse hub-network highest in interventional drag), with the aggregate/collective mode staying sharp across every topology. This is inherited, not discovered — it is multicollinearity [Marquardt 1970 ⊙], parameter sloppiness [Transtrum, Machta & Sethna 2010; Transtrum et al. 2015 ⊙], and PID attribution non-uniqueness [Williams & Beer 2010 ⊙] under three names. What AOP adds is the *reading*: the stiff/sloppy split is the mathematical shadow of the "no-ownership" refusal. **[Component results SETTLED; topology-family reading SYNTHESIS; the interpretive link is a lens, not a falsifiable forecast — say so.]** The star realizes this from its own physics: linearizing the adiabatic pulsation equation on a Lane–Emden n=3 polytrope gives a tridiagonal shell-to-shell operator whose spectrum is a graded stiff-to-sloppy band; per-shell weights are unresolvable (inferential) and gravity resists intervention entirely (interventional), while the collective mode stays defined. Crucially this is a **demonstration of self-consistency, not a test that could fail** — any strongly coupled linear system has a stiff/sloppy spectrum, so a real integrated system could not have come out *un*-blurred.

**The gravitational Integration floor.** Gravity is unscreenable (no negative mass, infinite range), so it forms no statistical boundary and imposes a floor: **nothing is ever perfectly decoupled**, so TC never hits zero for gravitating matter. This is settled, not AOP's invention: long-range interactions (potential ∼1/rᵅ, α ≤ d) are **non-additive** — the energy of a whole is not the sum of its parts' — with negative specific heat and ensemble inequivalence the thermodynamic signatures [Campa, Dauxois & Ruffo 2009 ⊙, canon full-text verified]. The single settled synthesis of the paper's star: this *one* property is the common root of three features at once — gravity's anti-boundary character, the Integration floor, **and** the star's self-regulation (negative specific heat ⇒ the thermostat). The property that denies the star a boundary is the property that stabilizes its drive. **[Base SETTLED; "one root of three" is SYNTHESIS. Scope: this is a floor for gravitating systems, not a *universal* Integration floor — the canon scoped it after the OAI reviewer flagged the universal reading as overreach.]**

**Traps already fallen into / to avoid on this axis:**
- **coalition / Möbius = integration** — CLOSED (report T2 "added nothing"): coalition-value / Möbius-interaction structure is not a new integration axis; it re-describes interdependence. Do not resurrect it as a fifth measure.
- **TC as unity** — TC is interdependence / shared variance, **not** proven irreducible wholeness and **not** causal unity. The panel exists to stop a lone TC scalar from smuggling in "the parts are one." Irreducibility is Φ_MIP's job, and even Φ_MIP is the minimum-cut member, not a canonical Φ.
- **measure-shopping for a sign** — because six measures disagree on magnitude and sign, picking the measure that gives the sign you want is the cardinal sin. Only *directional* claims (rank-robust across measures) are admissible [Comolatti & Hoel 2025 ✓ is the license: direction is consilient, magnitude is not].
- **entanglement entropy** — do **not** use quantum entanglement entropy as the integration measure for classical persisters; use classical mutual information / total correlation. The quantum monogamy relation [Koashi & Winter 2004 ?, unverified lead — cited only as the technical reason classical correlation is the right object; treat as a lead, not a fact until read].
- **TC sign-blindness** — TC is blind to whether integration *helps* persistence; the viability-alignment second coordinate (§6 mask) is required. High TC in a pathological coupling can be load-bearing *against* the system.

**Over-claims to fix:** (1) Fig T / Table 2 M–I "|corr| < 0.05" — reword to measured dependence (raw I–M 0.61, free only after controlling coupling); same class of overclaim as Boundary's Fig T. (2) Any place TC is allowed to read as "unity" rather than "interdependence."
